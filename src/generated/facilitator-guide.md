# Comprehensive Facilitator's Guide: PMP Mastery Framework

## 🏁 The Vision: "Strategic Peer" Leadership
The **PMP Mastery Framework** is not a standard test-prep course; it is a clinical training program for project leaders. As a facilitator, you do not "teach" the ECO—you **internalize** it for the candidate. Your goal is to move them from "knowing the rules" to "embodying the leader's response."

---

## 🏛️ Pedagogical Pillars
1. **Situational Synthesis**: Every lesson must culminate in a "What would the PM do?" scenario.
2. **Active Rationale**: We prioritize the **Why** (The Rationale) over the **What** (The Answer).
3. **Domain Integration**: We never teach People, Process, or Business in silos; we highlight the bidirectional impacts (e.g., how a Process failure causes a People conflict).

---

## 🛠️ The Artifact Ecosystem
Each day's delivery relies on four distinct tools:
1. **Executive Summary**: The "Hook." Used for high-level alignment and 5-minute pre-checks.
2. **Self-Study Lesson**: The "Meat." Deep technical content, formulas, and situational examples.
3. **Marp Slides**: The "Visuals." Designed for high-impact classroom and virtual delivery.
4. **Presentation Guide**: The "Script." Coaching notes on tone, hooks, and specific peer insights.

---

## 🧭 The 13-Week Journey

### Month 1: The People Pillar (Weeks 1-4, 9-10)
- **Focus**: Building the high-performance team.
- **Key Pivot**: Transitioning from a "Manager" to a "Servant Leader."
- **Critical Milestone**: Work Group 1 & 4 Peer Assessments.

### Month 2: The Process Pillar (Weeks 5-8)
- **Focus**: The "Plumbing" of projects—Integration, Scope, Schedule, Cost, Quality.
- **Key Pivot**: Mastering the **Integrated Change Control** and **Risk Management** loops.
- **Critical Milestone**: Work Group 2 & 3 Review Assessments.

### Month 3: Business & Mastery (Weeks 11-12)
- **Focus**: Strategic Value and Final Polish.
- **Key Pivot**: The "Business Strategist"—linking internal delivery to external PESTLE/VUCA shifts.
- **Goal**: Passing the 180-question simulations on Day 78 and Day 81.

---

## 🤝 Embodying the "Strategic Peer"
- **Tone**: Professional, encouraging, and authoritative but approachable.
- **Language**: Use PMI-aligned terminology (Servant Leadership, MVP, CCB, WBS).
- **Behavior**: Model the PMP Mindset. If a student disagrees with an answer, don't argue—**Analyze** their logic and guide them back to the ECO standard.

---

## 🛡️ Managing the "Red Zones" (Common Pitfalls)
- **EVM Fear**: Handle math early and often. Focus on interpretation, not just calculation.
- **Ethics Gaps**: Use the **Day 80 Ethics Reference** for every situational tie-breaker.
- **Agile Resistance**: For candidates from Waterfall backgrounds, emphasize that "Agile is not chaos; it is disciplined transparency."

---

## 🏆 Final Success Metric
Success is achieved when the candidate stops asking "What is the correct answer?" and starts saying "Based on the PMP mindset, the only logical action is X because..."
