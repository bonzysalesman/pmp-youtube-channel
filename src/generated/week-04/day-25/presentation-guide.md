# Presentation Guide: Day 25 - Budget, Resources & Artefacts

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is about **Truth** and **Legacy**. You are moving from the "theoretical" planning to the "hard data" of execution. Your tone should be **Incisive** and **Accountable**. You are coaching your peers on how to read the project's "vitals" and how to leave a professional trail.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Planning is easy; management is where the real work happens. Welcome to Day 25, where we look at the data and the documentation that makes a PM a professional."

### Slide 2: Recap
- **Connecting the dots**: Briefly remind them that we estimated on Day 24; now we measure.

### Slide 3: Controlling Costs (Section 2.5.3)
- **The Core Focus**: It's about monitoring the *burn* against the *baseline*. 
- **Peer Insight**: "Report the numbers honestly. An executive would rather know you’re 20% over budget now than find out the project is bankrupt later."

### Slide 4: EVM Framework
- **The technical hurdle**: Many students fear EVM. Reframe it: 
- *EV is the value we've unlocked.*
- *AC is what it cost to unlock it.*
- *PV is what we promised to unlock by now.*

### Slide 5: Forecasting
- **The "So What?"**: EVM isn't just a history lesson; it's a weather forecast. If the current trend continues, will we make it?

### Slide 6: Resource Lifecycle (Section 2.5.4)
- **The Logic**: Acquisition is a negotiation. Control is an optimization.
- **Key Concept**: **Resource Histograms**. Mention that we use these to see when the team is "over-allocated" (burned out).

### Slide 7: Managing Artefacts (Section 2.12)
- **The Definition**: Clarify that 'Artefacts' isn't just a fancy word for documents. It includes *everything* the project produces—physical and digital.

### Slide 8: Why Artefacts Matter
- **The "Newborn" Test**: "If a new PM took over your project tomorrow, would your artefacts allow them to survive the first day?"

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Recovering Project"
**Objective**: Interpret EVM metrics and propose a strategic correction.
- **Analysis**: SPI 0.70 (Very behind schedule); CPI 1.15 (Under budget).
- **The Fix**: You are "Rich but Slow." The recommendation should be **Crashing**. Spend that extra budget to add resources (overtime or more staff) to recover the time lost on the schedule.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **EVM Memorization**: Remind them not to just memorize formulas but to understand the *meaning*. 
- *If CPI = 0.9, just say: 'We are spending $1.00 to get 90 cents of work.' It makes the logic stick.*
- **Artefact Overload**: Clarify that "Managing" artefacts includes knowing what to *archive* or *delete*. We aren't hoarders; we are librarians.
