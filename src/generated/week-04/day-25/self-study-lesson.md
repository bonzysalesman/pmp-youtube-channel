# Self-Study Lesson: Day 25 - Performance, Resources, & Artefacts

## 🧠 The Strategic Perspective

In this lesson, we cover three critical areas of the **Process Domain**:
1. **Financial Control** (EVM)
2. **Resource Stewardship** (Acquisition and Utilization)

As a strategic peer, you don't just "check the bank account." You use **Earned Value Management** to see into the future of the project budget.

---

## 1. The EVM Cheat Sheet: The 4 Indicators You Must Master
On the PMP exam, you will be given numbers and asked what they mean. Memorize this logic:

- **Variance (CV/SV)**: `EV - AC` or `EV - PV`. 
    - *Logic*: If it's **Positive**, you are under budget/ahead of schedule. If **Negative**, you are over/behind.
- **Index (CPI/SPI)**: `EV / AC` or `EV / PV`.
    - *Logic*: If it's **> 1.0**, you are performing well. If **< 1.0**, you are underperforming.
- **EAC (Estimate at Completion)**: Predicted final cost. 
    - *Equation (Typical)*: `BAC / CPI`.

## 2. ECO Deep Dive: The Logic of 2.5 & 2.12

### Enabler 1: Monitor Budget Variations (2.5.3)
This is about **Intervention**.
- **The PM's Job**: If CPI is 0.8, you don't just report it. You **analyze the root cause** and take corrective action (e.g., renegotiating vendor contracts or optimizing resource allocation).

### Enabler 2: Plan and Manage Resources (2.5.4)
This is the **Acquisition & Growth phase**. 
- **Resource Leveling**: Shifting work to balance the workload (often delays the schedule).
- **Resource Smoothing**: Shifting work within the "float" to avoid peaks and valleys (doesn't delay the schedule).
- **The Human Element**: Remember that "Developing the Team" (Task 1.6) is a key part of your resource strategy.

### Enabler 3: Manage Project Artefacts (2.12)
Artefacts are the "Institutional DNA."
- **Focus**: Version control, retrieval, and disposition.
- **Strategic Value**: During a project audit, your artefacts are your defense. They prove that you followed the approved processes and baselines.

---

## 🎯 PMP Mindset Tip: "Assess, Then Act"
If you see a cost variance, the exam will ask: "What should the PM do NEXT?"
1. **Analyze**: Calculate the variance and find the cause.
2. **Communicate**: Inform stakeholders of the performance (if it hits a threshold).
3. **Change**: If a baseline adjustment is needed, follow the formal **Change Control Process**.

## 🛠️ Self-Reflection Activity: The EVM Health Check
Think about a project where you felt "behind."
1. **The Math**: If you had calculated your **SPI**, what would it have been? (e.g., were you doing 80% of the work you planned to do?)
2. **The Artefact**: Where is your project's "Issue Log" right now? Is it in a shared folder, or is it in someone's head?
3. **Internal Goal**: Organize your most critical project documents (Artefacts) into a central repository today to ensure knowledge isn't lost if a team member leaves.
