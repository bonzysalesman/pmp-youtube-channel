## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "What gets measured, gets managed."

- **Objective 1**: Control costs using data-driven metrics.
- **Objective 2**: Manage the human and physical resource lifecycle.
- **Objective 3**: Govern the project's institutional memory (Artefacts).

---

## 📊 Controlling Costs: The EVM Framework (ECO 2.5.3)

### The Performace "X-Ray"
- **PV (Planned Value)**: What we *meant* to spend.
- **AC (Actual Cost)**: What we *actually* spent.
- **EV (Earned Value)**: The *value* of the work completed.

### The Success Indicators
- **CPI (Cost Performance Index)**: EV / AC (Efficiency).
- **SPI (Schedule Performance Index)**: EV / PV (Velocity).
- **Target**: > 1.0 (Healthy Project).

---

## 📈 Forecasting the Future

- **CV & SV (Variances)**: Is the delta significant?
- **EAC (Estimate at Completion)**: Where will we finish?
- **Correction**: Use Integrated Change Control for baseline adjustments.
- **Governance**: Reporting performance to the right stakeholders.

---

## 👥 Resource Management Lifecycle (ECO 2.5.4)

### The Acquisition & Allocation Logic
1. **Acquire**: Secure the team and equipment (Internal vs. External).
2. **Develop**: Move the team through Tuckman’s Stages.
3. **Manage**: Support performance and resolve conflicts.
4. **Control**: Monitor utilization (Resource Histograms).
5. **Release**: Transition resources when they are no longer needed.

---

## 📂 Managing Project Artefacts (ECO 2.12)

### The Institutional Memory
- **What is an Artefact?**: Plans, registers, logs, reports, and deliverables.
- **The Process**: 
    - Create/Collect.
    - Store/Organize.
    - Manage/Version Control.
    - Dispose/Archive.

---

## 🛡️ Why Artefacts Matter

- **Knowledge Transfer**: Protecting the project from "brain drain."
- **Compliance & Audit**: Proving the project followed its own rules.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Feeding lessons learned back into the organization.
