# Executive Summary: Day 26 - WG2 (Part 1) Review & Practice

## 🎯 Strategic Objective
In the **PMP Mastery Framework**, Day 26 serves as our first **Integrative Milestone** for the Process Domain. Having covered the "How" (Methodology), the "What" (Scope), and the "Price" (Budget/Resources), we now consolidate these into a unified strategic perspective. A strategic peer knows that any change in one of these pillars forces a reaction in the others.

---

## 🔑 Weekly Consolidation (Foundational Pillars)

### 1. Methodology Selection (ECO 2.13)
- **The Core Decision**: Choosing between Predictive, Agile, or Hybrid based on **Complexity** and **Stability**.
- **Refinement**: Methodology isn't just a label; it defines our operational DNA.

### 2. Scope Stewardship (ECO 2.8)
- **The Foundation**: Requirements Elicitation -> Decomposition (WBS/Backlog) -> Validation.
- **The Shield**: Protecting the project from **Scope Creep** through formal boundaries and stakeholder alignment.

### 3. Fiscal & Resource Governance (ECO 2.5)
- **The Logistics**: Estimating (Analogous vs. Bottom-Up), Reserve Management (Contingency vs. Management), and Performance (EVM).
- **The Reality**: Ensuring the "Fuel" (Budget/Resources) matches the "Engine" (Methodology) and the "Load" (Scope).
