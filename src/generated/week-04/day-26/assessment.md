- **Protocol**: Choose the **BEST** answer based on the "Strategic Peer" mindset and PMP ECO guidelines.

### Questions

**Question 1**
A project manager is assigned to a project where the requirements are expected to evolve significantly as the product is developed, but the rigid organizational budget requires a fixed-price contract with external vendors. Which approach should the project manager recommend?
A. A pure Predictive approach to ensure the budget is respected.
B. A pure Agile approach to handle the evolving requirements.
C. A Hybrid approach using Predictive for vendor management and Agile for product development.
D. A Waterfall approach with an increased Management Reserve for the vendor.

**Question 2**
During a project's execution, a team member suggests adding a small feature that would significantly enhance user experience and only take two hours to complete. The customer is known to be very demanding. What should the project manager do?
A. Approve the change to build goodwill with the demanding stakeholder.
B. Instruct the team member to proceed but document the change in the status report.
C. Inform the team member that unapproved work (Gold Plating) is not allowed.
D. Ask the customer if they would like the feature included before starting work.

**Question 3**
A project manager is calculating the project vitals and finds that the Cost Performance Index (CPI) is 1.1 and the Schedule Performance Index (SPI) is 0.85. What is the status of the project?
A. Under budget and ahead of schedule.
B. Over budget and behind schedule.
C. Under budget and behind schedule.
D. Over budget and ahead of schedule.

**Question 4**
A project manager is building a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS). A stakeholder asks why they are including "Project Management" as a work package at the top level. What is the best explanation?
A. It accounts for the PM's salary and overhead costs.
B. According to the 100% Rule, all work required for the project, including management, must be in the WBS.
C. It is a placeholder for the Management Reserve.
D. It ensures the PM has authority over the other work packages.

**Question 5**
A project is facing high technical uncertainty and the stakeholders are frequently changing their minds about the UI design. However, the hardware components must be ordered 6 months in advance from a single supplier. Which methodology is best suited here?
A. Agile, to remain flexible for the UI.
B. Predictive, to ensure the hardware is ordered on time.
| C. Hybrid, using Predictive for hardware procurement and Agile for UI development.
D. Incremental, to deliver the hardware in stages.

**Question 6**
A project manager needs an accurate cost estimate for a complex new project. They have access to the detailed WBS and expert team members. Which estimation technique should they use?
A. Analogous
B. Parametric
C. Bottom-Up
D. Rough Order of Magnitude (ROM)

**Question 7**
During the formal validation of a deliverable, a key stakeholder refuses to sign off because they feel the feature doesn't meet their subjective aesthetic standards, even though it meets all documented requirements. What should the project manager do?
A. Escalate the stakeholder's refusal to the project sponsor.
B. Initiate a Change Request to adjust the UI to the stakeholder's preference.
C. Review the requirements and acceptance criteria with the stakeholder to confirm the deliverable meets the "Definition of Done."
D. Tell the team to redo the work to ensure stakeholder satisfaction.

**Question 8**
A risk that was identified in the Risk Register has occurred, costing the project $5,000 to resolve. Which reserve should the project manager use to cover this cost?
A. Management Reserve
B. Contingency Reserve
C. Project Baseline
D. Operational Budget

**Question 9**
A project manager is managing a global team. They find that the current physical resource utilization is at 110%, leading to burnout and equipment failure. What should the PM do FIRST?
A. Hire more staff immediately.
B. Perform an impact analysis and review the Resource Management Plan.
C. Lower the project's quality standards to reduce workload.
D. Ask the team to work overtime until the peak is over.

**Question 10**
A new project manager takes over a project in mid-execution. They need to find out why a specific design decision was made three months ago. Which project artefact should they consult?
A. Project Charter
B. Issue Log
C. Decision Log or Meeting Minutes
D. WBS Dictionary

### Answer Key & Strategic Rationale

| Q# | Correct | Task | The Strategic Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **C** | 2.13 | **The Hybrid Bridge.** Use Predictive for the fixed-price constraint/contract and Agile for the fluid discovery work. |
| **2** | **C** | 2.8 | **No Gold Plating.** Any work outside the baseline is a failure of scope management, even if the intentions are good. |
| **3** | **C** | 2.5 | **Rich but Slow.** CPI > 1.0 = Under Budget. SPI < 1.0 = Behind Schedule. |
| **4** | **B** | 2.8 | **The 100% Rule.** WBS is the sum of ALL work. Management is work. |
| **5** | **C** | 2.13 | **Contextual Tailoring.** Procurement is stable/long-lead (Predictive); software/UI is fluid (Agile). |
| **6** | **C** | 2.5 | **Precision Aggregation.** Bottom-up is the most accurate when you have the WBS and the experts. |
| **7** | **C** | 2.8 | **Objectivism over Subjectvism.** Validate against the *agreed* criteria. Subjective feedback belongs in a new Change Request, not as a reason for rejection of compliant work. |
| **8** | **B** | 2.5 | **Known-Unknowns.** Risks identified in the register are funded by the Contingency Reserve. |
| **9** | **B** | 2.5 | **Assess then Act.** You must understand the root cause and resource plan before adding headcount or forcing overtime. |
| **10** | **C** | 2.12 | **Institutional Memory.** Artefacts like Decision Logs are the primary source for historical context. |
