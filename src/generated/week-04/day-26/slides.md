## 🧠 Strategic Pillar 1: Methodology (ECO 2.13)

### The Decision Core
- **Assess Complexity**: Stacey Matrix logic.

---

## 📐 Strategic Pillar 2: Scope (ECO 2.8)

### The Boundary Core
- **Requirements**: Prioritize using MoSCoW/Kano.
- **Decomposition**: WBS (Predictive) vs. Backlog (Agile).
- **Control**: Preventing Scope Creep through formal validation.

---

## 💰 Strategic Pillar 3: Budget & Resources (ECO 2.5)

### The Fiscal Core
- **Estimation**: Analogous / Parametric / Bottom-Up.
- **Reserves**: Contingency (Known risks) vs. Management (Unknowns).
- **Control**: Using EVM (CPI/SPI) to monitor project vitals.

---

## 🔗 The Integrative Link (ECO 2.9)

### The Ripple Effect
- If **Scope** expands, **Budget** must follow.
- If **Resources** are limited, **Schedule** will delay.
- If **Methodology** is Agile, **Scope** remains fluid.

**Challenge**: A junior PM tells you, "I don't need a WBS; I have a list of tasks in my head."

## 📝 Practice Question Strategy

1. **Scenario**: Identify the situation.
2. **Task**: What are you being asked? (Next step? First action?)
3. **Mindset**: Think like a Strategic Peer. 
   - *Proactive over Reactive.*
   - *Assess over Action.*
   - *Formal over Informal.*
