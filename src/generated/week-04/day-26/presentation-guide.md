# Presentation Guide: Day 26 - WG2 (Part 1) Review & Practice

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today is about **Mastery**. This is a review day, but it shouldn't feel passive. You are a **mentor** today. Your tone is **Encouraging** but **Rigorous**. You are checking for cracks in their foundation before moving to more complex schedule and quality topics next week.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Well done on surviving Week 4. We’ve built the engine, defined the load, and estimated the fuel. Today, we make sure it all actually works together."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: Emphasize that today is for **Consolidation**. We aren't adding new info; we are solidifying the existing logic.

### Slide 3: The Journey
- **Recap**: Briefly touch on the four days. Ask the group, "Which of these four days felt the most challenging in practice?"

### Slide 4-6: The Pillars
- **Quick-Fire Review**: Don't lecture. Ask questions instead.
- *Methodology*: "When would you choose Hybrid over pure Agile?"
- *Scope*: "What is the danger of Gold Plating if the customer likes it?"
- *Budget*: "Who owns the Management Reserve?"

### Slide 7: The Integrative Link
- **The Core Message**: This is the most crucial takeaway. PMP Mastery is about seeing the **connections**. 

### Slide 8: Mentorship Activity
- **Roleplay**: Have them practice explaining the WBS to a junior staffer. This forces them to simplify the 100% Rule logic.

### Slide 9: Question Strategy
- **Coaching Tip**: Remind them that on the exam, the PM is always **proactive**. If the question asks "What should the PM do next?", the answer is usually **Assess**, **Analyze**, or **Review document**, not "Fix it immediately."

---

## 🎯 Practice Session Management
- Encourage them to complete the **Day 26 Practice Unit** (10 questions).
- **Debrief**: Walk through the rationales. Focus on the *Why* of the correct answer, not just the fact that it is correct.

---

## 🛑 Common Misconceptions to Clarify
- **WBS = Schedule**: Clarify one last time: WBS is **What** we deliver. Schedule is **When** we deliver it.
- **CPI and SPI interpretations**: Ensure they know that < 1.0 is ALWAYS bad in indexing, but a negative Variance is what matters for reporting thresholds.
- **Agile is "No Documentation"**: Remind them that Agile has heavy artefact management (Backlog, Burn charts, User Stories)—it’s just *different* documentation.
