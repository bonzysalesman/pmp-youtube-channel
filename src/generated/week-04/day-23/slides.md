# Day 23: Plan and Manage Scope
### Defining Boundaries and Delivering Value (ECO 2.8)

---

## 🎯 The Strategic Goal of Scope

> "Include all the work required, and ONLY the work required."

- **Objective**: Prevent unmanaged expansion (Scope Creep).
- **Outcome**: Successful achievement of project goals.
- **Metric**: Formal stakeholder acceptance of deliverables.

---

## 🧠 The Scope Management Cycle

1. **Elicit**: Gather needs from stakeholders.
2. **Analyze**: Clarify, categorize, and validate feasibility.
3. **Break Down**: Decompose into manageable units (WBS/Backlog).
4. **Govern**: Monitor status, manage changes, and validate results.

---

## 🔍 Requirements Intelligence (ECO 2.8.1)

### Tools for Elicitation
- Interviews & Focus Groups.
- Workshops (JAD/QFD).
- Prototyping & Observation.

### Prioritization (The Value Filter)
- **MoSCoW**: Must, Should, Could, Won't.
- **Kano Model**: Delighters vs. Basic Needs.
- **Agile**: The Product Backlog (Prioritized by Value).

---

## 🏗️ Breaking Down Scope (ECO 2.8.2)

### The WBS (Predictive)
- **100% Rule**: Total work = Sum of work packages.
- **Deliverable-Oriented**: Focus on what is built, not how.
- **WBS Dictionary**: The "Manual" for each work package.

### The Backlog (Agile)
- **Dynamic List**: Prioritized by the Product Owner.
- **Continuous Refinement**: Decomposition happens "Just-in-Time."

---

## 🛡️ The Scope Baseline

Three critical components for Predictive projects:
1. **Project Scope Statement**: Objectives, Exclusions, Acceptance Criteria.
2. **WBS**: Hierarchical chart of deliverables.
3. **WBS Dictionary**: Deep-dive details for every WBS element.

---

## 🚧 Stewardship: Monitor & Control (ECO 2.8.3)

### Validate Scope
- **Focus**: Formal **Acceptance**.
- **Action**: Stakeholder inspection of deliverables.
- **Result**: Signed acceptance vs. Change Requests for rework.

### Control Scope
- **Focus**: Preventing **Scope Creep**.
- **Action**: Management via Integrated Change Control.
- **Mantra**: "No work begins without a clear, approved requirement."

---

## 📂 Strategic Integration

Scope planning is the "Foundational Sheet" for:
- **Schedule**: You can't estimate time for undefined work.
- **Budget**: Every scope item has a cost.
- **Quality**: Scope defines the standards we must meet.
- **Resources**: Who does the work?
