# Presentation Guide: Day 23 - Plan & Manage Scope

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is about **Discipline**. Scope Management is often where "polite" PMs fail. Your tone should be **Steadfast** and **Protective**. You are teaching your peers how to build a shield around the project's vision.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Scope is the most vulnerable part of our project plan. Today, we learn how to define the boundaries and, more importantly, how to defend them."

### Slide 2: What is Scope?
- **Coach’s Corner**: Emphasize the word **"ONLY"**. Many high-achieving PMs think "more is better." For the PMP, "more" is a failure of baseline management (Gold Plating).

### Slide 3: Learning Outcomes
- **Strategic Connection**: Connect 2.8.1 (gathering) to 2.8.3 (controlling). You can't control what you didn't define correctly at the start.

### Slide 4: Requirements intelligence (ECO 2.8.1)
- **Peer Insight**: "In the real world, we often just take what we're given. In the PMP world, a lead PM evaluates and prioritizes based on **Value**."
- **Discussion**: Ask the group, "Have you ever had a project fail because you said 'Yes' to too many 'Could-haves'?"

### Slide 5: Breaking Down Scope (ECO 2.8.2)
- **Key Technical Note**: Hammer the **100% Rule**. If it’s not in the WBS, it’s not in the project. If it’s in the WBS, it MUST be in the budget and schedule.

### Slide 6: The Scope Baseline
- **The "Audit Trail"**: Explain that the Scope Statement isn't just a list; it includes **Exclusions**. Telling the client what you *aren't* doing is as important as telling them what you *are* doing.

### Slide 7: Monitoring & Validating (ECO 2.8.3)
- **Crucial Distinction**: Clarify **Validate Scope** vs. **Control Quality**. 
- *Analogy*: Quality is the chef checking if the steak is cooked properly. Validation is the customer tasting it and deciding if it’s what they ordered.

### Slide 8: Inputs
- **Holistic View**: Use this to show that Day 22's decision (Methodology) drastically changes how Day 23 is executed.

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Boundary Guard"
**Objective**: Test the "Assess before Action" mindset.
- **Correct Answer logic**: 
    1. **FIRST** action: Assess the impact on the project (Time, Cost, Resources).
    2. Document the request and take it through the formal **Change Control Process**.
    3. *Never* allow the team to begin work without formal approval, even if it's "small."

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **The "Yes-Man" PM**: Address the fear of telling executives "No." Reframe it as: "I’m not saying no to the feature; I’m saying no to unmanaged risk."
- **WBS confusion**: Make sure they understand that a WBS is **not** a task list (activities). It is a list of **results** (deliverables). Tasks come in Day 29 (Schedule).
