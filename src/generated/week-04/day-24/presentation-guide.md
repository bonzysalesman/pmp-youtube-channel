# Presentation Guide: Day 24 - Budget & Resources (Pt 1)

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today is about **Precision** and **Accountability**. When talking about money and resources, your tone should be **Clinically Objective**. You are helping your peers see the budget as a strategic asset, not just a spreadsheet.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Scope is what we want; Budget is what we can afford. Today, we synchronize the two. Welcome to Day 24."

### Slide 2: Introduction to 2.5
- **The Process domain**: Remind them that this is where the bulk of the 'technical' exam questions live. 50% of the exam comes from these process groups.

### Slide 3: Purpose & Importance
- **The Triple Constraint**: Remind them that Budget and Resources are the "Fuel" for the Scope/Schedule/Quality triangle.

### Slide 4: Key Activities (Task 2.5)
- **The Lifecycle**: Briefly walk through Estimate -> Budget -> Acquire -> Manage -> Control. Emphasize that these aren't siloed; they're a continuous cycle.

### Slide 5: Estimating Budgetary Needs (ECO 2.5.1)
- **Strategic Note**: Focus on the **Analogous/Parametric/Bottom-Up** distinction. This is a favorite area for the exam. 
- **Coach’s Tip**: "Analogous is for speed; Bottom-Up is for accuracy. Parametric is for data."

### Slide 6: Reserves (The Hierarchy)
- **Crucial Distinction**: Spend extra time here. Candidates *always* mix up Contingency vs. Management reserves.
- **Rule of Thumb**: "If you wrote a risk about it, use the Contingency. If you didn't see it coming, call the Sponsor for Management reserves."

### Slide 7: Resource Intelligence
- **Human vs. Physical**: Ensure they don't forget the physical side (equipment/licenses).
- **Availability**: Explain that a PM often "borrows" staff in a Matrix org. Availability is a constant negotiation.

### Slide 8: The Plan
- **The Manual**: The Resource Management Plan is the "How-to" guide. It defines the *strategy* for getting and keeping resources.

### Slide 9: Interconnections
- **The Web**: Show how a change in Scope (Day 23) forces a re-estimation in Budget (Day 24), which then impacts the Schedule (Day 29).

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Estimation Challenge"
**Objective**: Practical application of Parametric vs. Three-Point estimation.
- **Expected Outcome**: 
    1. For the migrations: **Parametric** (10 past migrations @ 40hrs each = 400 hrs).
    2. For the custom modules: **Three-Point (PERT)** or **Bottom-Up** with a healthy **Contingency Reserve**.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **"Budget" vs. "Baseline"**: Remind them that the Cost Baseline is what *they* manage. The Total Project Budget is what the *Company* sees (Baseline + Management Reserves).
- **Resource Histograms**: Briefly mention that we will see more of this in Part 2, but the plan must account for "When" people are available.
