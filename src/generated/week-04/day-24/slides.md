# Day 24: Plan & Manage Budget and Resources
### Fueling the Venture (ECO 2.5)

---

## 🎯 The Financial Goal

> "Deliver value without depleting the treasury."

- **Objective**: Ensure the project is adequately funded and staffed.
- **Outcome**: A defensible **Cost Baseline** and **Resource Management Plan**.
- **Metric**: Adherence to the Performance Measurement Baseline (PMB).

---

## 🏗️ The Budgeting Lifecycle

1. **Estimate**: Quantify the cost of work and resources.
2. **Budget**: Aggregate estimates and add reserves.
3. **Acquire**: Secure the people and materials.
4. **Govern**: Control the burn rate and utilization.

---

## 🔍 Estimation Strategies (ECO 2.5.1)

| Technique | Accuracy | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Analogous** | Low | Early "Ballpark" estimates. |
| **Parametric** | High | Repetitive, data-driven work. |
| **Bottom-Up** | Very High | Final planning; detailed WBS. |
| **Three-Point** | Risk-Adjusted | Account for uncertainty (PERT). |

---

## 🛡️ The Hierarchy of Reserves

- **Contingency Reserves**:
    - For "Known-Unknowns" (Identified Risks).
    - Part of the **Cost Baseline**.
- **Management Reserves**:
    - For "Unknown-Unknowns" (Unforeseen Events).
    - Part of the **Project Budget** but *not* the Baseline.

---

## 👥 Resource Intelligence (ECO 2.5.2)

### Human & Physical Capital
- **Labor**: Skilled vs. Unskilled, Internal vs. External.
- **Materials**: Consumables needed for the build.
- **Equipment**: Heavy machinery, software licenses, facilities.

### Availability Audit
- Assess internal skills vs. external requirements.
- Identify training needs (Links to Task 1.5).

---

## 📜 The Resource Management Plan

**Components of the Strategy**:
- **Acquisition**: How do we get them? (Hiring vs. Contracting).
- **Allocation**: When do we need them? (Resource Histograms).
- **Control**: How do we track their usage?
- **Development**: How do we improve the team's capacity?

---

## 📂 Strategic Integration

Budget/Resource planning is the "Fuel Gauge" for:
- **Scope**: Defines what we are buying.
- **Schedule**: Resource availability impacts activity duration.
- **Risk**: Many risks are purely financial or resource-based.
- **Procurement**: Defines how we interact with vendors.
