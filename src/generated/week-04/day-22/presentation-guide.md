# Presentation Guide: Day 22 - Determine Methodology

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today, you are coaching your peers on making one of the most consequential decisions in the project life cycle. Your tone is **Analytical** and **Authoritative**. You aren't just teaching definitions; you are helping them build the framework for **Strategic Selection**.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Welcome to Month 2. We’ve built our team; now it’s time to choose the engine that will drive us. Day 22 is where we define our operational logic."
- **Context**: We are entering the **Process Domain**—the heavy-lifting part of the exam (50%).

### Slide 2: Why Methodology Matters
- **Strategic Pivot**: Emphasize that choosing a methodology is a risk management activity. 
- **Peer Insight**: "As senior PMs, we’ve all seen what happens when you try to force a Waterfall process onto an innovation project. It’s a waste of energy."

### Slide 3: Life Cycle Spectrum
- **The Nuance**: Don't just define; compare. 
- **Coaching Tip**: Remind them that **Iterative** is about getting the *right* answer (rework), while **Incremental** is about getting *fast* answers (adding pieces).

### Slide 4: Assessing Project Needs (ECO 2.13.1)
- **Discussion Point**: Ask, "Which factor is your biggest constraint right now—Requirements or Culture?"
- **Keywords**: "Clarity," "Complexity," "Culture."

### Slide 5: Execution Strategy (ECO 2.13.2)
- **The Real World vs. PMP**: Acknowledge that in the real world, we sometimes get forced into a strategy by a contract. For the exam, the PM should **recommend** the strategy that fits the methodology choice.

### Slide 6: Choosing the Methodology (ECO 2.13.3)
- **Mindset Rule**: "Assess before Action." You don't choose because you like Agile; you choose because the assessment *demanded* it.

### Slide 7: Tailoring (ECO 2.13.4)
- **The Strategic Differentiator**: This is the most important slide. 
- **Peer Insight**: "Tailoring isn't breaking the rules. Tailoring IS the rule. A Waterfall project with Stand-ups isn't 'confused'; it's 'Optimized'."

### Slide 8: Integration
- **The Holistic View**: Remind them that this decision ripples through every other Day in this Month. If we choose Agile, our Scope (Day 23) will look like a Backlog. If we choose Predictive, it will look like a WBS.

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Verdict"
**Objective**: Practice the "Assessment -> Recommendation" logic.
- **Expected Outcome**: They should suggest a **Hybrid** approach. Predictive/Waterfall for the "Hard" compliance/regulatory tracks, and Agile for the "Soft" AI development tracks.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **The "Agile is Better" Bias**: Remind them that on the PMP exam, Predictive is not "old-fashioned"; it is "Appropriate" for certain scenarios.
- **The Hybrid Trap**: Many candidates think Hybrid is just a "middle ground." Clarify that it is the **intentional use** of both methods simultaneously on different workstreams.
