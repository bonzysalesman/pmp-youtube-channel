# Day 22: Determine Appropriate Project Methodology
### Aligning Frameworks with Project DNA (ECO 2.13)

---

## 🗺️ The Process Domain: Month 2
### Starting the Project

- **Phase 1**: Determine Methodology (Our current focus)
- **Phase 2**: Scope, Budget, and Resource Planning
- **Phase 3**: Schedule, Quality, and Procurement Integration

---

## 🎯 Why Selection Matters

> "There is no 'one-size-fits-all' in project management."

- **The Goal**: Maximize value, minimize waste.
- **The Risk**: Using rigid processes for fluid scope, or loose processes for high-risk complexity.
- **The PM's Job**: Clinical assessment of project characteristics.

---

## 🧬 Understanding the Life Cycle Spectrum

| Methodology | Best Suited For... | Key Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Predictive** | Stable requirements, repetitive tech. | Scope/Schedule/Cost Baselines. |
| **Iterative** | High complexity; need to refine quality. | Correctness of solution. |
| **Incremental** | Need for speed; frequent small delivery. | Speed of delivery. |
| **Agile** | High uncertainty; evolving scope. | Customer Value & Adaptability. |
| **Hybrid** | Blended certainty levels. | Strategic Balancing. |

---

## 🔍 Assessing Project Needs (ECO 2.13.1)

Before you choose, you must **Audit**:
1. **Scope Stability**: How likely is it to change?
2. **Technical Uncertainty**: Have we done this before?
3. **Organizational Culture**: Will the company support Agility?
4. **Criticality**: What is the cost of failure?

---

## 📈 Recommending Execution Strategy (ECO 2.13.2)

### The Strategic Alignment
- **Contracting**: Does the vendor agreement support changes?
- **Funding**: Is the budget released in chunks or all at once?
- **Resources**: Does the team have the skills for the chosen approach?

---

## ⚖️ Choosing the Methodology (ECO 2.13.3)

### The Decision Matrix
- **Waterfall/Predictive**: "We know exactly what we are building."
- **Agile**: "We know the goal, but the path is uncertain."
- **Hybrid**: "We are building a stable house (Predictive) but the interior design is evolving (Agile)."

---

## 🛠️ The Art of Tailoring (ECO 2.13.4)

### Incorporating Iterative Practices
Regardless of the core methodology, use:
- **Lessons Learned**: Continuous improvement.
- **Prototyping**: Early feedback.
- **Short Feedback Loops**: Maintain stakeholder alignment.
- **Pilots/Demos**: Validate assumptions early.

---

## 📂 Considerations & Integration

### The Holistic View
- **Integration**: Methodology dictactes how you plan Scope, Schedule, and Budget.
- **Collaboration**: Engage stakeholders early to ensure buy-in on the *way* we work.
- **Standards**: Respect organizational process assets (OPAs) while remaining proactive.

---

## 🎯 Activity: The Verdict

**Scenario**: You are leading a project to develop an AI-driven marketing tool. The technology is new, but the regulatory compliance requirements are rigid and fixed.

1. Which primary methodology would you choose?
2. How would you **Tailor** it to handle the fixed compliance vs. the uncertain tech?
