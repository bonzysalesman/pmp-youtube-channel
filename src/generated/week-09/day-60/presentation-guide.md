# Presentation Guide: Day 60 - The Master of Dissonance

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Emotional Architects**. You are teaching your peers how to be the "Calm in the Storm." Your tone should be **Steadfast**, **Empathetic**, and **Neutral**. You are helping them master the situational judgment required to de-escalate tension while keeping the project moving.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "A project without conflict is a project that isn't doing anything important. Welcome to Day 60."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: This is about **Preservation**. Preserving the team's ability to work together.

### Slide 3: The Conflict Diagnostic
- **The Core Focus**: "The Why."
- **Peer Insight**: "Most project conflicts are structural, not personal. If people are arguing over resources, don't fix the relationship—fix the resource plan."

### Slide 4: Resolution Modes
- **The Technical "Why"**: This is the heart of the exam section.
- **Coaching Tip**: "The exam loves to trick you into 'Compromising.' Remind them that Collaborate is the only true prize. Always reach for Collaborate first."

### Slide 5: The EQ Layer
- **The Strategy**: De-escalation through ego state management.
- **Peer Insight**: "If you enter a conflict as a 'Parent' or a 'Child,' you've already lost. Stay in 'Adult' mode—curious, objective, and calm."

### Slide 6: The Resolution Lifecycle
- **The Strategy**: Precision.
- **Coaching Note**: "Conflict management is a process. Don't skip the analysis phase just because you're uncomfortable with the tension."

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Resource Tug-of-War"
**Objective**: Moving from competition to collaboration.
- **Action**: **Facilitate Win-Win**.
- **Resolution**: Bringing both leads together (Collaborate). Ask: "The objective is for *both* milestones to be hit. How can we re-schedule or scale the testing so both can happen?" You act as the mediator, not the judge. Only use "Force" if they cannot reach a resolution in time for the work to begin.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **Avoidance**: Many PMs avoid conflict, hoping it goes away. Remind them: "Unresolved conflict becomes a technical debt of the team."
- **Favorite-Playing**: Warn them against taking sides. The PM must remain neutral to maintain trust.
- **Subjective Reasoning**: If you use "Force," base it on the **Project Charter** or **Strategic Value**, not personal preference.
