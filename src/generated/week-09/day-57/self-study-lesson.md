# Self-Study Lesson: Day 57 - The Servant Architect

## 🧠 The Strategic Perspective
Welcome to Day 57. Today, we return to the **People Domain**. 

As a Strategic Peer, you know that "Leadership" is often tested through the lens of **Servant Leadership** and **Inclusion**. On the PMP exam, you aren't just a boss; you are a facilitator. You must be able to choose the right style for the right moment and ensure the team remains motivated through vision, not authority.

---

## 1. The Servant Leadership Core (Sec 1.2.3)
This is the highest-yield concept in the People Domain.
- **Primary Goal**: To serve the team’s needs first.
- **The Checklist**:
    - Do you remove impediments?
    - Do you coach instead of direct?
    - Do you foster collaboration?
    - Do you build trust through transparency?
- **Relationship to Agile**: Servant leadership is the *mandatory* style for Agile teams.

---

## 2. Navigating Leadership Styles (Sec 1.2.4)
The exam tests your ability to **Adapt**. 
- **Directive**: For low-maturity teams or urgent crises.
- **Coaching**: For teams needing skill development.
- **Delegating**: For high-performing, mature teams.
- **The Goleman Styles**: Know when to be *Visionary* (Change) vs. *Democratic* (Building consensus) vs. *Commanding* (Emergency only).

---

## 3. Motivation & Influence (Sec 1.2.5 - 1.2.6)
How do you get people to work without using "Position Power"?
- **Intrinsic Motivation**: Purpose, Autonomy, Mastery.
- **Influence**: Using your network and expertise rather than your title.
- **The PMP Way**: Always seek to understand the *person's* motivators (Herzberg/McClelland) to align them with the project's goals.

---

## 🎯 PMP Mindset Tip: "The Leader’s Default"
In situational questions, if the team is mature and the task is clear, **Delegate**. If the team is struggling with a blocker, **Serve**. If they lack direction, **Invision**. Avoid "Commanding" unless there is a fire (literally or figuratively).

---

## 🛠️ Performance Activity: The Style Swap
1. **Current Style**: Identify your default leadership style (e.g., Democratic).
2. **The Stress Test**: Imagine your project has just been fast-tracked by 3 months. Does your style need to shift? (e.g., to Coaching or momentarily Directive).
3. **The Inclusion Audit**: Look at your team. Who is the "quietest" voice? How can you use your influence to bring their perspective to the next planning session?
