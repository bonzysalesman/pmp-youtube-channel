# Day 59: The Path-Clearer
### Eradicating Blockers to Velocity (ECO 1.7)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "The obstacle is the way."

- **Objective 1**: Proactively **Identify** impediments vs. blockers.
- **Objective 2**: **Prioritize** based on critical path and impact.
- **Objective 3**: Leverage **Networks** for resolution.

---

## 🔍 Spotting the Friction (ECO 1.7.1)

- **Technical**: Bugs, environment issues.
- **Process**: Excessive approvals, unclear DoD.
- **Organizational**: Siloes, resource conflicts.
- **Environmental**: Distractions, poor tools.

**Strategic Tool**: The **Daily Stand-up** is your primary radar.

---

## ⚖️ Strategic Prioritization (ECO 1.7.2)

### The Rank order
1. **Critical Path Impact**: Stop the bleeding first.
2. **Team-Wide Blockers**: High-scale friction.
3. **High Frustration**: Morale-saving wins.
4. **Minor Inconveniences**: Low-hanging fruit.

---

## 🤝 The Resolution Cycle (ECO 1.7.3)

1. **Identify**: Clear definition of the problem.
2. **Ownership**: Assigning it to the right person/department.
3. **Facilitate**: Providing the resources needed to fix it.
4. **shield**: Keeping the team working while the fix is in progress.

**PM Rule**: Do not let the team "Solve for Bureaucracy." That's your job.

---

## ✅ Continuous Reassessment (ECO 1.7.4)

- **Verification**: Is it really gone?
- **Systemic Audit**: Is this a one-time issue or a systemic flaw?
- **OPA Update**: Document the solution to prevent recurrence across the organization.

---

## 🎯 Activity: The Bottleneck

**Scenario**: A middle manager from another department is refusing to release a required approval, claiming they "don't do Agile." The team is blocked on a critical milestone.

1. Do you **Inform** the team they have to wait?
2. Do you **Demand** the approval via email?
3. What is your **Strategic Influence Move**? (Answer: Face-to-Face meeting to align goals).
