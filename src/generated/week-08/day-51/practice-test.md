# WG3 Comprehensive Practice Test: Doing the Work

## 🎯 Exam Instructions
This test contains **40 situational questions** designed to simulate the complexity and ambiguity of the PMP exam. Use the **PMP Mindset** (Assess -> Plan -> Action) to navigate these scenarios.

---

### Part 1: Risk & Value (Days 36-37)

**Q1:** A project manager is leading a hybrid project. During a sprint review, a stakeholder identifies a new risk that could impact the project's ability to deliver its primary value proposition. What is the project manager's **first** action?
A. Update the Risk Register.
B. Implement the most likely contingency plan.
C. Facilitate a meeting with the team to assess the impact and probability.
D. Escalate the risk to the Project Sponsor.

**Q2:** A project team is using an incremental delivery approach to ensure early value. After delivering the second increment, the customer provides feedback that the features do not fully meet their needs. What should the project manager do?
A. Charge the customer for a change request to fix the features.
B. Review the project's business case and the Definition of Done.
C. Incorporate the feedback into the Product Backlog and reprioritize.
D. Inform the customer that the requirements were baselined during planning.

**Q3:** A risk that was previously identified as "Low Probability" has occurred, causing a significant delay. The Project Manager discovers that no specific contingency plan was developed for this risk. This situation represents a breakdown in:
A. Risk Identification.
B. Risk Response Planning.
C. Qualitative Risk Analysis.
D. Monitoring and Controlling Risks.

**Q4:** To maximize value in a highly uncertain environment, a project manager should prioritize:
A. Rigid adherence to the original scope statement.
B. The delivery of the Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
C. Extensive upfront planning and documentation.
D. Strict control over stakeholder communications.

### Part 2: Communication & Stakeholders (Days 38-39)

**Q5:** A key stakeholder complains that they are being overwhelmed with technical reports that are not relevant to their role. Which document should the Project Manager review and update?
A. Project Management Plan.
B. Stakeholder Engagement Plan.
C. Communications Management Plan.
D. Information Management System.

**Q6:** A project manager notices that two critical stakeholders have conflicting expectations regarding a project milestone. What is the **best** way for the project manager to handle this?
A. Make an executive decision on the milestone date.
B. Facilitate a meeting with both stakeholders to align expectations and find consensus.
C. Follow the instructions of the stakeholder with higher organizational authority.
D. Escalate the conflict to the Change Control Board.

**Q7:** You are a Project Manager leading a virtual team. You notice that communication is becoming siloed and team members are losing trust. Which **tacit knowledge** sharing technique would be most effective?
A. Mandating a weekly technical report.
B. Increasing the frequency of "all-hands" status meetings.
C. Organizing virtual "coffee chats" or open retrospectives.
D. Updating the Project Management Information System (PMIS).

**Q8:** Which communication method is most appropriate for discussing sensitive or complex issues that require immediate feedback?
A. Push Communication (Email).
B. Pull Communication (SharePoint).
C. Interactive Communication (Face-to-Face or Video Call).
D. Formal Communication (Project Report).

### Part 3: Artifacts & Changes (Days 43-44)

**Q9:** A project manager is auditing the project's artifacts and finds that a draft version of the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) was used for cost estimating. What is the primary risk here?
A. A failure in Stakeholder Engagement.
B. A breakdown in Configuration Management.
C. An error in the Communications Management Plan.
D. A lack of proper Procurement Management.

**Q10:** A change request has been submitted to add a feature that will cost $10,000 and delay the project by one week. The Project Manager's assessment shows that this feature is critical for regulatory compliance. What should the PM do?
A. Approve the change immediately to ensure compliance.
B. Reject the change due to the schedule delay and budget impact.
C. Perform a thorough impact analysis and present the request to the CCB.
D. Ask the Legal Department to approve the change first.

**Q11:** Once a change request is **approved** by the Change Control Board (CCB), what is the Project Manager's **first** administrative action?
A. Inform the project team.
B. Update the Project Management Plan and baselines.
C. Update the Change Log.
D. Issue a payment to the vendor for the additional work.

**Q12:** A project manager is working in a predictive environment. A stakeholder asks for a change that does not impact the baselines but does improve the "look and feel" of a report. The PM should:
A. Implement the change as it is a "minor tweak."
B. Request a formal change request as per the Change Management Plan.
C. Ask the Sponsor for permission.
D. Ignore the request as it is out of scope.

### Part 4: Issues & Knowledge (Days 43, 45)

**Q13:** An issue is logged because a critical piece of equipment has failed. The team implements a "temporary fix" to keep the project moving while they wait for a replacement part. This is an example of a:
A. Corrective Action.
B. Preventive Action.
C. Workaround.
D. Defect Repair.

**Q14:** To prevent the recurrence of a systemic problem, which analytical technique is most appropriate?
A. Monte Carlo Simulation.
B. Root Cause Analysis (RCA).
C. Sensitivity Analysis.
D. Earned Value Analysis.

**Q15:** A project manager is concerned about the "Single Point of Failure" regarding a developer who holds unique knowledge of the system's legacy code. What is the best strategy to manage this **tacit knowledge**?
A. Write a 50-page technical manual.
B. Implement a "Job Shadowing" or "Pair Programming" initiative.
C. Record a series of training videos.
D. Store all source code in a Version Control System.

**Q16:** You are managing a project where a vendor is consistently delivering late. You have reviewed the contract and confirmed they are in breach of their delivery SLAs. Your **first** step should be:
A. Terminate the contract immediately.
B. File a formal claim through the legal department.
C. Meet with the vendor to discuss the performance issues and seek a negotiated resolution.
D. Deduct the late fees from the vendor's next invoice without notice.

### Part 5: Integrated Scenarios (Holistic)

**Q17:** A project manager is informed that a new regulation has been passed that will take effect in six months. This new regulation will require significant changes to the project's deliverables. What is the Project Manager's **sequence of actions**?
A. Update the Risk Register -> Perform Impact Analysis -> Submit Change Request.
B. Submit Change Request -> Perform Impact Analysis -> Update Project Plan.
C. Tell the team to stop work -> Call the CCB -> Inform the Sponsor.
D. Perform Impact Analysis -> Update the Risk Register -> Inform the Stakeholders.

**Q18:** During project execution, a team member discovers an innovative way to reduce the project's energy consumption, which aligns with the organization's sustainability goals. However, implementing this would require changing the already-purchased materials. The PM should:
A. Reject the idea because the materials have already been bought.
B. Implement the change immediately to capture the value.
C. Ask the team member to submit a Change Request and perform a cost-benefit analysis.
D. Escalate the opportunity to the Project Sponsor to ask for more budget.

**Q19:** A project manager is leading a project where the team is struggling with highly technical issues. The PM decides to hire an external consultant. This is an example of which risk response?
A. Mitigate.
B. Transfer.
C. Share.
D. Avoid.

**Q20:** A project manager finds that the Issue Log is rarely updated, and many team members are resolving problems without documenting them. Why is this a strategic concern?
A. It makes the PM look uninformed to senior management.
B. It prevents the capture of Lessons Learned and threatens Configuration Management.
C. It reduces the project's budget as undocumented work is harder to track.
D. It increases the probability of stakeholders demanding more changes.

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Rationales (Strategic Peer Analysis)

1.  **C**. **Assess then Act**. You must assess before you update the register or implementation.
2.  **C**. **Agile Adaptive Planning**. Value is driven by feedback. Reprioritize the backlog.
3.  **B**. **Response Planning Failure**. Qualitative analysis assesses; Response Planning creates the contingency.
4.  **B**. **Value Centricity**. The MVP is the fastest way to validate assumptions in uncertain conditions.
5.  **C**. **Governance Alignment**. Communications Plan dictates the frequency, format, and recipient of information.
6.  **B**. **Collaborative Leadership**. The PM is a consensus builder. Seek alignment through direct engagement.
7.  **C**. **Tacit Knowledge Transfer**. Real trust and "know-how" are shared through human interaction, not reports.
8.  **C**. **Richness of Channel**. For complex issues, interactive communication minimizes misunderstanding.
9.  **B**. **Version Integrity**. Configuration management ensures the integrity of the project's artifacts. Using an old version is a classic breakdown.
10. **C**. **Institutional Process**. Even critical changes must pass through the Integrated Change Control process.
11. **B**. **Consistency of Source**. The PMP Plan is the project's "Bible." Update it before you tell anyone to start working.
12. **B**. **Process Stewardship**. In Predictive, even "minor" changes follow the Change Management Plan to prevent scope creep.
13. **C**. **Tactical Resilience**. A workaround is an immediate, temporary response to an unplanned problem.
14. **B**. **Systemic Stewardship**. RCA aims to find the underlying cause to prevent recurrence.
15. **B**. **Experiential Learning**. You cannot codify (Explicit) a master's intuition (Tacit). They must teach through doing.
16. **C**. **Negotiated Settlement**. Always try to solve problems collaboratively before moving to legal or terminal actions.
17. **A**. **The Governance Flow**. Recognize (Risk) -> Assessment (Analyze) -> Governance (Change).
18. **C**. **Value-Based Governance**. Innovation is good, but any baseline change requires an impact analysis and formal CR.
19. **A**. **Mitigation**. Hiring expertise is a classic way to reduce the probability or impact of technical risks.
20. **B**. **Institutional Knowledge**. Undocumented resolutions are lost opportunities for growth and undermine document integrity.
