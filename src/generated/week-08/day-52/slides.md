## 🕵️‍♂️ Dissecting the Distractor

### Scenario: A Stakeholder asks for a tweak.
- **Distractor A**: "Tell them no." (Too aggressive).
- **Distractor B**: "Do it." (Scope creep/No governance).
- **Distractor C**: "Submit to CCB." (Premature - no analysis).
- **✅ Correct**: "Assess the impact." (Mindset: **Assess then Act**).
