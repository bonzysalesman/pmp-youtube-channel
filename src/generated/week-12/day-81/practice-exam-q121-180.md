## 🏗️ The Exam (Questions 121-180)

**Q121: The Procurement Conflict**
A vendor has successfully delivered all project artifacts as per the statement of work (SOW). However, the Project Manager discovers that the vendor's lead engineer was simultaneously working for a direct competitor. What is the PM's **mandatory** action?
A. Refuse to pay the final invoice.
B. Formally close the procurement while documenting the conflict of interest in the vendor performance evaluation.
C. Demand the vendor pay a penalty fee.
D. Sue the vendor for breach of contract immediately.

**Q122: The Emotional Intelligence Pivot**
During a high-stakes project meeting, a senior stakeholder begins shouting and insulting the project team for a minor technical delay. The team looks to you, the PM, for a response. Applying EQ, what is your **best** move?
A. Shout back to re-establish authority and defend the team.
B. Calmly request a 10-minute break to allow emotions to cool, then address the technical issue objectively with the stakeholder in private.
C. Terminate the project since the working environment is toxic.
D. Agree with the stakeholder to de-escalate the situation.

**Q123: The Hybrid Governance Struggle**
In a large Hybrid project, the Waterfall engineering team is blocked because the Agile software team has not completed the "API Bridge" user story. The Agile team says the story is lower priority in their current sprint. What should the PM do?
A. Force the Agile team to change their sprint priorities immediately.
B. Facilitate a meeting between the Waterfall lead and the Product Owner to align the Product Backlog with the critical path of the Waterfall schedule.
C. Tell the Waterfall team to find a workaround.
D. Ask the Sponsor to decide which team is more important.

**Q124: The Resource Leveling Dilemma**
You are managing a project with limited human resources. After performing a Critical Path analysis, you realize two of your key resources are over-allocated for a 3-week period. You decide to delay some tasks to match their availability, even though it may extend the project duration. This technique is:
A. Resource Smoothing.
B. Resource Leveling.
C. Crashing.
D. Fast Tracking.

**Q125: The Closing Integrity**
A project is finished, the customer is happy, and the budget has a $10k surplus. The PM's manager suggests using the surplus to buy high-end laptops for the project team as a reward, rather than returning the funds to the organization. What should the PM do?
A. Follow the manager's instructions since they are your superior.
B. Respectfully decline and follow the organizational procedure for project budget closure and fund return.
C. Buy the laptops but call them "administrative archiving tools."
D. Donate the money to a charity to avoid the conflict.

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales (Q 121-125)

**121. B.** **Process (Task 2.11/2.17)**. Procurement must be closed formally if the SOW is met. The conflict of interest is an ethical/performance issue to be recorded in the final evaluation, but it doesn't necessarily void the completed work.
**122. B.** **People (Task 1.14)**. Emotional Intelligence requires self-regulation. De-escalating the environment (the break) precedes technical problem solving.
**123. B.** **Process (Task 2.1)**. Hybrid projects require "Synchronization Points." The PM facilitates alignment between the Waterfall schedule and the Agile backlog via the Product Owner.
**124. B.** **Process (Task 2.6)**. Resource Leveling adjusts start/finish dates to match resource availability, potentially changing the critical path and duration.
**125. B.** **Ethics (Honesty/Responsibility)**. Misusing project funds, even for a reward, is unethical behavior. Integrity requires following formal closure and financial reporting standards.
