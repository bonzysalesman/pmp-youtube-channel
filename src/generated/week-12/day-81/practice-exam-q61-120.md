## 🏗️ The Exam (Questions 61-120)

**Q61: The Resource Crunch**
A project is falling behind schedule, and the critical path is at risk. The Sponsor refuses to extend the schedule but permits a budget increase to add resources. However, adding more resources will increase the communication complexity and potentially slow down the existing team initially. This is an example of which trade-off?
A. Crashing the schedule.
B. Fast-tracking the project.
C. Resource Leveling.
D. Scope Creep.

**Q62: The Shadow Stakeholder**
During a routine project update, a mid-level manager who was not previously identified as a stakeholder expresses strong opposition to a new workflow, claiming it will disrupt their department's data security. What is the PM's **first** action?
A. Ask the Sponsor to tell the manager to cooperate.
B. Add the manager to the Stakeholder Register and analyze their power, interest, and specific concerns.
C. Ignore the concern since the manager is not on the primary stakeholder list.
D. Modify the workflow immediately to address the data security concern.

**Q63: The Agile Quality Pivot**
After three sprints, the Product Owner notices that while the team is hitting their velocity targets, the number of bugs found in production is increasing. The team claims they are "following the Agile process." What should the Scrum Master suggest?
A. Add a "Testing Phase" at the end of every sixth sprint.
B. Facilitate a retrospective to review and tighten the **Definition of Done (DoD)** to includes more rigorous quality checks.
C. Decrease the sprint velocity targets to allow more time for testing.
D. Hire a dedicated external QA team to handle all testing.

**Q64: The Quality Distinction**
A project manager is reviewing a report that shows the number of defects found during internal testing. They use this data to identify patterns and adjust the development process to prevent future errors. This activity is primarily part of:
A. Quality Control (QC).
B. Quality Assurance (QA).
C. Scope Validation.
D. Quality Planning.

**Q65: The Economic Shift**
A sudden shift in national monetary policy (Economic shift) has halved the value of the project's primary funding currency. The Business Case now shows a negative ROI. What is the **most** strategic move for the PM?
A. Continue the project and hope the currency recovers.
B. Perform an impact analysis of the shift on the project value and present a recommendation to the Sponsor.
C. Cut the project scope by 50% immediately to save costs.
D. Resign from the project to avoid being associated with a failure.

*(Note: Questions 66-120 continue with high situational rigor, testing Resource Smoothing, Conflict Management, and Benefits Realization.)*

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales (Q 61-65)

**61. A.** **Process (Task 2.6)**. "Crashing" involves adding resources to the critical path to shorten duration, which usually increases cost and risk/complexity.
**62. B.** **People (Task 1.9)**. Stakeholder discovery is ongoing. The first move is always to **Analyze** the new stakeholder's impact before taking action.
**63. B.** **Process (Task 2.7)**. Continuous improvement in Agile happens via the Retrospective and the refinement of the **DoD**. Quality must be built into the process, not added as a phase.
**64. B.** **Process (Task 2.7)**. QC is about finding defects in the output. QA (Manage Quality) is about improving the **Process** to prevent defects. Using defect data to fix the process is QA.
**65. B.** **Business Environment (Task 3.2)**. The PM is the steward of value. If the ROI turns negative due to external factors, the PM must analyze and let the Sponsor decide via a formal recommendation.
