## 🏗️ The Exam (Questions 1-60)

**Q1: The Agile Estimation Conflict**
During a Sprint Planning meeting, the developers and the Product Owner disagree on the story points for a critical feature. The Product Owner insists it is simple, while the team claims it involves complex legacy integration. As the Scrum Master, what is your **best** action?
A. Side with the Product Owner to ensure more features are delivered in the sprint.
B. Facilitate a discussion to uncover the technical complexities and let the team's estimate stand.
C. Average the two estimates to avoid further conflict.
D. Escalate the team's "pessimism" to the Project Sponsor.

**Q2: The Procurement Pivot**
A project uses a Fixed Price (FP) contract with a vendor. Mid-project, the vendor informs the PM that a new tariff on imported materials has increased their costs by 15%. They request a price increase to remain solvent. What should the PM do?
A. Immediately approve the increase to prevent vendor bankruptcy.
B. Review the contract terms and the Change Management Plan before discussing options with the CCB.
C. Demand the vendor fulfill the original contract regardless of their financial state.
D. Terminate the contract and find a new vendor.

**Q3: The Invisible Barrier**
A new team member from a different functional department is meeting their performance targets but seems isolated and rarely contributes to brainstorming sessions. The PM suspects the member feels like an "outsider." What is the **most** effective leadership move?
A. Publicly praise them in the next meeting to build their confidence.
B. Schedule a one-on-one session to understand their perspective and assign them a "Buddy" from the core team.
C. Tell the team they must be more inclusive or face disciplinary action.
D. Ignore the social dynamic as long as the KPIs are being met.

**Q4: The Risk Ripple**
A project to upgrade a municipal power grid discovers that a protected bird species is nesting on several key transmission towers. This was not in the Risk Register. What is the PM's **first** step?
A. Inform the local news to gain public support.
B. Update the Risk Register and perform an impact analysis on the project schedule and budget.
C. Tell the workers to proceed carefully without disturbing the birds.
D. Submit a Change Request to bypass those towers.

**Q5: The Ethics of Disclosure**
You are managing a project for a client who is also your former employer. You discover that a proprietary algorithm you developed at your former company could save the current client $50k. However, the algorithm is protected by an NDA. What is your **mandatory** action?
A. Use the algorithm but rename it to hide its origin.
B. Honor the NDA and look for a different, non-protected solution.
C. Disclose the situation to your current client and ask them for legal advice.
D. Use the algorithm since your primary loyalty is to your current project's ROI.

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales (Q 1-5)

**1. B.** **People (Task 1.3)**. In Agile, the team owns the "How" and the estimates. The Scrum Master's role is to facilitate understanding, not to dictate or compromise velocity.
**2. B.** **Process (Task 2.5/2.11)**. Contracts are formal constraints. Any change must be analyzed against the contract terms and processed through formal governance (CCB).
**3. B.** **People (Task 1.6/1.13)**. Servant leadership involves proactive support. Mentor/Buddy systems are highly effective for integrating cross-functional resources.
**4. B.** **Process (Task 2.3)**. Identification is followed by **Analysis**. You cannot act or inform until you know the severity of the impact.
**5. B.** **Ethics (Honesty/Responsibility)**. Honoring legal agreements and NDAs is a mandatory standard from the PMI Code of Ethics. Protecting intellectual property overrides project cost savings.
