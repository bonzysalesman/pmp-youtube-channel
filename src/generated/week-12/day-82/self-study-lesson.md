## 1. The Top 5 Mindset Rules (The "Automatic Pivot")
If you remember nothing else, remember these five rules:
1. **Analyze First**: Never act, inform, or escalate before you update a register or assess the impact.
2. **Servant Leader**: The PM supports, clears blockers, and empowers. We do not "order" or "dictate" unless it is a safety crisis.
3. **Value Focus**: The project exists for the Business Case. If the value is gone, be ready to pivot or terminate.
4. **Active Stakeholders**: Identify, Analyze, and Engage. We don't "manage" stakeholders; we collaborate with them.
5. **Agile Integrity**: The team owns the "How"; the Product Owner owns the "What"; the Scrum Master owns the "Process."

---

## 2. Exam Day Time Management (The 60-60-60)
The PMP is 180 questions in 230 minutes. You have two optional 10-minute breaks.
- **Section 1 (Q1-60)**: Target 75 Minutes. Take the break.
- **Section 2 (Q61-120)**: Target 75 Minutes. Take the break.
- **Section 3 (Q121-180)**: Target 80 Minutes.
- **Strategic Tip**: If you spend more than 90 seconds on a question, flag it, choose your "best guess," and move on. Don't let one hard question drain your energy for the 50 easy ones ahead.

---

## 3. The Final Ethics Check
Review the **PMI Code of Ethics Reference** one last time. Remember:
- **Responsibility**: Own your failures.
- **Respect**: Professionalism even in conflict.
- **Fairness**: Disclose every conflict of interest.
- **Honesty**: Truth always, even if it hurts the project stats.
