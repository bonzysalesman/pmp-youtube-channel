## 🔍 Step 2: Mindset Alignment Check
Review the rationales for the following high-stakes areas. Did you choose the action based on these principles?

### 1. The Servant Leader Priority (People)
- [ ] Did I choose to **Empower** the team rather than **Direct** them?
- [ ] Did I address the **Cause** of conflict rather than the **Symptom**?
- [ ] Did I use **Active Listening** before recommending a solution?

### 2. The Analytical Guardrail (Process)
- [ ] Did I **Analyze** (Risk Register/Impact Analysis) before initiating a **Change Request**?
- [ ] Did I strictly follow the **Integrated Change Control** loop?
- [ ] Did I check the **Definition of Done** or **Project Charter** before making scope decisions?

### 3. The Value Sentinel (Business Environment)
- [ ] Did I prioritize the **Business Case** over the internal project metrics?
- [ ] Did I consider **Compliance** as a non-negotiable constraint?
- [ ] Did I evaluate the **PESTLE** environment when shifts occurred?
