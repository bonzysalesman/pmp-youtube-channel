# Presentation Guide: Day 80 - The Precision Finisher

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Precision Coach
Today’s session is for the **Masters-in-Waiting**. You are no longer "teaching" new content; you are **Tuning** an engine for peak performance. Your tone should be **Encouraging**, **Demanding**, and **Hyper-Focused**. You are the corner-man for a heavyweight champion before the final round.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Don't practice until you get it right; practice until you can't get it wrong. Welcome to Day 80."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: Moving from "Knowing" to **Automatic Correctness**.

### Slide 3: EVM at a Glance
- **The Core Focus**: Removing the fear of numbers.
- **Peer Insight**: "Tell them: The math is easy; the *interpretation* is hard. CPI 0.9 isn't a number; it's a 'Budget Leak' that needs fixing."

### Slide 4: The Order of Operations
- **The Technical "Why"**: This is where 30% of Process errors occur.
- **Coaching Tip**: "Remind them: If 'Analyze' is an option, it's usually the right one. The PMP hates impulsive project managers."

### Slide 5: Conflict Resolution
- **The Strategy**: Ethical Leadership.
- **Peer Insight**: "Conflict is just un-met stakeholder needs. Solving the need solves the conflict."

### Slide 6: The Agile Self-Correction
- **The Strategy**: Servant Leadership.
- **Coaching Note**: "The PM's power in Agile comes from their ability to **Clear the Path**, not to lead the march. If the developer is blocked, you're the first one there to help."

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Change Trap"
**Objective**: Protecting project governance.
- **Action**: **Process Integrity**.
- **Resolution**: This tests the "Nice PM" trap. You can be a servant leader while still being a disciplined process manager. Allowing "tiny" changes without analysis undermines the integrity of the whole plan. The PM must be the "Sentinel" who protects the team from scope creep.

---

## 🛑 Final Polish Checklist (For the Coach)
- **Stamina Check**: "Remind them that the 180-question exam is as much about physical endurance/mental focus as it is about knowledge."
- **Ethics Review**: "Briefly mention that when in doubt, the PMI Code of Ethics (Honesty, Responsibility, Respect, Fairness) is the ultimate tie-breaker."
- **The "Feelings" check**: "Ask: 'Which part of the exam still scares you?' Address that fear directly with a concrete strategy."
