## 📊 EVM at a Glance

| Metric | What it Tells You | The "Good" Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CPI** | Cost Efficiency | > 1.0 |
| **SPI** | Schedule Efficiency | > 1.0 |
| **CV / SV** | Variance in $ / Hours | Positive (+) |
| **TCPI** | Efficiency needed to finish | < 1.0 |

**Strategic Tip**: If TCPI is high (>1.1), the project is in deep trouble.

## 🧭 The Order of Operations

1. **Information Arrives**: (Issue, Risk, Change, Feedback).
2. **Analysis**: Update Register, Assess Impact, Evaluate Options.
3. **Approval**: CCB (Waterfall) or PO (Agile).
4. **Execution**: Implement the change.
5. **Communication**: Close the loop with stakeholders.

**Rule**: Never skip Step 2.

---

## 🤝 Conflict Resolution (The Hierarchy)

1. **Problem Solve**: Face the issue and find a solution.
2. **Reconcile**: Find areas of agreement.
3. **Smooth**: Emphasize common ground (Avoidant).
4. **Withdraw**: Step away.
5. **Force**: My way or the highway.

**PMP Goal**: Be a "Problem Solver," not a "Forcer."

---

## 🚀 The Agile "Self-Correction"

- **Scrum Master**: Removes impediments; Coaches the team.
- **Product Owner**: Owns the Backlog; Negotiates Value.
- **The Team**: Owns the "How"; Estimates the work.

**Mindset**: If the team makes a mistake, the PM's first move is to **Facilitate a Retrospective**, not to assign blame.

---

## 🎯 Final Check: "The Change Trap"

**Scenario**: A stakeholder walks up to a developer and asks for a "tiny" change. The developer agrees and does the work in 10 minutes.

- **Is this okay?** NO. This is "Scope Creep" or "Gold Plating."
- **The PM's Action**: Stop the work, remind the team of the **Change Management Plan**, and ensure the request is analyzed for impact on the baseline.
