## 🏗️ The Exam (Questions 121-180)

**Q121: The Sunk Cost Trap**
A project is 90% complete and has spent $1.2M. A new technology has entered the market that would have made the project unnecessary. The remaining work will cost $200k. The total projected benefit is now estimated at $150k due to the market shift. What should the PM do?
A. Finish the project since $1.2M has already been spent.
B. Recommend termination to the Sponsor based on the updated ROI analysis.
C. Ask the team to work for free to make the project profitable.
D. Use the remaining $200k to try and integrate the new technology.

**Q122: The Ethical Dilemma**
During a vendor selection process, a project manager is offered a paid weekend retreat at a luxury resort by one of the bidders. The bidder claims it is a "standard industry marketing event." What is the PM's **first** action according to the PMI Code of Ethics?
A. Accept the offer but ensure the decision remains objective.
B. Decline the offer and report the incident to the appropriate organizational authority.
C. Accept the offer if all other bidders are also invited.
D. Decline the offer and immediately disqualify the bidder.

**Q123: The Scope Creep Shadow**
In a Hybrid project, the Waterfall schedule for the infrastructure is solid, but the Agile team developing the software keeps adding "Small improvements" to the backlog that are not in the original scope. The Project Manager notices the budget is depleting faster than planned. What should the PM do?
A. Tell the Agile team to stop all work and fix the schedule.
B. Review the Definition of Done (DoD) and ensure all new stories are justified by the Business Case and approved by the Product Owner.
C. Demand a budget increase from the Sponsor to cover the improvements.
D. Ignore it, as "Agile embraces change."

**Q124: The Global Gap**
A co-located team is being transitioned to a global virtual model. Team members in the new region have a high "Power Distance" culture and are hesitant to speak up about issues in meetings with the PM. What is the **most** effective response?
A. Tell them that they must speak up or be removed from the team.
B. Use anonymous surveys or one-on-one "safe space" sessions to gather feedback until trust is established.
C. Assume there are no issues since no one is complaining.
D. Replace the new team members with people from the original region.

**Q125: The Formal Finale**
A project has met all technical requirements and the customer has signed off on the deliverables. The team is eager to start their next assignment. What is a **mandatory** step before the PM releases the team?
A. Host a celebration party.
B. Archive project records and document Lessons Learned.
C. Update the PM's resume with the project success.
D. Ask the customer for a personal referral.

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales (Q 121-185)

**121. B.** **Business Environment (Task 3.2)**. Sunk costs are irrelevant. If the *remaining* cost ($200k) is greater than the *remaining* benefit ($150k), the project should be terminated to protect organizational resources.
**122. B.** **Ethics (Responsibility/Fairness)**. PMI Code of Ethics requires avoiding even the *appearance* of a conflict of interest. Reporting maintains organizational integrity.
**123. B.** **Process (Task 2.5/2.1)**. Hybrid projects still require governance. The PM must ensure the Product Owner is managing the backlog against the Business Case to prevent unapproved "Gold Plating."
**124. B.** **People (Task 1.11)**. A leader adapts to the team's cultural context. Using tools that lower the barrier to communication is more effective than demanding a sudden cultural shift.
**125. B.** **Process (Task 2.17)**. Closing a project involves formal administrative steps. Archiving records and capturing lessons learned are critical for organizational knowledge.
