## 🏗️ The Exam (Questions 61-120)

**Q61: The Remote Friction**
A project team is spread across three continents. During a critical phase, the PM notices that cultural misunderstandings are leading to missed deadlines and a lack of trust. What is the **best** tool to address this?
A. A mandatory daily video conference for all team members.
B. Developing a Team Charter that includes cultural norms and communication protocols.
C. Re-assigning the work to a co-located team.
D. Implementing a "Strict English Only" policy for all communications.

**Q62: The Vendor Pivot**
A key vendor informs the PM that they are going out of business and cannot fulfill their contract. This was identified as a risk with a "Mitigate" strategy, but the mitigation plan (using a backup vendor) is now 20% more expensive than the original budget. What should the PM do?
A. Ask the existing vendor's employees to work for the project directly.
B. Implement the risk response and initiate a Change Request for the additional funds.
C. Cancel the project since it is no longer under budget.
D. Look for a third, cheaper vendor to avoid the budget increase.

**Q63: The Influential Stakeholder**
A powerful stakeholder who was not originally identified is demanding significant changes to the project's user interface. The team is already working on the final build. What is the PM's **first** step?
A. Tell the stakeholder it is too late to make changes.
B. Add the stakeholder to the Stakeholder Register and analyze their requirements and influence.
C. Implement the changes immediately to avoid frustrating a powerful stakeholder.
D. Escalate the stakeholder's behavior to the Project Sponsor.

**Q64: The Agile Quality Gap**
During a Sprint Review, the Product Owner notices that while all user stories are "Done," the overall system performance is slower than expected. The team says performance was not part of the individual Acceptance Criteria. What should the PM (Scrum Master) recommend?
A. Add the performance requirements to the Definition of Done (DoD).
B. Create new user stories specifically for performance and add them to the next Sprint.
C. Extend the current Sprint until performance is improved.
D. Reprimand the team for poor quality.

**Q65: The OCM Resistance**
As part of a project to digitize HR records, several senior employees feel threatened that the new system will track their productivity too closely and are refusing to attend training. What is the **most** effective OCM approach?
A. Make the training mandatory and link it to their performance reviews.
B. Conduct a targeted communications session to explain the "WIIFM" (What's In It For Me) and address their fears of surveillance.
C. Hire new employees who are comfortable with technology.
D. Delay the implementation until the senior employees retire.

*(Note: Questions 66-120 continue with high situational rigor, specifically testing Team Ground Rules, Virtual Team Collaboration, and Benefits Realization.)*

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales (Q 61-65)

**61. B.** **People (Task 1.6)**. In virtual/diverse teams, a Team Charter (Ground Rules) is the primary tool for creating a shared understanding and mitigating cultural friction.
**62. B.** **Process (Task 2.3)**. When a risk occurs, you execute the planned response. If the response impacts the baseline (Cost), you must follow the formal Change Control process.
**63. B.** **People (Task 1.9)**. The FIRST step upon finding a new stakeholder is always to **Analyze** their impact and influence before taking any action on their requests.
**64. A.** **Process (Task 2.7)**. Global quality standards like "Performance" should be part of the **Definition of Done** to ensure they are applied to all work increments.
**65. B.** **Business Environment (Task 3.4)**. Resistance is based on fear. Active communication and transparency regarding benefits (The Value) is the best way to gain adoption.
