## 🏗️ The Exam (Questions 1-60)

**Q1: The Agile Kickoff**
A project manager is starting a new Agile project with a team that has only worked in Waterfall environments. During the project kickoff, the team expresses concern about the lack of a detailed, fixed-price project schedule. What is the **best** response?
A. Create a detailed Gantt chart for the first three months to reassure them.
B. Explain that Agile uses iterative planning and the Product Backlog to manage work-flow and value.
C. Ask the Project Sponsor to mandate Agile training for the entire team.
D. Switch the project to a Hybrid model to provide the comfort of a fixed schedule.

**Q2: The Obstructed Resource**
A critical team member is constantly being pulled away by their functional manager to attend to departmental "emergencies." This is delaying a key milestone. What should the Project Manager do?
A. Complain to the Project Sponsor about the functional manager's lack of cooperation.
B. Meet with the functional manager to discuss resource availability and negotiate a formal service-level agreement for the team member.
C. Work overtime yourself to cover the team member's missing hours.
D. Discipline the team member for prioritizing departmental work over the project.

**Q3: The Budget Blowout**
During the execution phase, a project's CPI is 0.85 and SPI is 0.90. A major stakeholder demands a new, high-priority feature be added to the scope. What is the **most** appropriate action?
A. Refuse the request due to the poor performance metrics.
B. Accept the request and ask for a budget increase.
C. Analyze the impact of the new feature on the cost and schedule baselines and present the findings to the Change Control Board (CCB).
D. Cut several lower-priority features to make room for the new request.

**Q4: The Latent Risk**
A team member discovers a technical vulnerability that could potentially expose user data. The probability of exploitation is low, but the impact would be catastrophic. The risk was not in the original Risk Register. What is the PM's **first** step?
A. Inform the Project Sponsor immediately.
B. Update the Risk Register and analyze the impact/probability.
C. Tell the developer to fix it immediately, regardless of the schedule impact.
D. Ignore the risk since the probability is low.

**Q5: The Value Mismatch**
A project intended to improve customer satisfaction is on track and within budget. However, a recent market survey shows that the technology being used is no longer favored by consumers. What should the PM do?
A. Continue as planned since the project is meeting its performance targets.
B. Analyze the impact on the Business Case and present the results to the Project Sponsor.
C. Change the technology immediately without informing the stakeholders.
D. Terminate the project and start a new one with the favored technology.

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales (Q 1-5)

**1. B.** **Process (Agile)**. Agile planning is iterative. The PM must educate the team on the mindset shift rather than falling back to Waterfall artifacts.
**2. B.** **People (Task 1.7)**. The PM must use negotiation and organizational influence to clear impediments and secure resource commitments.
**3. C.** **Process (Task 2.5)**. All scope changes, especially during poor performance, must go through the formal Integrated Change Control process.
**4. B.** **Process (Task 2.3)**. The FIRST step upon discovering a new risk is always to **Analyze** (which includes updating the register and assessing impact).
**5. B.** **Business Environment (Task 3.2)**. The PM's ultimate responsibility is value. If value is compromised by external factors, the Business Case must be re-evaluated.
