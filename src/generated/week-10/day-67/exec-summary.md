# Executive Summary: Day 67 - The People Domain Strategist

## 🎯 Strategic Objective
In the **PMP Mastery Framework**, Day 67 serves as the **Synthesis Point** for Work Group 4: Keeping Team Track. As a strategic peer, you have now mastered the art of sustaining a high-performing environment. Today, we consolidate the twin pillars of this work group: **Operational Support** (Impediments, Conflict, Performance) and **Soft-Power Influence** (Collaboration, Mentoring, EQ). We are ensuring your People domain mindset is integrated, empathetic, and resilient.

---

## 🔑 Work Group 4 Holistic Pillars

### 1. Operational Sustainment (Part 1 Recap)
- **Leadership Adaptation**: Shifting styles (Directive to Delegating) based on team maturity.
- **Performance Optimization**: Using KPIs and constructive feedback to tune the team's engine.
- **Friction Eradication**: Proactively identifying and removing impediments to maintain velocity.

### 2. Relational Sustainment (Part 2 Recap)
- **Active Collaboration**: Moving from "Engagement" to shared ownership and trust with stakeholders.
- **Mentorship & Wisdom**: Investing in long-term professional growth and tacit knowledge transfer.
- **Emotional Mastery**: Applying EQ (Social Awareness & Relationship Management) to navigate high-stakes interpersonal dynamics.
