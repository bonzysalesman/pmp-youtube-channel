# Self-Study Lesson: Day 67 - The Integrated Leader

Welcome to Day 67. You have reached the summit of the **People Domain**.

To pass the PMP, you must be able to solve "People Problems" with the same rigor you apply to "Schedule Problems." Work Group 4 has taught you that leadership isn't just about being a "nice boss"—it's about being a **Servant Architect** who designs the environment for success. Today, we review the integrated flow of these tasks.

- **Remove Impediments (1.7)**: Shield the team. Clear the path.

## 2. The Relationship Ecosystem (Relational)
Recapping the "Softer" side of Soft Skills:
- **Collaborate with Stakeholders (1.9)**: Build trust. Foster shared ownership.
- **Mentor Stakeholders (1.13)**: Transfer tacit knowledge. Foster growth.
- **Emotional Intelligence (1.14)**: Self-regulate. Read the room. Influence ethically.

---

## 3. The "Strategic Peer" Decision Matrix
When faced with a People domain question, use this hierarchy:
1. **Analyze (EQ)**: What is the emotion? What is the root cause?
2. **Engage (Collaboration)**: Have a private conversation. Verify understanding.
3. **Serve (Servant Leadership)**: What can *I* remove or provide to help this person?
4. **Govern (Professionalism)**: Use the Team Charter or Project Management Plan as the objective standard.

---

## 🎯 PMP Mindset Tip: "The Domain Bridge"
Many questions bridge the People and Process domains. For example: A **Change Request (Process)** causes **Conflict (People)**. In these cases, you must satisfy the **Process** (Impact analysis) while using **EQ** (Empathy) to manage the people's reaction.
