# WG 4 Review Assessment: The Integrated Leader

## 📝 Performance Objectives
This assessment verifies your ability to integrate the diverse tasks of Work Group 4. A Strategic Peer must be able to move seamlessly from removing technical impediments to mentoring stakeholders through high-stakes emotional triggers.

---

## 🏗️ Assessment Questions

**Q1: The Hybrid Blocker**
You are managing a hybrid project. The development team (Agile) is blocked because the Architecture Board (Predictive/Governance) only meets once a month and hasn't approved the API design. As a Servant Leader, what do you do?
A. Tell the team to follow the official governance schedule and wait for the meeting.
B. Negotiate a special session or an asynchronous approval process with the Architecture Board lead to clear the impediment.
C. Skip the approval and tell the team to start coding to maintain velocity.
D. Escalate the "slow" behavior of the Architecture Board to the Project Sponsor immediately.

**Q2: The Performance Divergence**
KPIs for the current iteration show that the team's velocity has increased, but the quality of the deliverables has dropped. After a private discussion with the team, you find that a recent conflict over priorities has led them to "cut corners." What is your **next** step?
A. Implement a stricter quality audit process for every deliverable.
B. Facilitate a collaborative session to re-align the team on the definition of "Success" and resolve the underlying priority conflict.
C. Force the team to redo the low-quality work on their own time.
D. Re-baseline the schedule to give the team more time to meet the quality standards.

**Q3: The Tacit Gap**
A key technical stakeholder is retiring in three months. They have 20 years of experience with the legacy system the project is replacing. What is the most effective way to protect the project's intellectual capital?
A. Ask the stakeholder to write a 100-page "Operations Manual" (Explicit Knowledge).
B. Schedule regular mentoring sessions between the senior stakeholder and a junior developer to transfer experience (Tacit Knowledge).
C. Record all their meetings and save them in the project's knowledge repository.
D. Hire a consultant to interview them during their final week.

**Q4: The EQ Outreach**
A powerful stakeholder who was previously supportive has become critical and obstructive after a change in their department's budget. What is your **first** move using Emotional Intelligence?
A. Ignore the behavior and stick to the communication plan.
B. Use Social Awareness to empathize with their new departmental pressures and have a private conversation to find common ground.
C. Publicly challenge their "negativity" in the steering committee meeting.
D. Offer them a larger role in the project to "buy" their support.

**Q5: The Feedback Audit**
You mentored a team member last month on their negotiation skills. In a recent meeting, you noticed they used the "Collaborate" technique effectively to resolve a resource dispute. To close the sustainment loop, you should:
A. Do nothing; the mentoring worked.
B. Publicly praise them for using the specific technique and document the performance improvement in their development record.
C. Tell them they still have more to learn.
D. Update the Risk Register to reflect the lower team conflict.

---

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales

**1. B.** **Impediment Removal (Influence)**. A PM uses their influence to navigate organizational siloes and bridge methodology gaps.
**2. B.** **Conflict/Performance Integration**. You must solve the *root cause* (the priority conflict) rather than just treating the *symptom* (low quality).
**3. B.** **Mentorship (Tacit Transfer)**. Holistic experience cannot be captured in manuals. 1-on-1 mentoring is the gold standard for tacit transfer.
**4. B.** **Social Awareness (EQ)**. Understanding the "Why" behind the "How" of stakeholder behavior is the foundation of relationship management.
**5. B.** **Verification & Recognition**. Closing the loop involves recognizing the improvement and reinforcing the behavior (Enabler 1.3.4).
