# Presentation Guide: Day 67 - The Integrated Leader

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Master Integrators**. You are teaching your peers how to stop seeing "people tasks" as separate chores and start seeing them as a unified ecosystem of leadership. Your tone should be **Cultivated**, **Visionary**, and **Disciplined**. You are the final guide for the People domain.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Eighty percent of the PMP is about People. Twenty percent is about Process. One hundred percent of your success depends on how you integrate them. Welcome to Day 67."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: This is about **Synthesis**. The 'Why' behind the 'How' of team sustainment.

### Slide 3: The Sustainment Engine
- **The Core Focus**: Operational Discipline.
- **Peer Insight**: "Ask them: 'Is a PM who doesn't remove impediments really leading?' Force them to see 'Servant Leadership' as a performance requirement, not just a buzzword."

### Slide 4: The Influence Ecosystem
- **The Technical "Why"**: The "Soft" side of the People domain.
- **Coaching Tip**: "Remind them that Mentorship and Collaboration are how you scale your impact as a PM. You can't be everywhere, but a mentored team can."

### Slide 5: The Strategic Peer Mindset
- **The Strategy**: The Decision Matrix.
- **Peer Insight**: "The PMP exam doesn't just ask what a tool is; it asks *what you do next*. This slide gives them the order of operations for situational questions."

### Slide 6: Closing the Loop
- **The Strategy**: Rigor in People tasks.
- **Coaching Note**: "Verification isn't just for software; it's for people. If you give feedback, monitor the results. If you remove a blocker, verify it's gone."

---

## 🎯 Final People Domain Coaching
**Objective**: Transitioning to the test mindset.
- **Advice**: "Tell your peers to trust their **Servant Leader** instincts. On the exam, if an answer feels 'mean,' 'authoritarian,' or 'avoidant,' it's probably wrong. If it feels 'collaborative,' 'analytical,' and 'supportive,' it's likely the right path."

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **People Fatigue**: Acknowledge that the 'people stuff' can be exhausting. Remind them: "Your technical plan is only as good as the team that executes it."
- **Over-Simplification**: Warn them not to treat EQ or Mentorship as "easy." These require more discipline than a Gantt chart because people are more complex than tasks.
- **Losing the Vision**: Remind them that every leadership action must ultimately serve the project and business value.
