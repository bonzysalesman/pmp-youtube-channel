# Day 65: The Sage of the System
### Sustaining Intelligence through Mentorship (ECO 1.13)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "A mentor is someone who allows you to see the hope inside yourself."

- **Objective 1**: Distinguish **Mentoring** from **Coaching**.
- **Objective 2**: Master the **OSCAR** model for growth.
- **Objective 3**: Capture and transfer **Tacit Knowledge**.

---

## ⚖️ Coaching vs. Mentoring (ECO 1.13)

| Feature | Coaching | Mentoring |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Performance / Task | Development / Career |
| **Goal** | Immediate Skill | Long-term Wisdom |
| **Duration** | Short-term | Long-term |
| **Source** | Explicit Knowledge | Tacit Knowledge |

---

## 🏛️ The OSCAR Framework (ECO 1.13.1)

- **O**utcome: Clear goals.
- **S**ituation: Reality check.
- **C**hoices: Options & obstacles.
- **A**ctions: Commitment to steps.
- **R**eview: Measuring the shift.

**Strategic Action**: Schedule time to serve. Mentorship is an investment, not an accident.

---

## 🧠 Transferring Tacit Knowledge

- **Explicit**: Written in the plans $(E=MC^2)$.
- **Tacit**: Learned through experience (How to negotiate).

**The PM as a Conduit**: Spotting moments to share the "Unwritten Rules" of project success.

---

## 🔍 Teachable Moments (ECO 1.13.2)

- **The Mistake**: "Why did we miss that risk?"
- **The Success**: "What enabled that collaboration?"
- **The Struggle**: "What is the blocker beneath the blocker?"

**PMP Mindset**: Mistakes are not failures; they are the most effective mentoring tools.

---

## 🎯 Activity: The Aspirational Leader

**Scenario**: A junior PM on your team is technically brilliant but struggles to manage senior stakeholder expectations. They aren't "failing" a task, but they aren't growing their influence.

1. Do you **Coach** them on a specific report?
2. Do you **Mentor** them on the "Art of Influence"?
3. What model do you use to start the session? (Answer: OSCAR).
