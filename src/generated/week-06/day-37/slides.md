# Day 37: The Velocity of Value
### Urgency & Incremental Delivery (ECO 2.1)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "Done is better than perfect, and early is better than late."

- **Objective 1**: Assess opportunities for Incremental Value.
- **Objective 2**: Examine Business Value throughout the project lifecycle.
- **Objective 3**: Support the team in architecting the Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

---

## 🚀 Why "Urgency"? (ECO 2.1.1)

### Moving Beyond the Completion Date
- **Traditional**: Value = End of Project.
- **Modern**: Value = Continuous Stream.

**Goal**: Minimize the "Time-to-Value." Every day a deliverable sits on a shelf is a lost day of ROI.

---

## 🏗️ Identifying Incremental Opportunities

- **Decomposition**: Breaking "Monoliths" into "Modules."
- **Prioritization**: Value vs. Risk vs. Dependency.
- **Feedback Loops**: Short cycles = early validation.

**Strategic Key**: If it's usable, deliver it. If it's not usable, make it usable.

---

## 📦 The MVP: Minimum Viable Product (ECO 2.1.3)

### The Strategic Core
- **Definition**: The smallest set of features used for validation and learning.
- **The Goal**: Build -> Measure -> Learn.
- **The PM Role**: Supporting the team to find the "Must-Haves."

---

## ⚖️ Examining Business Value (ECO 2.1.2)

### The Continuous Audit
- **Business Case**: Is the "Why" still valid?
- **KPIs**: Measuring ROI, NPV, and Benefit-Cost Ratio.
- **Adaptability**: If the value vanishes, the project must either pivot or stop.

---

## 🛠️ Leading for Value

- **MoSCoW Prioritization**: Must, Should, Could, Won't.
- **Backlog Refinement**: Keeping the team focused on the "High-Value" items.
- **Timeboxing / Spikes**: Using urgency to solve complex value blockers.

---

## 🎯 Activity: The Value Pivot

**Scenario**: You are 40% through a project to build a new CRM. A major competitor just released a free version of the feature you were planning to charge for.

1. What is your **First Strategic Response**?
2. How do you support the team in redefining the **MVP**?
