# Presentation Guide: Day 37 - Execute for Value

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Value Drivers**. You are teaching your peers how to stop "Counting Tasks" and start "Measuring Impact." Your tone should be **Urgent**, **Commercial**, and **Empowering**. You are coaching them to recognize that speed without value is just activity, but value with speed is **Strategic Advantage**.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "The business cares about one thing: Value. They don't care about your Gantt chart; they care about when they can start solving their problems. Welcome to Day 37."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: This isn't about working harder; it's about working **First** on what matters **Most**.

### Slide 3: Why Urgency?
- **The Core Focus**: "Time-to-Value." 
- **Peer Insight**: "In a changing market, the project plan you wrote six months ago is probably partially obsolete. Urgency allows you to ship while the requirements are still relevant."

### Slide 4: Incremental Opportunities
- **The "So What?"**: Delivery is the best way to manage risk. 
- **Coaching Tip**: "If you're stuck in Waterfall thinking, ask: 'What is the smallest part of this that a customer would actually pay for?'"

### Slide 5: The MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
- **The Technical "Why"**: The exam wants to see the PM *supporting* the team, not *dictating* the MVP. 
- **The Hook**: "MVP is a learning tool. If you aren't using it to gather data and Pivot, you're just delivering a small project."

### Slide 6: Examining Business Value
- **The Strategy**: "No value, no project." 
- **Peer Insight**: "Be the PM who is brave enough to suggest stopping a project that no longer makes sense. That is true leadership."

### Slide 7: Leading for Value
- **The Strategy**: Tools like MoSCoW and Backlog Refinement.
- **Coaching Note**: Emphasize that the PM protects the team's focus on value.

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Value Pivot"
**Objective**: Strategic adaptability.
- **Response**: **Examine the Business Value**. You must immediately re-analyze the Business Case. If the "Free" version makes your product worthless, the ROI has collapsed.
- **MVP Support**: Facilitate a **Reprioritization** session. Help the team identify a NEW "Must-Have" that the competitor doesn't have. If there isn't one, recommend termination to the Sponsor.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **MVP as "Phase 1"**: No. Phase 1 is a delivery plan; MVP is an **experimental/learning** plan.
- **Urgency as "Working Overtime"**: Clarify this immediately. Urgency means **stripping away waste** and focusing on the core. It's about flow, not burnout.
- **Value is "Just Money"**: Correct this. Value can be regulatory compliance, customer satisfaction, or knowledge acquisition (e.g., a "Spike").
