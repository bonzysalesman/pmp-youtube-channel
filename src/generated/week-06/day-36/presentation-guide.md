# Presentation Guide: Day 36 - Assess & Manage Risks

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Navigators**. You are teaching your peers how to read the "Radar" of the project. Your tone should be **Alert**, **Skeptical (but not cynical)**, and **Decisive**. You are coaching them to recognize that a PM who isn't managing risk is just waiting for the project to fail.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "There are two types of Project Managers: those who manage risk, and those who manage disasters. Welcome to Day 36."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: Emphasize that risk management is **Proactive**. If you are reacting to a problem that already happened, you are managing an **Issue**, not a **Risk**.

### Slide 3: The Risk Framework
- **The Core Focus**: "Appetite vs. Threshold." 
- **Peer Insight**: "Don't guess how much risk your sponsor wants to take. Ask. Determine the boundary where a risk becomes a crisis."

### Slide 4: Identifying Shadows
- **The "So What?"**: Identification must involve the whole team. 
- **Coaching Tip**: "The person closest to the work usually knows the risk first. Build a culture where identifying a risk is rewarded, not punished."

### Slide 5: The Analysis Lifecycle
- **The Distinction**: 
- *Qualitative*: Quick, prioritization-focused.
- *Quantitative*: Accurate, decision-focused.
- **Exam Hook**: Remember that **Monte Carlo** and **Tornado Diagrams** are Quantitative.

### Slide 6: Response Strategies (Threats)
- **The Technical "Why"**: The exam will give scenarios and ask which strategy is being used. 
- **The Key**: 
  - *Transfer* = Vendor/Insurance/Contracts.
  - *Avoid* = Changing the scope/requirements.
  - *Mitigate* = Training/Testing (Action before the event).

### Slide 7: Response Strategies (Opportunities)
- **The "Strategic Pivot"**: Most PMs forget opportunities. 
- **Hook**: "Exploiting an opportunity is just as valuable as avoiding a threat. Don't leave project value on the table."

### Slide 8: Managing the Lifecycle
- **The Workflow**: Update Register -> Communicate in Report -> Implement when Triggered.

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Trigger Crisis"
**Objective**: Moving from planning to execution logic.
- **Step**: **Identify the Issue**. Since the trigger fired, the Risk has now become an **Issue**. You must update the **Issue Log** and implement the pre-planned response.
- **Reserve**: **Contingency Reserve**. These funds were set aside for "Known-Unknowns" (the delay we identified). **Management Reserve** is for "Unknown-Unknowns."

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **Risk vs. Issue**: A risk is a future possibility. An issue is a current reality.
- **Contingency vs. Workaround**: A contingency is a **planned** response. A workaround is an **unplanned** (emergency) response to an issue that wasn't identified as a risk.
- **"The Risk is Closed"**: Remind them to retire risks. If the shipping confirmation arrives, the risk is gone—close it in the register to clear the team's cognitive load.
