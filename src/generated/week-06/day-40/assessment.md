# Work Group 3: Process Domain (Part 3) - Review Assessment

## 📝 Strategic Context
This assessment focuses on the transition into the **Execution Phase** (Doing the Work). As a Strategic Peer, your goal is to analyze these scenarios through the lens of a **Proactive Leader** who prioritizes Risk Stewardship, Business Value, and High-Integrity Engagement.

---

## ❓ The Assessment

### Question 1: The Risk Transition
A Project Manager has been tracking a risk that a third-party API would be unavailable for the integration phase. Today, the vendor confirms the API will not be ready for another 30 days.
**What is the Project Manager's first strategic action?**
A. Implement the pre-planned contingency response and update the Risk Register.
B. Analyze the impact of the delay and update the Issue Log.
C. Escalate the situation to the Sponsor and request a budget increase for a new vendor.
D. Conduct a Root Cause Analysis to determine why the vendor failed.

---

### Question 2: The Opportunity Exploit
During execution, a team member discovers a new automation tool that could reduce the testing phase by 15% without increasing costs. This was not identified in the initial risk planning.
**What should the Project Manager do next?**
A. Direct the team to begin using the tool immediately to capture the value.
B. Document the opportunity in the Risk Register and perform a Qualitative Analysis.
C. Submit a Change Request to the CCB to update the testing methodology.
D. Share the results with other Project Managers in the organization.

---

### Question 3: The Value Pivot
A project is building a new mobile app. The original Business Case was based on a subscription model. Halfway through execution, market data shows that users now significantly prefer a "Freemium" model with in-app purchases.
**What is the Project Manager's most professional course of action?**
A. Continue with the original plan to ensure the project remains "on time and on budget."
B. Redefine the MVP features and update the project schedule to include the new model.
C. Perform a جدید (new) Business Value assessment and present the findings to the Sponsor.
D. Instruct the team to add the in-app purchase features as "Gold Plating" to satisfy stakeholders.

---

### Question 4: The Push-Back
A senior stakeholder complains that they are being "spammed" by daily automated status reports that are too technical for their needs. They are starting to ignore all project communications.
**How should the Project Manager resolve this using the Communications Management Plan?**
A. Increase the frequency of reports to ensure they don't miss any critical updates.
B. Meet with the stakeholder to re-analyze their information needs and adjust the level of detail.
C. Switch the communication method from "Push" to "Interactive" by calling them daily.
D. Remove the stakeholder from the distribution list to reduce their information overload.

---

### Question 5: The Verification Gap
The Project Manager sends out a critical update regarding a shift in project direction. Two days later, a key team lead makes a decision based on the *old* direction, causing a 3-day rework. 
**Which part of the communication model failed?**
A. The Encoder (The PM failed to write clearly).
B. The Medium (The email was not a suitable channel).
C. The Feedback Loop (The PM failed to confirm understanding).
D. The Noise (External factors interrupted the message).

---

### Question 6: The Resistant SME
A Subject Matter Expert (SME) whose sign-off is required for the final delivery is consistently late to meetings and provides minimal input. They have recently become vocal about their dislike for the project's methodology.
**What is the best way to move this stakeholder from "Resistant" to "Supportive"?**
A. Use positional power to force their attendance through their functional manager.
B. Update the Stakeholder Engagement Plan to ignore their input and move forward.
C. Engage them in a 1:1 collaborative session to understand their concerns and incorporate their expertise.
D. Escalate to the Project Sponsor to have the SME replaced.

---

### Question 7: The Transition to Collaboration
You are managing a predictive (Waterfall) project. You notice that the team is working in silos and stakeholders only see results at major milestones. You want to move toward a more collaborative engagement model.
**Which technique should you implement FIRST?**
A. Switch the entire project methodology to Scrum.
B. Implement meaningful "Demo" sessions at the end of each phase to gather early feedback.
C. Mandatory daily stand-ups for all stakeholders.
D. Create an intranet site for Pull communication.

---

### Question 8: The MVP Stewardship
The team is struggling to define the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for a new internal portal. Some team members want to include every feature from the legacy system to satisfy all users.
**What is the PM's role in this situation?**
A. Dictate which features are in the MVP based on the Project Charter.
B. Support the team in using prioritization techniques like MoSCoW to find the core value.
C. Ask the Sponsor to decide on the feature set to avoid team conflict.
D. Build all features requested to ensure maximum stakeholder satisfaction.

---

### Question 9: The Indirect Feedback
During a team meeting, you notice that the Lead Developer is unusually quiet and avoids eye contact when the project's new timeline is discussed.
**What is your next strategic move as a Project Manager?**
A. Ignore the behavior as long as the work is being delivered on time.
B. Confront them in front of the group about their lack of engagement.
C. Seek a private interaction to listen for underlying risks or concerns they may be hesitant to share.
D. Update the Risk Register to note that "Team Morale is dropping."

---

### Question 10: Ethical Engagement
A vendor offers you a free pass to an expensive industry conference while you are in the middle of evaluating their bid for a project procurement. 
**According to the PMI Code of Ethics, how should you respond?**
A. Accept the pass but don't let it influence your decision.
B. Accept the pass and disclose it to your manager.
C. Refuse the pass to avoid even the appearance of a conflict of interest.
D. Accept the pass only if the other bidders are also given one.

---

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Rationales

### 1. Answer: B
**Rationale**: The risk has occurred; it is now an **Issue**. The first step is to document it in the **Issue Log** and analyze its impact before implementing responses. Update the Risk Register to reflect that it is no longer an active risk.

### 2. Answer: B
**Rationale**: This is an **Opportunity** (Positive Risk). Following the "Assess before Action" mindset, you must first document it and analyze its probability and impact before capturing the value.

### 3. Answer: C
**Rationale**: As a PM, your duty is to the Business Case. If market conditions change the value proposition, you must perform a new assessment and escalate to the Sponsor for a strategic decision (Pivot/Continue/Terminate).

### 4. Answer: B
**Rationale**: The stakeholder is experiencing information overload. The best response is to re-analyze their needs (Requirements Analysis) and tailor the frequency and detail, rather than just changing the method or frequency arbitrarily.

### 5. Answer: C
**Rationale**: Successful communication requires **Verification**. The PM sent the info but failed to ensure it was received and decoded correctly.

### 6. Answer: C
**Rationale**: Collaborative leadership (Personal Power) is always preferred over Positional Power. A 1:1 meeting allows for active listening and root cause identification of the resistance.

### 7. Answer: B
**Rationale**: Bringing stakeholders into the feedback loop earlier (through Demos or Prototypes) is the most effective way to transition from "Reporting" to "Collaborating" without disrupting the entire methodology.

### 8. Answer: B
**Rationale**: The PM's role in MVP definition is **Supportive**. You facilitate the team's decision-making process through prioritization frameworks.

### 9. Answer: C
**Rationale**: "Silent" feedback is a critical data point. A private interaction demonstrates empathy and allows for "Indirect" risk identification.

### 10. Answer: C
**Rationale**: To maintain the Integrity of the procurement process, you must avoid even the **appearance** of a conflict of interest or a breach of fairness.
