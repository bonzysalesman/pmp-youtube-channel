# Day 31: The Integrative Master Plan
### Holistic Governance & Baselines (ECO 2.9)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "The whole is greater than the sum of its parts."

- **Objective 1**: Unify subsidiary plans into a cohesive Project Management Plan (PMP).
- **Objective 2**: Establish the Performance Measurement Baseline (PMB).
- **Objective 3**: Leverage Progressive Elaboration to manage project uncertainty.

---

## 📐 The Architecture of Integration (ECO 2.9.1)

### The Subsidiary Components
- **Managerial Plans**: How we manage (Risk, Comm, Stakeholder, etc.).
- **Operational Baselines**: What we protect (Scope, Schedule, Cost).
- **Life Cycle Description**: The project's developmental DNA.

---

## 🏗️ Building the PMP

1. **Gather Inputs**: Charter, Subsidiary Plans, Baselines.
2. **Identify Interdependencies**: Finding the "Ripple Effect."
3. **Establish a Consistent Framework**: Unified terminology and reporting.
4. **Communicate**: Ensuring buy-in from the Steering Committee.

---

## 🌊 Progressive Elaboration

### Planning in Waves
- **Detail for the Now**: Near-term work is high-fidelity.
- **High-level for the Future**: Long-term work remains fluid.
- **The Value**: Prevents "Planning Paralysis" and adapts to emergent data.

---

## 🔍 Assessing the Plan (ECO 2.9.2)

### The Integrity Audit
- **Gap Analysis**: Finding inconsistencies between the Schedule and the Budget.
- **Dependency Analysis**: Ensuring cross-functional alignment.
- **Value Verification**: Does this plan still achieve the business goals set in the Charter?

---

## 📊 Data-Driven Governance (ECO 2.9.3 - 2.9.5)

### Critical Information Requirements
- **Executive Vitals**: KPIs, Critical Path Status, TOP Riscs.
- **Decision Support**: Using Trend and Variance analysis to justify pivots.
- **Governance**: Reporting exactly what is needed for decision-making—nothing more, nothing less.

---

## 🏆 The PMP Performance Baseline

- **The Scope Baseline** (WBS).
- **The Schedule Baseline** (Network Diagram).
- **The Cost Baseline** (Time-phased Budget).
- **Integration**: The unified standard for project success.

---

## 🎯 Activity: The Conflicting Plan

**Scenario**: You are integrating plans and discover the **Quality Management Plan** requires a 10-day testing phase, but the **Schedule Baseline** only has 4 days allocated.

1. As a Strategic Peer, what is your first action?
2. Which project document do you update to resolve this *before* seeking final PMP approval?
