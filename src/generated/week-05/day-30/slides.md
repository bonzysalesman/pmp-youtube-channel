# Day 30: Governing Project Integrity
### Quality Stewardship & Prevention (ECO 2.7)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "Quality is not an act; it is a habit."

- **Objective 1**: Define measurable quality standards based on requirements.
- **Objective 2**: Master the "Prevention over Inspection" mindset.
- **Objective 3**: Leverage Root Cause Analysis (RCA) to bridge quality gaps.

---

## 📐 Defining the Standards (ECO 2.7.1)

### Quality as Conformance
- **Vague**: "The product must be user-friendly."
- **Strategic**: "Task completion in < 3 clicks; Error rate < 2%."

### The Stewardship Checklist
1. Identify Requirements.
2. Translate to Measurable Standards.
3. Document Metrics in the **Quality Management Plan**.

---

## 💰 The Economics of Quality (COQ)

### Conformance (Prevention)
- Training, Audits, Process Design.
- *ROI: High.*

### Non-Conformance (Failure)
- Rework, Scrapping, Warranty, Brand Damage.
- *ROI: Negative.*

**PM Mindset**: Spending $1 on Prevention saves $10 in Rework.

---

## 🔍 Quality Assurance vs. Quality Control

### A (Assurance) = Process
- *Focus*: Are we following the rules?
- *Tool*: Audits, Process Analysis.

### C (Control) = Product
- *Focus*: Is the deliverable correct?
- *Tool*: Inspections, Testing.

---

## 🛠️ The Diagnostic Toolset (ECO 2.7.2)

- **Fishbone (Ishikawa)**: Finding the "Why" behind the "What."
- **Pareto Chart**: Strategic Prioritization (80/20 Rule).
- **Control Charts**: Measuring the "Voice of the Process."

---

## 🔄 Quality in Agile

- **DoD (Definition of Done)**: The standardized quality contract.
- **TDD (Test-Driven Development)**: Prevention through code.
- **CI (Continuous Integration)**: Automated appraisal.

---

## 🔄 The PMP Sequence

1. **Plan Quality**: Set the rules.
2. **Manage Quality**: Check the process.
3. **Control Quality**: Check the results.
4. **Validate Scope**: Customer signs off.

---
