# Day 32: Strategic Sourcing & Partnering
### External Delivery & Risk Allocation (ECO 2.11)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "Procurement is the art of buying the right risk."

- **Objective 1**: Execute a rigorous "Make-or-Buy" analysis.
- **Objective 2**: Select the optimal Contract Type for the project's risk profile.
- **Objective 3**: Establish objective Source Selection Criteria.

---

## 🏗️ The Procurement Strategy (ECO 2.11.4)

### The Legal Framework
- **Delivery Method**: How the work is structured.
- **Contract Type**: How we pay and who owns the risk.
- **Procurement Phases**: The sequencing of the acquisition.

**Goal**: Aligning external capabilities with internal project milestones.

---

## ⚖️ The Risk Continuum: Contract Types

- **Fixed-Price (FP)**: 
  - *Risk*: Seller owns cost overruns.
  - *Context*: Clearly defined scope.
- **Cost-Reimbursable (CR)**: 
  - *Risk*: Buyer (You) pays actual costs.
  - *Context*: High R&D / Unclear scope.
- **Time & Materials (T&M)**: 
  - *Risk*: Shared.
  - *Context*: Short-term / Emergency support.

---

## 📐 Defining the Need: The SOW (ECO 2.11.1)

### The Procurement Statement of Work
- **Technical Specifications**: The "What."
- **Performance Requirements**: The "How well."
- **Quantity & Schedule**: The "When & How many."

**Strategic Key**: An ambiguous SOW is a breeding ground for Change Requests and Claims.

---

## 📢 Communicating with Sellers (ECO 2.11.2)

### Tools of the Trade
- **RFP (Request for Proposal)**: Sell us your solution.
- **RFQ (Request for Quotation)**: Give us your price.
- **IFB (Invitation for Bid)**: Standardized competitive bidding.
- **Bidder Conferences**: Ensuring a "Level Playing Field."

---

## 🔍 Source Selection (ECO 2.11.2)

### Beyond the Price Tag
- **Weighted Criteria**: Technical ability, financial health, past performance, and cultural fit.
- **The "Strategic Tie-Break"**: Who is most likely to act as a **Partner**, not just a vendor?

---

## 🛠️ Managing the Supplier (ECO 2.11.3)

### Procurement Administration
- **Performance Reviews**: Are they delivering against the SOW?
- **Claims Management**: Resolving disputes before they require legal intervention.
- **Payments**: Ensuring fiscal stewardship throughout the vendor lifecycle.

---

## 🔄 Collaborating on the Solution (ECO 2.11.5)

### Agile Procurement
- **Shared Risk / Shared Reward**.
- **Collaborative Planning**: Bringing the seller into the "Integrated Team."
- **Continuous Alignment**: Iterative feedback loops on external deliverables.
