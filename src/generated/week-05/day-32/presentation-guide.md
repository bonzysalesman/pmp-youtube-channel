# Presentation Guide: Day 33 - Governance & Closure

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Institutional Leaders**. You are teaching your peers how to ensure their projects are governed with integrity and closed with precision. Your tone should be **Formal**, **Accountable**, and **Reflective**. You are coaching them to recognize that a PM's reputation is built on how they steward the project's final hours.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "A project without governance is a project without a pilot. A project without closure is a project that never ends. Welcome to Day 33."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: This is about **Professional Responsibility**. High-achieving PMs don't just "get it done"; they get it done *within the rules*.

### Slide 3: Establishing Governance
- **The Core Focus**: Governance is the "Structure of Power." 
- **Peer Insight**: "Don't fight the organization's governance; leverage it. Use the Steering Committee as your strategic shield."

### Slide 4: Escalation Paths
- **The Technical "Why"**: The exam loves scenarios where the PM is overstepping their authority. 
- **The Hook**: "If the decision changes the project's **Business Value**, it's usually not your decision to make. Know your thresholds."

### Slide 5: Planning for Closure
- **The Core Focus**: "Agreement upfront prevents friction at the end." 
- **Coaching Note**: Emphasize that closure criteria should be agreed upon during the **Planning** phase, not the **Closing** phase.

### Slide 6: Performing the Closure
- **The Checklist**: This is a direct test of the "Close Project or Phase" process.
- **Peer Insight**: "Archiving is not just for auditors; it’s for the PM who wants to learn from their own history."

### Slide 7: Lessons Learned
- **The Distinction**: Ensure they know the difference between a "Retrospective" (Agile/Incremental) and "Lessons Learned" (Formal/Phase-end). Both are for continuous improvement.

### Slide 8: Managing the Transition
- **The "Business" View**: Handing over a product is useless if no one knows how to use it. Transition is about **Benefits Realization Readiness**.

### Slide 9: WG 2 Review
- **Recap**: Briefly touch on the journey from Methodology to Governance. This builds confidence in the "Big Picture."

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Surprise End-Date"
**Objective**: Professionalism under pressure.
- **Response**: **Impact Assessment**. You must determine the state of the project at the moment of cancellation.
- **Closure**: **YES**. You still follow the formal closure process. You archive what you have, you release the team, and you document the reason for termination. You do NOT just walk away.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **Governance as "Micro-management"**: Correct this mindset. Governance is about **Support** and **Direction**, not checking every task.
- **Skipping Closure for "Success"**: Even if the project was a 10/10 success, you MUST close the books.
- **Procurement vs. Project Closure**: Remember, you must close ALL procurements *before* you can formally close the project.
- **Agile "Closure"**: Remind them that in Agile, we "close" at the end of every iteration (Retrospectives/Demos), but there is still a final Release closure.
