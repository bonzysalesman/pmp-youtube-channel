# Self-Study Lesson: Day 32 - The Sourcing Strategist

## 🧠 The Strategic Perspective
Welcome to Day 32. Today, we look **Beyond the Organization**. 

As a Strategic Peer, you know that your responsibility for the project outcome doesn't end where the vendor's contract begins. On the PMP exam, procurement is often the area where students get "tripped up" by legalistic jargon. Our goal is to boil it down to its strategic essence: **Who owns the risk, and how do we pay for it?**

---

## 1. The Strategic Filter: Make-or-Buy
The PMP doesn't want you to be a shopping expert; it wants you to be a decision expert. 
- **The Calculation**: You must compare the *Total Cost of Ownership* for internal development (Make) vs. external acquisition (Buy).
- **The "Why"**: Sometimes "Buying" is faster but more expensive; sometimes "Making" keeps intellectual property internal but drains your core team. 

**Strategic Pivot**: If the project requires specialized skills for a short duration, **Buy** is almost always the "Strategic" answer on the exam.

---

## 2. Mastering the Contract Types (The Big Three)
The exam tests your ability to match the contract to the situation.

| Type | When to use? | Risk Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed Price (FP)** | Scope is 100% clear. | Seller (Vendor) |
| **Cost Plus (CR)** | Scope is R&D/Unclear. | Buyer (You) |
| **Time & Materials (T&M)** | Short term / Augmentation. | Shared |

**Strategic Differentiator**: If you change the scope of a **Fixed Price** contract, you expect a **Change Request** and a price increase. If you change the scope of a **Cost Plus** contract, you just expect to pay more for the extra hours.

---

## 3. The Sourcing Sequence
How do we find the right partner?
1. **Procurement SOW**: What are we buying? (Requirements).
2. **Bid Documents**: RFQ (Price), RFP (Solution), IFB (Standard).
3. **Source Selection Criteria**: The "Scorecard" (Price, Experience, Financial Stability).
4. **Bidder Conferences**: Ensuring all potential sellers have the same information at the same time (Fairness).

---

## 🎯 PMP Mindset Tip: "Fairness & Ethics"
PMI is obsessed with procurement ethics. 
- **The Conflict**: If a potential seller is your brother-in-law, you MUST disclose it. 
- **The Equalizer**: You cannot give "extra info" to one bidder over another. The Bidder Conference is the tool for total transparency.

---

## 🛠️ Performance Activity: The Vendor Audit
Think about a vendor you are currently working with.
1. **The Contract**: If the scope doubled tomorrow, who would "Lose" the most money? (Check your contract type).
2. **The SOW**: Is the SOW clear enough that a third party could audit the deliverables without asking you questions?
3. **Internal Goal**: Identify one "Secondary Risk" introduced by a vendor today (e.g., "The vendor might go bankrupt"). What is your plan for that?
