# Presentation Guide: Day 29 - Plan & Manage Schedule

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Architects**. You are moving from the "What" (Scope) to the "When." Your tone should be **Mathematical** and **Strategic**. You are helping your peers see the project as a living, logical network, not just a list of dates.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Every PM has a calendar; very few PMs have a logic-driven schedule. Today, we bridge that gap. Welcome to Day 29."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: Emphasize that we aren't "scheduling," we are **architecting velocity**.

### Slide 3: Transforming the WBS
- **The Core Focus**: Explain that the WBS dictionary often contains the seeds of the activities. 
- **Peer Insight**: "Don't decompose just to have a long list. Decompose until you can assign a clear owner and a clear estimate."

### Slide 4: Critical Path Method (CPM)
- **The Technical "Why"**: This is where many fail the exam. 
- **The "Strategic Pivot"**: "Critical Path isn't about being 'important.' It’s about being **inflexible**. If it moves, we move."

### Slide 5: Historical Benchmarks
- **Context**: Acknowledge that in the real world, we often "guess." On the exam, we "Utilize Organizational Process Assets."

### Slide 6: Schedule Compression
- **The Trade-off**: This is the most tested part of schedule management. 
- **Logic**: Crashing = $$$ | Fast Tracking = ⚠️. 
- **Coaching Note**: Ensure they know they can *only* compress activities on the Critical Path to save time. Compressing an activity with float does nothing for the end date.

### Slide 7: Resource Leveling vs. Smoothing
- **The Nuance**:
- *Leveling*: "We don't have enough people. The project HAS to take longer."
- *Smoothing*: "We have enough people, but we're making them work 12 hours today and 2 hours tomorrow. Let's even it out."

### Slide 8: Agile vs. Predictive
- **The Key Term**: **Rolling Wave Planning**. Explain that this is the bridge between the two methodologies.

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Schedule Crisis"
**Objective**: Practical application of compression logic.
- **Constraint**: Fixed Deadline + Fixed Budget.
- **The Answer**: **Fast Tracking**. Since you cannot spend more money (No Crashing), you must take the risk of rework by overlapping tasks.
- **The Risk**: You must communicate the potential for **Rework** and **Quality Degradation**.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **Lags vs. Leads**: Remind them that a **Lead** speeds you up (negative time), while a **Lag** slows you down (positive time/waiting).
- **Float vs. Buffer**: Float is a mathematical result of the schedule logic. A buffer (as in Critical Chain) is an intentional management reserve.
- **Software Reliance**: Tell them: "The PMP won't ask you to click a button in MS Project; they will ask you to determine the impact of a dependency change."
