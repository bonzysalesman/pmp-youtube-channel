# Situational Analysis: Symptom vs. Cause
- **Tactical Fix**: Addressing the immediate symptom (e.g., "Sending a missing report").
- **Strategic Fix**: Addressing the underlying cause (e.g., "Analyzing the Communications Management Plan").
- **Key**: PMP rewards the leader who stabilizes the system.

# Performance Challenge 1
**A team member informs the PM that a task will be delayed by two days, potentially impacting the critical path. What should the PM do FIRST?**
A. Update the project schedule to reflect the delay.
B. Meet with the team member to assess the impact and identify workarounds.
C. Report the delay to the project sponsor immediately.
D. Direct the team to work overtime to ensure compliance.

---

<!-- Slide 9 -->
# Debrief: Challenge 1
**Answer: B. Meet with the team member to assess the impact.**
- **Strategic Logic**: Option B is an **Assessment**.
- Choices A and D are premature Actions. C is unjustified Escalation.
- **Mindset**: We do not adjust baselines until the impact is quantified.

---

<!-- Slide 10 -->
# Performance Challenge 2
**A Waterfall organization is transitioning to Agile. The team is waiting for the PM to assign tasks. What is the BEST action?**
A. Create a detailed task-tracking board with assigned responsibilities.
B. Request an experienced Scrum Master to replace the current lead.
C. Adopt a servant leadership style and coach the team on self-organization.
D. Escalate the team's behavior to the functional leadership team.

---

<!-- Slide 11 -->
# Debrief: Challenge 2
**Answer: C. Adopt a servant leadership style and coach the team on self-organization.**
- **Strategic Logic**: Agile Mindset = Team Empowerment.
- The PM’s role shifts from "Manager" to "Facilitator/Coach."
- Options A and D represent "Command & Control" behaviors that fail the Agile Lens.
