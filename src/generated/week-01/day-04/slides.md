# The Servant Leadership Archetype
- Evolution from "Authority" to "Facilitation."
- **Primary Strategic Objective**: Remove impediments and safeguard the team.
- Focus on professional development, coaching, and empowerment.
- "We serve the team’s needs so the team can deliver the project’s value."
