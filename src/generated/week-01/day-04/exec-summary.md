# Executive Summary: Day 4 - Leadership Archetypes & Core Resources

**Key Points:**
- **Servant Leadership Archetype**: The foundational PMP leadership style—prioritizing impediment removal and team empowerment.
- **Strategic Adaptation (Tailoring)**: Customizing project methodologies (Waterfall, Agile, Hybrid) to the specific organizational context.
- **Resource Hierarchy**:
  - **ECO**: The Strategic Roadmap (The "What").
  - **PMBOK 7 & Agile Guide**: The Performance Domains & Practices (The "How").
  - **Process Groups**: The Operational Framework (The "When").

**Strategic Peer Context:**
As experienced leaders, we understand that one size fits none. Day 4 is about **Calibrating our Resource Toolkit**. We are distinguishing between the "Theory" of PMBOK® 7 and the "Execution" of the Process Groups. For our professional cohort, the strategic goal is to know exactly which reference to leverage based on the project’s specific constraints. We aren't just reading books; we are building an analytical arsenal.
