**Notes (Verbatim):**
"In the PMP context, a leader is measured by their ability to facilitate. Your job isn't to dictate tasks; it’s to remove impediments. If the team is blocked, the PM is failing. We coach, we shield, and we empower. If an answer choice sounds like 'Command and Control'—cross it off immediately.”
