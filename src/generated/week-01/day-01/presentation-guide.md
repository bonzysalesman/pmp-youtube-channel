# Presentation Guide: Day 1 - Introduction to PMP & Course Overview

- **Verbatim Hook**: "Good morning/afternoon, everyone, and a very warm welcome to Day 1 of our PMP® Exam Preparation Course. My name is [Your Name], and I am excited to guide you through this journey to passing the Project Management Professional (PMP)® exam.”
- **Learning Objectives**: Understand the exam structure, master core concepts, apply the PMP Mindset, and develop study strategies.

Over the next few days/weeks, we will embark on a structured journey to equip you with the knowledge and strategies you need to successfully pass the Project Management Professional (PMP)® exam. ### Slide 2: Welcome & Introductions
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"Welcome everyone to our PMP® Exam preparation journey. As your instructor, my role is to guide you through the material and help you build the confidence to succeed. Before we dive into the content, let's take a brief moment to welcome everyone and get a sense of the diverse experience we have. Could you please briefly share your name and project management background?"

---

### Slide 3: Course Purpose & Focus
**Notes (Verbatim):**
”The core purpose of this course is clear and direct: to equip you with everything you need to confidently pass the PMP exam. A crucial point to understand from the outset is that the PMP exam is based on the PMP Exam Content Outline, or ECO. While resources like the PMBOK Guide are essential references, the ECO is the actual blueprint for the exam. This course is structured to guide you systematically through the ECO, providing a detailed review of each domain, task, and enabler.”

---

### Slide 4: Key Learning Outcomes
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"By actively engaging with this course, you will develop several key abilities crucial for passing the PMP exam. You will gain a deep understanding of the PMP Exam Structure, master the core concepts, and learn to identify key Tasks and Enablers. Equally important is cultivating the PMP Mindset – the critical thinking approach needed for exam scenarios. We will also cover strategies for analysing exam questions, developing your study strategy, and boosting your confidence.”

---

### Slide 5: Course Requirements
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"Before starting this course, it's important to ensure you meet the necessary requirements. Firstly, you should meet PMI’s eligibility requirements for the PMP exam. Secondly, you should have basic project management knowledge. Crucially, success requires commitment to study. Finally, having a strong desire to pass will be a key driver for your motivation throughout this journey.”

---

### Slide 6: Course Description - Deep Dive
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"To reiterate, simply reading through the ECO document is not sufficient to pass the exam. The PMP exam tests your deep understanding and application of the concepts. This course is designed to provide that depth through engaging methods. We provide a structured and in-depth walkthrough, ensuring you grasp the 'why' and 'how' behind each task. Whether you are starting fresh or looking to enhance an existing study plan, this course can serve as your foundation.”

---

### Slide 7: Intended Audience & Benefits
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"So, who is this course for? It's tailored for project managers who are serious about getting their PMP certification and want to pass on their first attempt. Provided you meet the PMI eligibility criteria, this course will offer you thorough preparation and a clear understanding of the exam structure and content. You’ll also gain guidance on applying the crucial PMP Mindset and develop effective time management strategies.”

---

### Slide 8: Your Learning Journey: Course Structure & Resources
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"The course follows a logical progression based on the ECO domains, broken down into manageable weekly chunks. To support your preparation, you have access to a range of valuable resources: the Comprehensive Study Guide, over 1000 practice questions, three full-length simulated exams, downloadable templates, and access to a private online forum.”

---

### Slide 9: Activity: Setting Your Goals
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"For our activity today, let's make this personal. Please take a few moments to review the Purpose and Key Learning Outcomes. Then, reflect on your personal goals for pursuing your PMP certification. What specifically motivates you? What career opportunities are you aiming for? Jotting down your motivations can be a powerful way to stay focused throughout your studies.”

---

### Slide 10: Q&A & Next Steps
**Notes (Verbatim):**
"To wrap up, I'd like to open the floor for any initial questions you might have. For your next steps, please begin to familiarise yourself with the course materials. I encourage you to engage with the online forum. Crucially, please download the official PMP ECO from PMI.org and start to familiarise yourself with it. Tomorrow, Day 2, will focus on Getting to Know Your Resources and the ECO in more detail. Thank you for your engagement today. I look forward to continuing our journey tomorrow.”
