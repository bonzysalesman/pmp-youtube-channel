# Self-Study Lesson: Day 1 - Introduction to PMP & Course Overview

**Study Guide Sections**: Sec 0: About Exam, Course Overview; Sec IV: PMP Mindset Intro; Sec V: Prep Intro, Resources

## Key Points from Guide

The course focuses on the **PMP Exam Content Outline (ECO)**, not solely the PMBOK® Guide. It covers three domains:
- **People (42%)**
- **Process (50%)**
- **Business Environment (8%)**

### Key learning outcomes:
- Understanding the exam structure (Domains & Weightings).
- Mastering core concepts relevant to the exam.
- Identifying key Tasks and Enablers within the ECO.
- Applying the **PMP Mindset**.
- Developing effective study strategies.

### The PMP Mindset
The PMP Mindset involves thinking proactively, focusing on value, engaging stakeholders, acting as a servant leader, embracing change, acting ethically, using data, tailoring approaches, and thinking holistically.

### Preparation Strategy
- Create a study plan.
- Use multiple resources (ECO, PMBOK® Guide, Agile Practice Guide, etc.).
- Focus on understanding over memorization.
- Practice exam questions regularly.
