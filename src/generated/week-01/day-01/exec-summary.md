# Key Points: Day 1 - Introduction to PMP & Course Overview

- **ECO Focus**: The exam is built on the PMP Exam Content Outline (ECO), which contains Domains, Tasks, and Enablers.
- **Domain Weightings**:
    - People: 42%
    - Process: 50%
    - Business Environment: 8%
- **PMP Mindset**: A critical component involving proactivity, value-focus, and servant leadership.
- **Success Strategy**: Understanding over memorization, structured study plan, and consistent practice.
- **Resources**: ECO (primary blueprint), PMBOK® Guide, and Agile Practice Guide.
