# Photo Style Guide: PMP Mastery Framework

This guide establishes the visual language for the PMP Mastery Framework training materials, ensuring consistency and a premium feel across all slides.

## Core Visual Principles

### 1. Environment & Lighting
- **Setting**: Modern, bright, and professional office or collaborative learning spaces.
- **Lighting**: Soft, natural daylight. Avoid harsh shadows or artificial-looking studio lights.
- **Backgrounds**: Clean, modern architecture with subtle depth of field (bokeh) to keep focus on the foreground elements.

### 2. Photography Style
- **Realistic Mixed Media**: Blend high-quality realistic photography with clean, digital graphic overlays (2D/3D).
- **Diversity**: Represent a diverse, global cohort of professionals (age, gender, ethnicity).
- **Candid Energy**: People should look engaged, collaborating, or in thought, rather than strictly posing for the camera.

### 3. Graphic Elements
- **Icons**: Use clean, flat-style icons (stroke or solid) for metaphors (e.g., gears for mindset, compass for guidance).
- **Infographics**: 3D-styled paths or roadmaps that feel integrated into the 3D space of the photo.
- **Brand Details**: Incorporate subtle, realistic branded items like "PMP" mugs, notebooks, or digital screens displaying project plans.

### 4. Color Palette
- **Primary**: Professional Deep Blue (`#1a365d`).
- **Accents**: Vibrant Orange (`#ed8936`) and Success Green (`#48bb78`).
- **Neutral**: Clean Whites and Slate Greys.
