# Defining the PMP Mindset
- The exam evaluates your **professional judgment** in high-pressure scenarios.
- It identifies how an ethical, adaptable, and proactive leader acts.
- Mastering the "PMI Lens" is the most efficient way to eliminate distractor options.
- "We aren't just solving problems; we are applying a proprietary logic."

# Core Pillars: The Mindset Arsenal
- **Proactive**: We anticipate, plan, and prevent.
- **Value-Driven**: We prioritize outcomes that support the business case.
- **Stakeholder-Focused**: We emphasize engagement, trust, and shared vision.
- **Collaborative**: We foster psychological safety and "servant leadership."
- **Ethical**: We uphold the non-negotiable standards of the PMI Code of Conduct.

# Strategic Pillar: Proactivity
- **Logic**: Prevention > Correction.
- Identifying potential impediments *before* they manifest.
- Early stakeholder engagement to mitigate future scope misalignment.
- Robust risk management as a foundational leadership habit.

- **Value**: Project success is measured by **Benefits Realization**, not just task completion.
- **Engagement**:
  - Continuous identification and analysis of those affected by the project.
  - Building trust through transparent communication and expectation management.

# Collaborative & Ethical Leadership
- **Collaboration**: Moving from "Command & Control" to "Servant Leadership."
- Fostering **Psychological Safety**: Ensuring the team can raise concerns early and without fear.
- **Ethics (PMI Code)**: Acting with Integrity, Responsibility, Respect, and Fairness in all scenarios.

<!-- Slide 8 -->
# Applied Logic: Navigating the Exam
- Every situational question is a test of your **Mindset Alignment**.
- Clues and Keywords: **"FIRST," "NEXT," "BEST," "MOST IMPORTANT."**
- The PMP favors:
  - Gathering data first.
  - Collaborative decision-making.
  - Formal change management protocols.

---

<!-- Slide 9 -->
# The Decision Cycle: Assess > Meet > Action > Track
- **Assess**: Perform root cause analysis and impact evaluation.
- **Meet**: Consult the appropriate stakeholders/team members.
- **Action**: Implement the logic-based solution.
- **Track**: Monitor results and update necessary documentation.
- **Rule**: Assess before you Act.
