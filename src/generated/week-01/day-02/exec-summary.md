# Key Points: Day 2 - Getting to Know Your Resources & the ECO

- **ECO is the Blueprint**: The exam content is derived directly from the ECO, not just the PMBOK Guide.
- **Resource Toolkit**: 
    - **PMBOK 7**: Focuses on principles and performance domains.
    - **Agile Practice Guide**: Crucial for 50% of the exam content.
    - **Process Groups**: Map the project lifecycle (Initiating to Closing).
- **Exam Structure**: 180 questions in 230 minutes; includes various question types (Matching, Hotspot, etc.).
- **Domain Weightings**: People (42%), Process (50%), Business Environment (8%).
