# day 2 amplified image prompts (brand-aligned)

These amplified prompts maintain the brand's clean vector-illustrative style, utilizing deep violet and gold accents against crisp white cards, within a professional 16:9 composition. This approach creates a premium corporate aesthetic with soft daylight, no clutter, and a strong focal hierarchy that includes space for text overlays.

## shared style preface
Professional PMP training visual, 16:9 composition, premium corporate aesthetic, soft daylight feel translated into clean vector-illustrative style, deep violet and gold accents, crisp white cards, subtle glass gradients, diverse and modern project-management motifs, no clutter, strong focal hierarchy, and space for text overlays.

## slide-specific prompts
1. **cover: toolkit + eco blueprint**  
   A hero scene displaying a project-management toolkit is the primary focus of this image prompt. It includes a laptop with timeline bars and a stacked set of study resources. A subtle PMP brand marker is also included in the scene, which is designed to have a bold visual anchor on the right, providing a quiet area on the left for title text.

2. **core study materials collage**  
   A structured collage of key PMP study materials is the central element of this image, presenting distinct cards for PMBOK, Agile Guide, ECO, and process-group context. Each card is organized with color-coded headings and consistent card spacing to ensure a clean layout.

3. **PMBOK guide focus**  
   This prompt details a premium spotlight visual that highlights the PMBOK guide. One dominant book-like panel is the central element, accompanied by supporting bullets that detail principles, value delivery, and tailoring, using a deep violet and gold color scheme.

4. **agile practice guide**  
   An agile workflow board with columns labeled "to do," "in progress," and "done" is the main subject of this image, incorporating colorful work cards to imply motion and iteration. The composition is designed to create a collaboration vibe while keeping the left area quiet for text.

5. **process groups flow**  
   This prompt specifies a cyclical process diagram with five clearly labeled nodes: initiating, planning, executing, monitoring and controlling, and closing. The visual is integrated into a clean, daylight environment with deep violet and gold lighting accents.

6. **exam format + course focus**  
   A dashboard-style visual is outlined in this prompt, combining a set of key exam metrics and an emphasis chart. The exam format section details 180 questions, 230 minutes, breaks, and question types, while the chart focuses on an ECO-first emphasis.

7. **ECO hierarchy**  
   A three-level model of the ECO is specified for this image, visualizing it as a progression from domains to tasks to enablers. The visual uses arrows and connectors to show the relationships, all presented with a strong card hierarchy.

8. **domain weighting**  
   A clean weighting chart is the core visual of this prompt, using readable labels to detail the division of domains: People at 42%, Process at 50%, and Business Environment at 8%. The chart includes subtle exam-study implication cues within the deep violet and gold color palette.

9. **activity: navigating ECO**  
   A guided-work scene is visualized in this prompt, framing a laptop and a checklist to detail practical actions. These actions include locating domains, tracing tasks, mapping enablers, and recording questions, all presented in a clean, uncluttered layout.

10. **review and next steps**  
    A forward-journey visual is described for this image, incorporating a path or roadmap to represent the progress toward Day 3 mindset study. The overall tone is one of confident progress, with minimal text blocks to maintain a clean aesthetic.
