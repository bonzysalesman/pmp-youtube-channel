## Key Points from Guide

The PMP® Exam is built on the **Exam Content Outline (ECO)**, which defines the syllabus.

### The Exam Format
- **Total Questions**: 180 questions.
- **Duration**: 230 minutes.
- **Breaks**: Two 10-minute breaks (optional).
- **Question Types**: Multiple-choice, multiple-response, matching, hotspot, and fill-in-the-blank.

### Core Resources
1. **PMBOK® Guide (7th Edition)**: Foundation for project management principles and good practices.
2. **Agile Practice Guide**: Essential for agile and hybrid project management concepts (50% of the exam).
3. **PMP® Exam Content Outline (ECO)**: The official blueprint mapping domains, tasks, and enablers.

### Project Management Structure
- **Process Groups**: Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring and Controlling, Closing.
- **Domains**: People (42%), Process (50%), Business Environment (8%).
