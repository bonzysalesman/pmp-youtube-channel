# Day 2: Amplified Image Prompts (Brand-Aligned)

These amplified prompts maintain the brand's clean vector-illustrative style, utilizing deep violet and gold accents against crisp white cards, within a professional 16:9 composition. This approach creates a premium corporate aesthetic with soft daylight, no clutter, and a strong focal hierarchy that includes space for text overlays.

## Shared Style Preface
> **Style Modifier:** Professional PMP training visual, 16:9 composition, premium corporate aesthetic, soft daylight feel translated into clean vector-illustrative style, deep violet and gold accents, crisp white cards, subtle glass gradients, diverse and modern project-management motifs, no clutter, strong focal hierarchy, and space for text overlays.

---

## Slide-Specific Prompts

### 1. Cover: Toolkit + ECO Blueprint
**Prompt:** A hero scene displaying a project-management toolkit as the primary focus. Includes a sleek laptop showing timeline Gantt bars, a stacked set of premium study resources (binders and books), and a subtle gold PMP brand marker. The composition is a bold visual anchor on the right, providing a quiet, deep violet area on the left for title text. Clean vector-illustrative style, soft daylight, premium corporate aesthetic.

### 2. Core Study Materials Collage
**Prompt:** A structured collage of key PMP study materials. Elements include distinct, floating white cards with glassmorphism edges for "PMBOK® Guide", "Agile Practice Guide", "ECO", and "Process Groups". Each card features gold headings, color-coded accents, and consistent spacing. Organized layout against a deep violet background, ensuring a clean and professional corporate aesthetic.

### 3. PMBOK® Guide Focus
**Prompt:** A premium spotlight visual highlighting the PMBOK® Guide. A single, dominant book-style panel with a gold-embossed "PMBOK®" seal is the central element. Accompanying floating white cards use deep violet and gold icons to detail "Principles", "Value Delivery", and "Tailoring". Soft daylight lighting, minimal clutter, focal hierarchy centered on the guide.

### 4. Agile Practice Guide
**Prompt:** A modern agile workflow board with clean columns labeled "To Do", "In Progress", and "Done". Colorful, miniature work cards with gold accents are arranged to imply motion and iterative progress. Deep violet background with a quiet area on the left for text. The composition evokes collaboration and efficiency in a premium vector-illustrative style.

### 5. Process Groups Flow
**Prompt:** A cyclical process diagram featuring five clearly labeled nodes: "Initiating", "Planning", "Executing", "Monitoring & Controlling", and "Closing". The diagram uses gold-accented arrows and deep violet nodes, integrated into a clean, daylight environment. The flow is presented as a professional 16:9 visual with a strong central hierarchy and no clutter.

### 6. Exam Format + Course Focus
**Prompt:** A dashboard-style visual combining key exam metrics and an emphasis chart. The layout includes clean white cards detailing "180 Questions", "230 Minutes", and "ECO-First Emphasis". A gold-accented donut chart highlights the Domain weightings. Professional 16:9 composition, deep violet background, crisp typography-ready space.

### 7. ECO Hierarchy
**Prompt:** A three-level hierarchy model of the Examination Content Outline (ECO). Visualized as a progression from "Domains" to "Tasks" to "Enablers" using sleek gold connectors and floating white cards. Strong vertical or horizontal hierarchy, deep violet accents, premium vector-illustrative style with a focus on structural clarity.

### 8. Domain Weighting
**Prompt:** A clean, professional weighting chart detailing the PMP Domains: "People (42%)", "Process (50%)", and "Business Environment (8%)". Uses deep violet and gold bars or segments with readable labels. Subtle exam-study cues like a gold PMP seal or a graduation cap icon are included. Uncluttered layout with space for overhead text.

### 9. Activity: Navigating ECO
**Prompt:** A guided-work scene framing a white laptop and a checklist card. The checklist details practical actions: "Locate Domains", "Trace Tasks", "Map Enablers", and "Record Questions". Soft daylight feel, deep violet and gold color palette, clean vector-illustrative style. The composition focuses on the interactiveness of the study material.

### 10. Review and Next Steps
**Prompt:** A forward-journey visual featuring a professional roadmap or path leading toward a horizon labeled "Day 3: Mindset Study". Confident, premium tone using deep violet and gold accents. Minimal text blocks and a clean aesthetic to maintain focus on the progression and next steps in the PMP journey.
