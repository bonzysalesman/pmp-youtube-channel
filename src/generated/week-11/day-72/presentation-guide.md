# Presentation Guide: Day 72 - The Master of Outcomes

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Value Architect
Today’s session is for the **Strategic Strategists**. You are teaching your team that delivering a project "On Time and On Budget" is merely the floor, not the ceiling. Your tone should be **Results-Oriented**, **Strategic**, and **Accountable**. You are the link between the board room and the project room.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "The organization doesn't want a project; it wants the *value* the project provides. Welcome to Day 72."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: The move from technical success to **Strategic Success**.

### Slide 3: Output vs. Benefit
- **The Core Focus**: The "Why."
- **Peer Insight**: "Ask them: 'What is the *actual* benefit of your current project?' If they answer with a feature list, correct them. A benefit is a business improvement."

### Slide 4: The Benefits Owner
- **The Technical "Why"**: Sustainability.
- **Coaching Tip**: "The PMP loves to ask who is responsible for the benefits *after* the project. Make sure they know it's almost always a functional leader or the Sponsor."

### Slide 5: Measuring ROI
- **The Strategy**: Performance Transparency.
- **Peer Insight**: "KPIs are the language of leadership. If you can't speak KPIs, you're not in the conversation about value."

### Slide 6: Incremental Value
- **The Strategy**: Agility.
- **Coaching Note**: "The faster we deliver even *some* value, the faster we prove the business case and reduce the risk of total project failure."

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Vanishing ROI"
**Objective**: Protecting the organization's investment over personal pride.
- **Action**: **Business Case Re-evaluation**.
- **Resolution**: This tests the "Sunk Cost" fallacy. Finish a project only if it still provides value relative to the current environment. The PM's role is to provide the data (the analysis of the ROI gap) so the **Sponsor** can make an informed decision on whether to pivot to the new tech or stop the project.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **The Sunk Cost Fallacy**: Remind them: "Money already spent is gone. Only look at the value of the money *remaining* to be spent."
- **Ignoring Intangibles**: Correct the idea that if it isn't money, it isn't value. Reputation and compliance are vital "Intangible" values.
- **PM-as-Owner**: Clarify that the PM cannot be the long-term owner of the benefit. Their role is to **Setup** the owner for success.
