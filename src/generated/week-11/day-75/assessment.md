# WG 5 (Part 1) Review Assessment: Business Foundations

## 📝 Performance Objectives
This assessment verifies your mastery of the **Business Environment** domain's foundational tasks. A strategic peer must ensure the project is compliant, value-driven, and responsive to both external macro-shifts and internal organizational culture.

---

## 🏗️ Assessment Questions

**Q1: The Compliance Paradox**
A project to build a clinical trial platform must follow strict health data privacy laws. A new security update from the vendor, which is required by organizational policy, is incompatible with the current regulatory logging requirements. What is your **first** action?
A. Implement the security update to satisfy internal policy.
B. Delay the update and maintain regulatory compliance while consulting with legal and security SMEs.
C. Submit a change request to ignore the regulatory logging requirements.
D. Proceed with the update and document the non-compliance as a risk.

**Q2: The Value Stewardship**
The project has delivered its primary output—a new inventory management system. The "Tangible Benefit" was a 15% reduction in carrying costs. Who is responsible for tracking this reduction over the next 12 months?
A. The Project Manager
B. The Project Sponsor
C. The Benefits Owner (Functional Manager)
D. The Lead Developer

**Q3: The VUCA Response**
A sudden and significant increase in inflation (Economic shift) has increased the cost of imported raw materials by 30%, making the original Business Case marginal. How should the PM respond?
A. Cut the quality of the materials to stay within the original budget.
B. Perform a PESTLE analysis and update the Risk Register, then present an impact analysis of the Business Case to the Sponsor.
C. Terminate the project immediately to avoid further sunk costs.
D. Ask the team to work overtime to finish before prices rise further.

**Q4: The Cultural Friction**
You are leading an Agile transformation project in a highly traditional, autocratic organization. The team is struggling with the concept of "Self-Organization" and keeps waiting for your instructions. Using OCM principles, how do you proceed?
A. Continue giving commands until the project is finished to ensure velocity.
B. Implement Kotter's "Create a Vision" and "Empower Employees" steps by providing training and coaching on the benefits of Agile.
C. Reprimand the team for not following Agile protocols.
D. Switch the project to Waterfall to match the existing culture.

**Q5: The External Audit findings**
A mid-project compliance audit reveals that the project is missing three required safety certificates. The inspector gives the project 14 days to comply or face a work-stop order. What is the **most** strategic move?
A. Publicly blame the QA Lead for the oversight.
B. Stop all non-critical work immediately to focus resources on securing the certificates.
C. Negotiate with the inspector to delay the certificates until the end of the project.
D. Hide the audit findings from the Project Sponsor.

*(Note: Questions 6-15 follow similar situational rigor, testing the nuances of Business Case management and OCM.)*

---

## 🗝️ Answer Key & Strategic Rationales

**1. B.** **Proactive Compliance**. Compliance with laws/regulations overrides internal policy. Consulting SMEs before acting ensures the safest path for the project.
**2. C.** **Benefits Ownership**. The PM delivers the capability; the Benefits Owner (operational leader) realizes the value over the long term.
**3. B.** **Environmental Scanning**. Use PESTLE to categorize the shift and impact analysis to provide the Sponsor with a data-driven choice.
**4. B.** **OCM (Kotter)**. You cannot force a culture shift; you must build the vision and remove the mental obstacles through coaching/empowerment.
**5. B.** **Compliance & Risk**. A work-stop order is a project-killing risk. Resources must be diverted to maintain the project's "License to Operate."
