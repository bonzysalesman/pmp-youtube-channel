# Presentation Guide: Day 45 - Knowledge & Suppliers

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Intellectual Capitalists**. You are teaching your peers how to treat "Knowledge" as a valuable asset and "Suppliers" as extensions of their team. Your tone should be **Cultivated**, **Observant**, and **Disciplined**. You are coaching them to think about the project’s legacy and its external dependencies.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "A project ends twice: once when the product is delivered, and once when the last lesson is forgotten. Welcome to Day 45."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: This is about **Sustainability**. Managing knowledge so it lasts, and managing suppliers so they deliver.

### Slide 3: Explicit vs. Tacit Knowledge
- **The Core Focus**: The human element. 
- **Peer Insight**: "Don't just point people to a SharePoint folder. That's explicit. Real knowledge transfer happens in the lunchroom, the standup meeting, and the 1-on-1 mentor session."

### Slide 4: Governing Suppliers
- **The Technical "Why"**: Post-award governance. 
- **Coaching Tip**: "The contract is your baseline here. If you are 'asking' for things not in the contract, you are introducing gold-plating or scope creep. Follow the change control system."

### Slide 5: Claims and Disputes
- **The Strategy**: Relationship protection. 
- **Peer Insight**: "On the exam, Negotiation is always the first answer. We are problem solvers, not lawyers."

### Slide 6: Creating the Environment
- **The Strategy**: Psychological Safety.
- **Hook**: "If your team is afraid to admit a mistake, you are losing your most valuable lessons. Your job as PM is to make the truth safe."

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Vanishing SME"
**Objective**: Immediate knowledge preservation.
- **Action**: **Knowledge Management Capture**. This is both 2.16 (Knowledge Transfer) and 2.11 (Contract Compliance).
- **Move**: Initiate **Shadowing/Pair Programming** immediately. Do not wait for a document. Also, check the supplier's contract for "Key Personnel" or "Knowledge Transfer" clauses to see if they are in breach or if you can leverage a transition period.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **"Lessons Learned are for the end"**: Correct this vigorously. Lessons are captured *daily*.
- **Supplier as "The Enemy"**: Remind them that a failed supplier usually means a failed project. Move from adversarial to collaborative governance.
- **Confusing Mentoring/Coaching**: (PMP High Yield). Mentoring is long-term/career; Coaching is short-term/task performance.
