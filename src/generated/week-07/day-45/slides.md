# Day 45: Knowledge & Dependency Stewardship
### Sustaining Intel and Governance (ECO 2.16 | 2.11)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "Knowledge is a deliverable. Suppliers are partners."

- **Objective 1**: Master the **Explicit vs. Tacit** knowledge divide.
- **Objective 2**: Govern the **Supplier Performance Lifecycle**.
- **Objective 3**: Foster a **Learning Culture** that survives the project.

---

## 🧠 Capturing Knowledge (ECO 2.16)

### Two Sides of Wisdom
1. **Explicit Knowledge**: Codified. Databases, White Papers, Reports. (Shared via PMIS).
2. **Tacit Knowledge**: Personal. Know-how, Intuition, Experience. (Shared via Mentoring, Shadowing, Storytelling).

**Strategic Action**: The PM must create an environment where **Sharing is Psychological Safety**.

---

## 🤝 Governing Suppliers (Sec 2.11.3)

### "Control Procurements"
- **Performance Reviews**: Measuring the seller against the SOW.
- **Inspections & Audits**: Verifying quality at the source.
- **Financial Control**: Linking payments to contract milestones.

**Key Mindset**: If it’s not in the contract, it’s not an obligation. If it is in the contract, it’s an audit item.

---

## 🏛️ Claims and Disputes

### The Path to Resolution
1. **Negotiation**: The preferred PMP route (Win-Win).
2. **ADR**: Alternative Dispute Resolution (Mediation/Arbitration).
3. **Litigation**: The last resort (High cost, Lose-Lose relationship).

**Strategic Peer Insight**: Resolve disputes early. A "legal win" is a "project loss" if it stops the work.

---

## 🌱 Creating the Environment (ECO 2.16.2)

- **Transparency**: No-blame lessons learned.
- **Inclusion**: Leveraging diverse perspectives for better solutions.
- **Continuity**: Ensuring knowledge survives transitions (team changes or project handoff).

---

## 🎯 Activity: The Vanishing SME

**Scenario**: Your lead supplier architect is moving to another company in two weeks. They hold all the configuration secrets for your new ERP.

1. Is this a **Knowledge Management** issue or a **Supplier** issue?
2. What is your **Immediate Strategic Move** to preserve the project's future?
