# Presentation Guide: Day 43 - Problem Solving

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Crisis Leaders**. You are teaching your peers how to keep their heads when things go wrong. Your tone should be **Steely**, **Analytical**, and **Empowering**. You are coaching them to recognize that an issue is an opportunity to prove the strength of their management framework.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "Every project has problems. The difference between a Senior PM and a Junior PM is the system they use to solve them. Welcome to Day 43."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: This is about **Integrity of Resolution**. It's not just "fixing things"; it's managing the impact.

### Slide 3: Risk vs. Issue
- **The Core Focus**: The ECO transition. 
- **Peer Insight**: "The exam will try to trick you here. If it says it's happening *right now*, it's an issue. Always use the Issue Log as your first administrative action."

### Slide 4: The Resolution Loop
- **The "So What?"**: Structure provides calm. 
- **Coaching Tip**: "The 'Verification' step is the most commonly missed. Don't take the team's word for it; see the result."

### Slide 5: Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
- **The Technical "Why"**: This is high-yield for situational questions. 
- **The Hook**: "If you don't find the root cause, you'll be solving this same problem again in three weeks."

### Slide 6: Collaborative Resolution
- **The Strategy**: Leading through people.
- **Peer Insight**: "You don't need to be the smartest person in the room; you need to be the one who gets all the smartest people in the room talking to each other."

### Slide 7: Escalation Stewardship
- **The Strategy**: Authority Management.
- **Coaching Note**: "Escalation is not failure. It's respect for the project's governance limits. But always come with a plan."

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Server Crash"
**Objective**: Balancing tactical recovery with strategic resolution.
- **Action**: **Assess the Impact**. How critical is those 4 hours vs. 2 days to the final deadline? 
- **Decision**: You likely do the **Patch** (Workaround) to keep testing moving *while simultaneously* planning the **Rebuild** (Corrective Action) for the weekend or a non-critical window. This is "Attacking the issue optimally."

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **"Issues are bad"**: Correct this mindset. Issues are just data.
- **Hiding Issues**: Emphasize that transparency builds trust, while hiding problems destroys it.
- **Closing too early**: Remind them that an issue isn't "Done" until the impact has been mitigated and the root cause addressed.
