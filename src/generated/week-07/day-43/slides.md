# Day 43: The Issue Steward
### Mastering Problem Solving (ECO 2.15)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "A problem defined is a problem half-solved."

- **Objective 1**: Distinguish between Risk and Issue with precision.
- **Objective 2**: Apply the "Attack" mindset for optimal resolution.
- **Objective 3**: Leverage Collaborative Leadership in crisis.

---

## 🚦 From Potential to Reality (ECO 2.15.1)

### The Pivot Point
- **Risk**: Uncertain future event (Risk Register).
- **Issue**: Current impediment (Issue Log).

**Strategic Action**: When a trigger occurs, the PM **immediately** logs the issue and assesses the impact on project objectives.

---

## 🛠️ The Resolution Loop (ECO 2.15.2)

1. **Identification**: See the deviation or hear the report.
2. **Logging**: Record in the **Issue Log** (Accountability).
3. **Analysis**: Perform **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)**.
4. **Evaluation**: Compare workarounds vs. corrective actions.
5. **Implementation**: Assign owner and execute.
6. **Verification**: Confirm resolution before closure.

---

## 🔍 Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

### Finding the "Why"
- **Ishikawa (Fishbone)**: Visualizing categories of causes (People, Process, Tech).
- **5 Whys**: Digging deep to find systemic failures.

**Goal**: Move beyond "fixing symptoms" to "eradicating causes."

---

## 🤝 Collaborative Resolution (ECO 2.15.3)

### The Team Approach
- **SME Integration**: Bringing in the technical experts.
- **Consensus Building**: Facilitating discussion to find the "Optimal Path."
- **Transparency**: Keeping stakeholders informed of impact and options.

---

## 🪜 Escalation Stewardship

- **Thresholds**: When is it too big for the PM?
- **The Sponsor Role**: Managing high-level blockers and budget impacts.

**Strategic Peer Insight**: Never escalate without a recommendation. Escalation is a request for **Authority**, not a request for a solution.

---

## 🎯 Activity: The Server Crash

**Scenario**: During a critical testing phase, the primary server crashes. The Lead Developer says they can fix it in 4 hours with a "patch" (workaround) or in 2 days with a "full rebuild" (corrective action).

1. What is your **First Strategic Move**?
2. How do you decide between the **Patch** and the **Rebuild**?
