# Day 44: Governance through Change
### Integrated Change Control (ECO 2.10)

---

## 🎯 Today's Strategic Focus

> "Change is a journey, not a destination. Manage the map."

- **Objective 1**: Master the **Integrated Change Control** workflow.
- **Objective 2**: Navigate the **Change Control Board (CCB)** with data.
- **Objective 3**: Protect the **Project Baselines** through rigorous assessment.

---

## 🏗️ Embracing the Inevitable (ECO 2.10.1)

### The Strategic Shift
- **Old View**: Change is a failure of planning.
- **Strategic View**: Change is a response to evolving reality.

**Goal**: Establish the **Change Management Plan** early so the process is ready before the pressure is on.

---

## 🔄 The Gold Standard Flow (ECO 2.10.3)

1. **Change Request (CR)**: The formal proposal.
2. **Impact Assessment**: The PM's work (Time, Cost, Scope, Risk).
3. **Communication**: Informing the team/stakeholders.
4. **CCB Review**: The formal decision body.
5. **Update Plans**: Reflecting the approved change.
6. **Communication**: Closing the loop with the requester.

---

## ⚖️ The Impact Assessment (ECO 2.10.2)

### The Ripple Effect
- **Scope**: What is added? What is removed?
- **Schedule**: Does it shift the Critical Path?
- **Cost**: Is there budget in the Management Reserve?
- **Quality**: Can we still meet the standards?
- **Risk**: Does this introduce NEW threats?

---

## 🏛️ The Change Control Board (CCB)

- **Authority**: Decides on changes exceeding PM limits.
- **Members**: Stakeholders, Sponsors, SMEs.
- **The PM Role**: Facilitator and Information Provider.

**Key**: The PM provides the **Impact Data**; the CCB provides the **Value Decision**.

---

## 📦 Agile vs. Predictive Changes

### Methodology Matters
- **Predictive**: Formal CR -> CCB -> Baseline Update.
- **Agile**: Backlog Refinement -> Prioritization -> Next Sprint.
- **Hybrid**: Strategic changes (Formal) vs. Feature changes (Backlog).

---

## 🎯 Activity: The "Urgent" Feature

**Scenario**: A key customer calls you and demands a new security feature "immediately." They claim the project will be useless without it.

1. Do you **Tell the Team to start work** or **Ask for a formal CR**?
2. What is your **First Strategic Question** to the customer?
