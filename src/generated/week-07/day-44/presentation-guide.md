# Presentation Guide: Day 44 - Manage Project Changes

## 🗣️ Facilitator Persona: The Strategic Peer
Today’s session is for the **Guardians of the Plan**. You are teaching your peers how to stop "Saying Yes" and start "Managing Impact." Your tone should be **Principled**, **Data-Driven**, and **Authoritative**. You are coaching them to recognize that the Change Management Process is their best defense against project failure.

---

## 🧭 Slide-by-Slide Briefing

### Slide 1: Introduction
- **Hook**: "The plan is not the project. The project is a living entity that evolves. Change management is how we control that evolution. Welcome to Day 44."

### Slide 2: Objectives
- **Strategic Focus**: This is about **Integrated Governance**. We don't change one thing; we change the *project*.

### Slide 3: Embracing the Inevitable
- **The Core Focus**: Readiness. 
- **Peer Insight**: "The most dangerous time for a project is when a famous stakeholder asks for a 'small favor.' That is when your process must be at its strongest."

### Slide 4: The Gold Standard Flow
- **The Technical "Why"**: The PMP exam tests the *order* of these steps. 
- **Coaching Tip**: "Assessment must ALWAYS come before the CCB. Never walk into a CCB meeting without clear data on the cost and schedule impact."

### Slide 5: The Impact Assessment
- **The "So What?"**: The ripple effect. 
- **Peer Insight**: "A change in scope is like a stone thrown into a pond. You must account for the ripples on the distant shores of Risk and Quality."

### Slide 6: The Change Control Board (CCB)
- **The Strategy**: Authority.
- **Hook**: "The CCB is your partner in governance. They take the strategic heat, while you provide the technical light."

### Slide 7: Agile vs. Predictive
- **The Methodology Bridge**: How we adapt to speed. 
- **Coaching Note**: "In Agile, the Product Owner is essentially the CCB for the backlog. Don't confuse flexibility with a lack of control."

---

## 🎯 Activity Debrief: "The Urgent Feature"
**Objective**: Protecting the process under pressure.
- **Action**: **Ask for a formal CR**. Even for "urgent" features, the process must be followed to ensure the project remains viable.
- **Question**: "**What is the business impact if we defer this to the next release?**" (Testing for actual urgency vs. stakeholder enthusiasm). This data is required for the Impact Analysis.

---

## 🛑 Common Pitfalls to Address
- **Changes as "Bad News"**: Correct this. Changes are often opportunities for more value.
- **Bypassing the CCB**: Remind them that bypassing the board puts the risk of failure solely on the PM's shoulders.
- **Updating only the "Affected" Doc**: Emphasize that Integrated Change Control requires updating the *entire* Integrated Management Plan.
