# PMP Mastery Framework: 13-Week Master Study Calendar

This calendar provides the definitive roadmap for the **PMP Mastery Framework**. It is designed to bridge professional experience with the PMP Exam Content Outline (ECO) over a structured 13-week journey.

## 📊 Summary of Journey
- **Duration**: 13 Weeks (87 Active Units)
- **Domain Focus**: People (42%), Process (50%), Business Environment (8%)
- **Rigor**: Adaptive (Predictive + Agile + Hybrid integration throughout)
