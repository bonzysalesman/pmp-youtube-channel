# Executive Summary: Day 8 - Building A Strategic Team

**Key Points:**
- **Dynamic Curation**: Team building is a strategic, continuous leadership responsibility, evolving throughout the project lifecycle.
- **Analytical Skills Assessment (Task 1.6.1)**: Systematically identifying required competencies (Technical, PM, Soft Skills) and mapping gaps using a **Skills Matrix**.
- **Resource Strategy (Task 1.6.2)**: Defining roles, authorities, and accountability via the **RACI Matrix** and the **Resource Management Plan**.
- **Capability Maturity (Task 1.6.3)**: Leveraging coaching and mentoring to continuously align team skills with evolving project complexity.
- **Institutional Knowledge Transfer (Task 1.6.4)**: Implementing formal processes to capture tacit knowledge (Experience) and explicit knowledge (Documentation).

**Strategic Peer Context:**
As we initiate the "People" domain (42% of the exam), we must shift our perspective from "Managing Resources" to **"Curing Talent."** The PMP framework emphasizes that a team isn't merely assembled; it is strategically developed. Your primary differentiator in situational questions is the use of the **Skills Matrix** and **RACI Chart** to drive objective decision-making. We don't solve for personnel gaps with intuition; we solve them with a deliberate Resource Management strategy.
