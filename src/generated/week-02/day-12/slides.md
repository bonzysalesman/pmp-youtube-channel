# Peer Interconnectivity: The Integration
- **Curation (1.6)** identifies the experts.
- **Norms (1.12)** define the experts' interaction model.
- **Negotiation (1.8)** secures the resources the experts need.

# Strategic Scenario 1
**A conflict has emerged regarding resource allocation. One member feels overloaded, while another feels their skills are underutilized. Which action best demonstrates EMPOWERMENT?**

A. Enforce the original project plan and remind the team of their commitments.
B. Facilitate a discussion where the team collaboratively determines the optimal task redistribution (Joint Problem-Solving).
C. Escalate to functional leadership for a directive reassignment.
D. Unilaterally rebalance the workload to ensure schedule adherence.

# Strategic Scenario 2
**A new team is experiencing frictional meetings due to frequent interruptions and ambiguous outcomes. Which action is most aligned with defining OPERATIONAL NORMS?**

A. Distribute a directive memo outlining proper meeting protocols.
B. Facilitate a workshop to co-create and document consensus-based behavior norms (Team Charter).
C. Personally intervene in all meetings to enforce discipline and order.

# Strategic Scenario 3
**A critical vendor offers a fixed price but an extended delivery timeline. Your priority is cost control with accelerated delivery. Which analytical tool best identifies the compromise threshold?**

A. Critical Path Method (CPM) - Float Analysis.
B. Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) - Decomposition.
C. BATNA Analysis (Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement).
D. ZOPA Evaluation (Zone of Possible Agreement).

---

<!-- Slide 13 -->
# Benchmarking Debrief
- **Scenario 1: Option B**. Empowerment shifts the problem-solving responsibility to the team.
- **Scenario 2: Option B**. Effective norms require consensus to drive ownership.
- **Scenario 3: Option D**. The ZOPA is the specific tool used to identify the overlap of interests.
- *Insight: We solve for the system, not the person.*
