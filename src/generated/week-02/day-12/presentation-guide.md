## 🎯 Practice Application (Strategic Perspective)
- **Scenario**: You're doing the practice session and you find yourself constantly choosing the 'Directive' answer because it feels 'faster' or 'more efficient.'
- **Insight**: "This is a critical indicator of a 'Managerial Bias.' On the PMP, 'fast' is often 'wrong.' The exam values the 'sustainable' path. It takes longer to facilitate a consensus-based ground-rule workshop than to send a memo, but the workshop builds a self-correcting system. The memo builds a dependency. Always solve for the system, not the immediate crisis."
