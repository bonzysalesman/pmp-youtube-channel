# Executive Summary: Day 9 - Defining Operational Ground Rules

**Key Points:**
- **The Social Contract**: Ground rules establish the professional norms for communication, decision-making, and conflict resolution (Working Agreements).
- **Consensus-Driven Ownership**: Norms must be co-created by the team to ensure buy-in; they are not managerial directives.
- **Team Charter Integration**: Ground rules serve as the operational core of the **Team Charter**, alongside values, objectives, and roles.
- **Strategic Modeling**: The PM must model the agreed-upon behaviors and facilitate team-led accountability.
- **Professional Conflict Resolution**: Addressing violations promptly and privately, focusing on the system/norm rather than the individual.

**Strategic Peer Context**:
Ground rules represent the difference between a collection of individuals and a high-performing unit. In the PMP environment, the **Team Charter** is your most critical operational artifact. Your strategic advantage in situational questions lies in recognizing that the team is the primary "owner" of these rules. If a conflict arises regarding team behavior, your first strategic action is to **Revisit the Team Charter**. We don't police behavior; we facilitate the team's own professional standards.
