# Self-Study Lesson: Day 9 - Establishing Operational Norms

## Strategic Alignment: Why This Matters
Yesterday we curated our talent; today we define our operational excellence. Ground rules are the professional "Table Stakes" for project delivery. On the PMP exam, the **Team Charter** is the definitive reference for resolving internal friction. Understanding that these rules are co-created through consensus—rather than imposed by authority—is critical for mastering the People Domain.

## Analytical Framework: The Core Concepts
We are focusing on three strategic pillars of team governance today:
1. **Organizational Calibration**: Ensuring team norms align with broader corporate policies and ethical standards.
2. **Active Adherence**: Shifting from "Enforcement" to "Modeling." As a leader, you serve as the benchmark for the team's behavior.
3. **Resolution Logic**: Managing deviations from the charter **Privately, Promptly, and Professionally**.

## The Strategic Perspective
My primary finding in this area is the **Power of the Charter**. Situational questions often present a scenario where a team is failing to collaborate. Your analytical instinct should immediately look for the "Consensus-based Agreement." If the team hasn't defined how they communicate or make decisions, they haven't yet institutionalized the PMP Mindset. The PM's role is to facilitate this definition process early in the project lifecycle.

## Performance Benchmarking
- **The Challenge**: Two senior leads are engaged in an escalatory debate in a public communications channel.
- **The Managerial Instinct**: "Correct their behavior publicly to remind the entire cohort of the professional standards." -> **INCORRECT**.
- **The Strategic Response**: "Intervene privately with both individuals to review the Team Charter’s ground rules on professional communication and resolve the underlying conflict collaboratively (Meet/Action)." -> **CORRECT**.
