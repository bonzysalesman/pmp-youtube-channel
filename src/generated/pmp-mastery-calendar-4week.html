Compression Strategy

From 13 Weeks to 4 Weeks

This intensive calendar compresses the comprehensive 13-week program into 4 weeks by combining related topics, eliminating rest days, and focusing on high-yield content. Designed for experienced PMs with strong foundational knowledge who need rapid certification preparation.

Prerequisites: 5+ years PM experience, familiarity with PMBOK concepts, ability to commit 25-30 hours per week. This is not a beginner track.

Study Load: 3.5-4 hours daily, 6 days per week. Each day combines 2-3 topics from the original calendar. Practice tests are integrated throughout rather than isolated.
