# Day 15: Ensure Team Trained
## PMP Mastery Framework
### People Domain: Task 1.5

---

# Learning Objectives
- **Systematize Analysis**: Conduct Skills Gap Analysis to identify competency deficits.
- **Optimize Delivery**: Evaluate training modalities based on ROI and project constraints.
- **Plan Resources**: Integrate training logistics into the project baseline.
- **Validate Impact**: Apply the Kirkpatrick Model to measure training efficacy.

---

# The Strategic Imperative
- **Risk Mitigation**: Skill gaps are latent risks. Training is the fix.
- **Human Capital Optimization**: Increasing team velocity through expert capability.
- **Stakeholder Alignment**: Ensuring all participants understand the "Rules of the Game."

---

# Step 1: Competency Assessment (1.5.1)
- **Review Scope**: What does the project *demand*?
- **Define Roles**: What competencies are *required*?
- **Gap Analysis**: Required vs. Current.
- **Dimensions**:
  - Technical / Domain
  - Project Management Governance
  - Professional / Soft Skills

---

# Step 2: Training Modalities (1.5.2)
- **Formal**: Courses and certifications.
- **Informal**: Mentoring, Coaching, OJT.
- **Digital**: CBT, E-Learning, Simulations.
- **Social**: Workshops, Lunch & Learns.
- **Strategy**: Match the *method* to the *man* and the *milestone*.

---

# Step 3: Resource Allocation (1.5.3)
- **Budgeting**: Fees, materials, travel.
- **The "Hidden Cost"**: Opportunity cost of lost productivity.
- **Scheduling**: Target low-intensity project periods.
- **Logistics**: Venue, tech, and "Cognitive Bandwidth."

---

# Step 4: Measuring Outcomes (1.5.4)
### The Kirkpatrick Model
1. **Reaction**: Did they find value?
2. **Learning**: Did they pass the test?
3. **Behavior**: Are they applying it on the job?
4. **Results**: Did the project performance improve?

---

# PMP Mindset: The "Assess First" Rule
- **Scenario**: A stakeholder complains they don't understand the reports.
- **Reactionary PM**: Sends them a 50-page manual.
- **Mastery PM**: **Assesses** the specific knowledge gap through a conversation, then provides a **targeted** 5-minute walkthrough (OJT).

---

# Summary & Next Steps
- Training is an **investment**, not an expense.
- Use **data** (Gaps) to drive the **plan** (Modalities).
- **Measure** the behavioral shift, not just attendance.
- **Tomorrow**: Day 16 - Virtual Teams: Engagement & Support.

---

# 🎯 Performance Benchmarking
- **Question**: A team member is struggling with a new software tool, impacting the critical path. The budget is tight. What is the BEST first step for the PM?
  - A) Send the member to an external 3-day certification.
  - B) Have a senior team member provide 1-on-1 mentoring.
  - C) Replace the team member with a more skilled contractor.
  - D) Request a budget increase for a formal workshop.

---

# 🎯 Debrief
- **Correct Answer: B**
- **Rationale**: Mentoring is low-cost, targeted (OJT), and provides immediate application to the project work. This respects the budget constraint while addressing the critical path risk.
