## 1. The Skill-Engagement-Alignment Loop
The topics of Week 3 are naturally interdependent. A breakdown in one often manifests as a failure in another.

- **The Skill Gap (Day 15)**: If the team isn't trained, "Shared Understanding" is impossible because the technical vocabulary is missing.
- **The Virtual Friction (Day 16)**: Distance amplifies misunderstandings. Without "Virtual Protocols," "Shared Understanding" drifts into "Islands of Logic."
- **The Alignment Gap (Day 17)**: Even a trained, co-located team will fail if they aren't working toward the same "Mental Map."

**Strategic Question**: Are you treating these as separate tasks, or as a single ecosystem of performance?

## 3. PMP Mindset: The "People" Mastery Filter
When approaching Day 19 (and the actual PMP exam), use these four filters for every "People" question:

1. **Assess First**: Never take an "Action" (like firing, training, or meeting) without first "Identifying" or "Assessing" the root cause.
2. **Seek Consensus**: Look for solutions that involve stakeholders (Consensus/Collaboration) rather than top-down mandates.
3. **Servant Leadership**: Choices that empower and remove blockers for the team are prioritized over "Command and Control."
4. **Consistency through Documentation**: If a new agreement is reached, "Update the Plan" or "Log the Decision."

## 🎯 Performance Benchmarking: The "Symphony" Scenario

**The Challenge**: You manage a virtual team of data scientists. A new AI model is failing. A skills gap analysis shows the team lacks "Vector Calculus" proficiency. You provide the training, but two weeks later, the model is still failing.

**Strategic Peer Analysis**: 
- **Was it the Training?**: You assessed the gap and provided the training (Day 15).
- **Was it the Distance?**: The team is virtual, leading to "Siloed Coding" (Day 16).
- **The Missing Link**: **Shared Understanding (Day 17)**. The team members are now technically proficient (Calculus), but they have different interpretations of the *Project Vision* for the AI model's output. 
- **The Mastery Action**: Facilitate a **Visual Mapping Session** (shared digital canvas) to align the "Model Logic" across the team. Update the **Technical Requirements** document with the unified vision.
