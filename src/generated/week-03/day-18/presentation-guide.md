### Slide 1: Title Slide

**Notes**: Briefly touch on the "Hero Lessons" of the week. Remind them that Training is the Floor, Virtual is the Bridge, and Understanding is the Compass.

**Notes**: Walk through the "developer" scenario slowly. Show them how the answer (Mentoring/OJT) hits all three pillars: it's training, it respects the person, and it verifies agreement.

### Slide 5: Common Pitfalls
**Notes**: This is "Exam Insider" info. Point out that the "Wrong" answers on the PMP exam often sound professional but are "Reactive" or "Top-Down."
- **Image Idea**: A caution sign over a generic "Manager" icon.

### Slide 6: Analytical Tool
**Notes**: This Decision Tree is their "Cheat Sheet" for human dynamics. Encourage them to memorize this logic flow.

### Slide 7: PMP Strategy
**Notes**: Talk about the "Clock." 77 seconds. You need a process to eliminate noise quickly.

**Notes**: Set the tone for Day 19. It's a "Diagnostic Assessment," not a "Final Exam." We want to find the holes in the armor now, not in the testing center.

## 🎯 Practice Application (Strategic Perspective)
- **Scenario**: A project is 10% over budget. A stakeholder suggests cutting the "Virtual Team Building" budget to save costs.
- **Question**: Based on Day 16 logic, what is the PM's response?
- **Debrief**: They advocate for the budget. Virtual team building is an investment in **Trust (The Foundation of Speed)**. Cutting it is a "False Economy" that will lead to communication friction and further budget overruns.
