# Presentation Guide: Day 17 - Build Shared Understanding

## 🎬 Overall Flow
- **The Hook (0-30s)**: "In project management, being 'right' is useless if you are alone. Success requires a collective mental map. Today, we learn how to synchronize those maps."
- **Learning Objectives**: Moving from "Broadcasting" to "Verifying."

---

## 📊 Slide-by-Slide Breakdown

### Slide 1: Title Slide
**Notes**: Set the stage. Shared understanding is the "People Domain's" equivalent of a project baseline.
- **Image Idea**: Two gears perfectly interlocking.

### Slide 2: Learning Objectives
**Notes**: Use the term "Alignment Analytics." We aren't just talking; we are diagnosing and cementing.
- **Image Idea**: A target with four distinct arrows hitting the bullseye.

### Slide 3: The Cost of Confusion
**Notes**: Connect confusion to the "Golden Triangle" (Scope/Time/Cost). Misunderstandings eat your contingency and destroy your schedule.
- **Image Idea**: A leaking bucket labeled "Project Resources."

### Slide 4: Step 1: Root Cause Analysis
**Notes**: Explain the "5 Whys." Don't stop at "They didn't read the email." *Why* didn't they? Was the email too long? Was the subject line vague? Is the culture "Email-blind"?
- **Image Idea**: A tree with deep roots.

### Slide 5: Step 2: Consensus Building
**Notes**: Consensus isn't "Happiness." It's "Informed Support." Mention Dot Voting as a quick way to see a room's temperature.
- **Image Idea**: A group of people around a whiteboard with colorful dots.

### Slide 6: Step 3: Supporting the Agreement
**Notes**: Emphasize the "Decision Log." Memories fade, but logs endure. Remind them that if it's not in the plan, it didn't happen.
- **Image Idea**: A stamp pressing "Approved" onto a document.

### Slide 7: Step 4: Prevention Over Inspection
**Notes**: This is the "Teach-Back" method. "Tell me your understanding of what we just decided." It's the most powerful tool in the kit.
- **Image Idea**: A shield icon.

### Slide 8: PMP Mindset: The "Proactive Alignment" Rule
**Notes**: Contrast "Mastery" vs. "Reactionary." Mastery involves finding the GAP before it becomes a CLIFF.
- **Image Idea**: A binoculars icon looking at a horizon.

### Slide 9: Summary & Next Steps
**Notes**: Close the day. Tomorrow is about consolidating everything from Week 3 (Training, Virtual, Understanding).
- **Image Idea**: A path leading to a "Review & Practice" mountain.

## 🎯 Practice Application (Strategic Perspective)
- **Scenario**: A major change is approved. You communicate it in the weekly meeting. A week later, a developer is still working on the old version.
- **Question**: Is this a failure of "Leading a Team" or "Shared Understanding"?
- **Debrief**: It is primarily a failure of **Shared Understanding (Verification)**. Sending a message in a meeting is not "Establishing Understanding." The PM failed to verify that the developer (specifically) had the new "Mental Map."
