## 2. PMP Simulation Protocol
To get the most value from this assessment, you must simulate the "Exam Environment."

**The Rules of Engagement:**
- **Timed Condition**: Aim for **77 seconds per question**. For this 40-question set, set your timer for **52 minutes**.
- **No Resources**: Close your Study Guide and source files. Rely on your analytical framework.
- **The "Strategic First Pass"**: If you are stuck for more than 90 seconds, mark the question and move on. Do not let one difficult stakeholder (question) derail your entire schedule (test).

## 3. Post-Test Diagnostic: The "So What?" Analysis
The real learning happens **after** the timer stops. Use the following categorization for every question you got wrong:

| Error Type | Strategic Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Knowledge Gap** | Re-read the specific Section in the Study Guide. |
| **Logic Mismatch** | Review the "PMP Mindset" rules (e.g., Assess before Action). |
| **Reading Error** | Practice identifying "Keywords" (BEST, FIRST, EXCEPT). |
| **Time Pressure** | Practice "Elimination Tactics" to reach the answer faster. |
