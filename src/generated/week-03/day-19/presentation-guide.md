# Presentation Guide: Day 19 - WG1 Comprehensive Practice Test

## 🎬 Overall Flow
- **The Hook (0-30s)**: "Today we stop practicing and start proving. This isn't just a test; it's a high-precision diagnostic. By the end of this hour, you will know exactly what stands between you and your PMP certification."
- **Learning Objectives**: Focusing on "Exam Stamina" and "Diagnostic Accuracy."

---

## 📊 Slide-by-Slide Breakdown

### Slide 1: Title Slide
**Notes**: Set a professional, focused tone. Today is the transition from "Student" to "Candidate."
- **Image Idea**: A trophy icon or a checked list.

### Slide 2: Learning Objectives
**Notes**: Frame the objectives as "Calibration." We are adjusting our internal compass.
- **Image Idea**: A compass icon.

### Slide 3: The Scope of the Mission
**Notes**: Explain the 40 questions. Remind them that the People domain is 42% of the real exam. Mastery here is the bridge to success.
- **Image Idea**: A mountain range with a "Summit" flag.

### Slide 4: The "Battle Plan"
**Notes**: Briefly review the "Elimination" and "Keyword" tactics we practiced in Day 18. This is their last "Tactical Briefing."
- **Image Idea**: A blueprint or map.

### Slide 5: Begin the Practice Test
**Notes**: Start the music/timer. Maintain silence.
- **Image Idea**: A large digital countdown timer (52:00).

### Slide 6: [Test Duration]
**Notes**: (No content).

### Slide 7: Post-Test: The Review Phase
**Notes**: This is the most important part of the day. Acknowledge the fatigue. Remind them that "Reviewing is where the PMP is won." 
- **Image Idea**: A magnifying glass over a results sheet.

### Slide 8: Strategic Pivot
**Notes**: Help them interpret their score. Be the "Empathetic Peer." A low score today is a "Gift of Clarity"—it tells you exactly where to look next.
- **Image Idea**: A road splitting into different directions based on performance.

### Slide 9: Transitions
**Notes**: Briefly tease Week 4. Agile vs. Waterfall vs. Hybrid. The "Process Domain" is the "Largest section of the exam."
- **Image Idea**: A gate opening to a new landscape.

---

## 🎯 Practice Application (Post-Test Debrief)
- **Review Strategy**: Pick the question with the lowest accuracy in the group. Deconstruct the logic together using the **PMP Mindset** (Assess > Meet > Action > Track).
- **The Goal**: Every student should leave the room feeling they know *how* to find the right answer next time.
