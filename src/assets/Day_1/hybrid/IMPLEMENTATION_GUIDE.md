## 🎬 Phase 2: Recording (Week 2)

### YouTube Recording Session
**Duration**: 2-3 hours total

**Setup:**
- Camera: 1080p or 4K
- Microphone: Clear audio, minimal echo
- Lighting: Professional 3-point lighting
- Background: Clean, branded

**Recording Process:**
1. Record intro/hook (3-5 takes)
2. Record main content (slide by slide)
3. Record transitions between sections
4. Record call-to-action/outro (3-5 takes)
5. Record B-roll footage (optional)

**Expected Output:**
- 20-25 minutes of raw footage
- Will edit down to 12-15 minutes

### Course Recording Session
**Duration**: 1-2 hours total

**Setup:**
- Same as YouTube but more formal
- Screen recording software ready
- Slides visible on screen

**Recording Process:**
1. Record complete lecture (45-50 minutes)
2. Allow natural pauses for emphasis
3. Record Q&A section separately
4. Record any corrections/pickups

**Expected Output:**
- 45-50 minutes of lecture
- 5-10 minutes of Q&A
- Total: 50-60 minutes raw

## 🔧 Technical Specifications

### Video Export Settings
**YouTube:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- Frame Rate: 30fps or 60fps
- Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Audio: AAC, 320kbps, 48kHz

**Course Platform:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- Frame Rate: 30fps
- Bitrate: 5-8 Mbps
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Audio: AAC, 192kbps, 48kHz

### Thumbnail Specifications
- Size: 1280x720 pixels
- Format: JPG or PNG
- File Size: Under 2MB
- Text: Large, readable at small sizes
- Branding: Include logo/colors

### PDF Specifications
- Size: Letter (8.5" x 11")
- Format: PDF/A for archival
- Fonts: Embedded
- Images: 300 DPI
- File Size: Under 10MB
