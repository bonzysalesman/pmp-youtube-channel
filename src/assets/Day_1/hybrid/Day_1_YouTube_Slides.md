📝 **180 Questions** Total

⚡ **Time Per Question**: ~1.3 minutes
⚠️ **Pretest Questions**: Some don't count (you won't know which)

### Speaker Notes:

Do the math: that's about 1.3 minutes per question. Sounds like a lot, but trust me, time flies. Here's something important: some questions are pretest questions that don't count toward your score. PMI uses them to test future exam questions. The catch? You won't know which ones they are, so you have to treat every question like it counts. Time management is absolutely critical on this exam."

## Slide 5: The 5 Question Types (2 min)
**Visual**: Use Day_1_Slide_5.png

### On-Screen Text:
# 5 Question Types You'll See

1️⃣ **Multiple Choice** (Most Common)
   - Select ONE best answer from 4 options

2️⃣ **Multiple Response**
   - Select ALL correct answers (2-3 typically)

3️⃣ **Matching**
   - Connect items from two lists

4️⃣ **Hotspot**
   - Click on specific areas of diagrams

5️⃣ **Fill-in-the-Blank**
   - Type specific terms or formulas

### Speaker Notes:
"You'll see five different question types on the exam. Multiple choice is the most common - pick the ONE best answer from four options. These are usually scenario-based. Multiple response questions ask you to select ALL correct answers - the question will tell you how many to pick. Matching questions have you connect related items - like matching processes to knowledge areas. Hotspot questions show you a diagram and you click on the correct area. And fill-in-the-blank questions have you type specific terms or formulas. The key? Multiple choice dominates the exam, so that's where we'll focus most of our practice."

**Timing**: 4:30 - 6:30
