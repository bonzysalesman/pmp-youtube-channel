**"Day 1: PMP Exam 2024 Complete Overview"**

### Target Audience
- YouTube subscribers
- Self-study candidates
- First-time visitors

- **Style**: Fast-paced, visual-heavy
- **Slides**: 11 slides (hybrid deck)

- Source: Keynote Slide 1

- "This determines how we'll study"

**Slide 4: Exam Format & Logistics** (2 min)
- Source: Keynote Slide 4
- 180 questions, 230 minutes
- 2 breaks, question distribution
- Time management preview

**Slide 5: Question Types** (2 min)
- Source: Keynote Slide 5
- 5 types with examples
- Quick tips for each type

**Slide 6: Key Study Resources** (2 min)
- Source: Keynote Slide 6
- ECO (blueprint), PMBOK (methodology), Agile Guide
- How to use each resource

**Slide 7: The PMP Mindset** (2 min)

- Proactive vs reactive

- "This is how you think on exam day"

**Slide 8: Study Time Allocation** (90s)
- Source: Keynote Slide 7

- Weekly hour recommendations

**Slide 9: 13-Week Roadmap** (2 min)

- Source: Generated Slide 9 (adapted)
- Download study calendar
- Join community

- "Make it real today"

**Slide 11: Next Steps & CTA** (30s)
- Source: Keynote Slide 9
- Tomorrow: Study resources deep dive
- Subscribe + notification bell
- Comment your exam date goal

### YouTube Optimization
- **Title**: "Day 1: PMP Exam 2024 Complete Overview | Pass on First Try"
