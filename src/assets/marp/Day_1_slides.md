# Exam Format at a Glance

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Questions** | 180 total (175 scored, 5 unscored pretest) |
| **Time allowed** | 230 minutes · two 10-min breaks |
| **Question types** | Multiple choice, drag-and-drop, matching, hotspot, limited multiple response |
| **Delivery** | Pearson VUE — test centre or online proctored |
| **Score reporting** | Above Target / Target / Below Target / Needs Improvement per domain |
| **Pass/fail** | PMI does not publish a numeric pass score |

# Project vs. Operations

| | **Project** | **Operations** |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Temporary, unique | Ongoing, repetitive |
| **Output** | Creates a unique product, service, or result | Sustains the business |
| **End state** | Closes when objectives met or terminated | Continues indefinitely |
| **Team** | Often assembled for duration | Permanent function |
| **Example** | Building a new e-commerce platform | Running that platform post-launch |

<span class="gold-callout">**Exam watch:** PMI often tests your ability to distinguish project work from operational work in scenario-based questions.</span>

# Exam Strategy: Day 1 Mindset
## How to approach the next 12 weeks

- **Read the ECO, not just the PMBOK®** · The ECO is the actual exam blueprint — know every task statement
- **Practise situational thinking** · For every question, ask: *what would an experienced PM do, not what does the textbook say*
- **Track your weak domains** · Log every practice question error by domain — pattern recognition is your friend
- **Simulate exam conditions weekly** · Timed mock sessions from Week 4 onwards — stamina is a real exam factor
- **Apply concepts to your own work** · The best retention comes from connecting theory to your lived PM experience
