07 — Do's & Don'ts

Logo Integrity

✓ Do
Use on approved backgrounds only
Light on white. Dark on deep purple or dark photography. Monochrome for all print surfaces.
✗ Don't
Place on busy or low-contrast surfaces
Never over complex imagery, colour gradients, or patterns that reduce legibility below 4.5:1 contrast.
✓ Do
Scale uniformly from approved PNGs
Use the canonical files at their native aspect ratio. Scale down as needed — never stretch or distort.
✗ Don't
Alter type, colour, or spacing
No different typeface, no adjusted tracking, no recolouring outside the three approved systems, no effects.
✓ Do
Honour the gold rule
The rule is a core component of the mark — always present, always gold. It spans the PMP block width precisely.
✗ Don't
Add borders or frames
The Mastery Framework mark has shed the legacy border box. Do not reintroduce enclosures of any kind.
✓ Do
Use monochrome for all print
Certificates, letterhead, guides, and any printed document uses black ink on white — full stop.
✗ Don't
Import base-tier brand colours
The base PMP Exam Prep product uses orange and teal. These never appear in Mastery Framework materials.
