# Day 2 Hybrid Content Strategy

## 🎯 Dual-Format Approach

Create TWO versions of Day 2 content, following the success of Day 1:

---

## 📺 Version A: YouTube Video (Public)
**"Your PMP® Exam Toolkit: Resources & ECO Blueprint"**

### Target Audience
- YouTube subscribers
- Self-study candidates
- Candidates confused about PMBOK 7 vs ECO

### Format
- **Duration**: 12-15 minutes
- **Style**: Fast-paced, illustrative, vector-motion style
- **Slides**: 11-12 slides
- **Delivery**: High energy, focusing on the "Most Important" factor (the ECO)

### Slide Structure (Optimized for YouTube)
1. **Hook**: "Don't study the wrong things."
2. **The Big 3**: Brief intro to PMBOK, Agile Guide, ECO.
3. **PMBOK 7 Positioning**: Why it's a reference, not a map.
4. **Agile Guide**: The 50% reality check.
5. **Process Groups**: Lifecycle time context.
6. **The ECO Blueprint**: Hierarchy of Domains/Tasks/Enablers.
7. **Domain Weighting**: 42/50/8 - Where to spend your time.
8. **Navigation Tip**: How to download and use the ECO today.
9. **Mindset Preview**: "Tomorrow we start the Mindset (Pt 1)."
10. **CTA**: Download the Study Guide / Join community.

---

## 🎓 Version B: Course Platform (Students)
**"Day 2: Master Your Resources & Navigate the ECO"**

### Target Audience
- Enrolled students
- Serious candidates wanting the "Passing Strategy"

### Format
- **Duration**: 35-45 minutes
- **Style**: Comprehensive, instructor-led "Masterclass"
- **Slides**: 15 slides (includes activity markers)
- **Delivery**: Deep dive with script-led presenter notes

### Expanded Slide Structure
1-5. **Foundation**: Detailed walkthrough of each guide's structure.
6. **Exam Logistics**: Deep dive into question types and timing strategy.
7. **ECO Deep Dive**: Mapping a single task from Domain to Enabler.
8. **Resource Mapping**: Showing exactly where PMBOK explains specific ECO tasks.
9. **Activity**: Structured "Locating Your Resources" exercise.
10. **Accountability**: Setting a study schedule for the 50% Process domain.
11-15. **Synthesis**: Wrap up, Q&A blocks, and Day 3 Prep.
