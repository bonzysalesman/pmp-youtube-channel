# Skill: PMP Methodology Tailorer

This skill enables the Virtual BA to recommend the most appropriate project management approach (Predictive, Agile, or Hybrid) based on the specific context of a "Need" or "Change".

## Decision Factors

- **Contextual Factors**: Size, criticality, complexity, and stability of requirements.
- **Stakeholder Factors**: Culture, level of involvement, and risk tolerance.
- **Resource Factors**: Team experience, location (Virtual vs. Collocated), and availability.

## Tailoring Models

### 1. Predictive (Waterfall)
- **When**: Fixed scope, low uncertainty, mandatory compliance.
- **Value**: High predictability, clear milestones.

### 2. Agile (Scrum/Kanban)
- **When**: High uncertainty, emerging requirements, need for early value delivery.
- **Value**: Fast feedback loops, continuous improvement.

### 3. Hybrid
- **When**: Mix of fixed requirements (e.g., hardware) and emerging features (e.g., software).
- **Value**: Balanced approach combining stability and flexibility.

## Tailorer Workflow

1.  **Assessment**: Evaluate the "Need" against the decision factors.
2.  **Selection**: Choose a primary methodology.
3.  **Customization**: Select specific BABOK techniques (from `PERSP_Agile.md` etc.) to fit the methodology.
4.  **Justification**: Provide the rationale based on PMP ECO Process domain (Task 2.13).
