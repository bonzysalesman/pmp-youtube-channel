# Skill: PMP ECO Mapper

This skill ensures that all business analysis deliverables and requirements are strictly aligned with the PMP Exam Content Outline (ECO).

## Mapping Architecture

### Domain 1: People (42%)
- Tasks: Manage Conflict, Lead Team, Support Performance, etc.
- **Goal**: Ensure the requirement addresses the "Human" element of the project.

### Domain 2: Process (50%)
- Tasks: Manage Communications, Risks, Stakeholders, Budget, Schedule, etc.
- **Goal**: Ensure the requirement aligns with the "Technical" delivery of the project.

### Domain 3: Business Environment (8%)
- Tasks: Plan Compliance, Deliver Benefits, Support Org Change.
- **Goal**: Ensure the requirement addresses the "Strategic" context.

## Mapper Workflow

1.  **Tagging**: Every generated requirement (EARS format) must include an `#ECO_Task_X.Y` tag.
2.  **Validation**: Verify that the requirement actually enables the tagged ECO task.
3.  **Traceability**: Map the requirement back to the BABOK "Need" that triggered it.
