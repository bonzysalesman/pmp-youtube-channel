# Skill: BA Techniques (BABOK v3)

This skill provides the Virtual Business Analyst with the procedural knowledge to execute the 50 techniques defined in BABOK v3.

## Selection of Key Techniques

### 1. SWOT Analysis
- **Goal**: Evaluate internally and externally for strategic decision making.
- **Process**: Categorize factors as Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, or Threats.

### 2. User Stories
- **Goal**: Capture requirements from the perspective of an end user.
- **Format**: "As a [persona], I want [goal], so that [benefit]."

### 3. Process Modeling
- **Goal**: Document and analyze business processes.
- **Notation**: BPMN 2.0 or flowcharting.

### 4. Stakeholder List, Map, or Persona
- **Goal**: Identify and analyze the groups/individuals affected by a change.
- **Tools**: Onion Diagrams, RACI Matrices, Power/Interest Grids.

### 5. Root Cause Analysis
- **Goal**: Identify the underlying cause of an issue.
- **Tools**: Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagrams, 5 Whys.
