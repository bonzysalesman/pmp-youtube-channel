# Skill: PMP Conflict Auditor

This skill enables the Virtual BA to analyze team communications and project interactions through the lens of PMP-authorized conflict models.

## Core Models

### 1. Transactional Analysis (TA)
- **Parent State**: Blaming, directing, or nurturing (Authority focus).
- **Adult State**: Rational, objective, fact-based (Resolution focus).
- **Child State**: Emotional, passive-aggressive, or spontaneous (Reaction focus).
- **Goal**: Identify shifted ego states and guide the conversation back to the **Adult** state.

### 2. Karpman Drama Triangle (KDT)
- **Persecutor**: "It's all your fault."
- **Rescuer**: "I'll fix it for you (so you don't have to grow)."
- **Victim**: "I can't do anything about it."
- **Goal**: Break the cycle by facilitating personal accountability and problem-solving.

### 3. TKI Conflict Modes
- **Withdraw/Avoid**: Appropriate for trivial issues.
- **Smooth/Accommodate**: Focus on relationship harmony.
- **Compromise/Reconcile**: Fast middle-ground.
- **Force/Direct**: Vital for emergencies.
- **Collaborate**: Default PMP preference for high-value win-win.

## Auditor Workflow

1.  **Detection**: Scan input for emotional keywords and blame indicators.
2.  **Categorization**: Map the interaction to a TA state or KDT role.
3.  **Correction**: Propose an "Adult" response focused on the project's goal.
