# Skill: Plan and Manage Finance (pmp_finance_management)

## Domain: Process

## Focus Areas
Financial Planning, Cost Estimation, Budget Tracking, Financial Reporting, Contingency, Governance

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Plan and manage finance" (Domain II, Task 6). The PM analyzes project financial needs, establishes tracking, plans reporting, and manages reserves — integrating financial oversight with the governance process throughout the project life cycle.

## ECO Enablers
- Analyze project financial needs.
- Quantify risk and contingency financial allocations.
- Plan spend tracking throughout the project life cycle.
- Plan financial reporting.
- Anticipate future finance challenges.
- Monitor financial variations and work with the governance process.
- Manage financial reserves.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain II Process — Task 6 (2026 ECO).
- **Exam Weight**: Part of Process domain (41% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: detailed cost baseline, earned value management (EVM), formal budget approval. Agile: rolling-wave cost estimates, burn rate tracking, lightweight financial forecasting per sprint. Hybrid: phased budget release tied to phase gates.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: AI can support financial forecasting and anomaly detection; sustainability costs (carbon offsets, green procurement premiums) are now explicit financial line items.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Cost baseline vs. budget: baseline is the approved plan; budget includes management reserves above the baseline.
- Contingency reserves: for known-unknowns (identified risks). Management reserves: for unknown-unknowns.
- Earned Value Management: PV (planned value), EV (earned value), AC (actual cost). Key metrics: CPI = EV/AC, SPI = EV/PV.
- Financial reporting cadence must align with governance requirements — sponsor, PMO, and steering committee reporting cycles.
- Financial variations trigger governance actions: cost variance (CV) below threshold triggers formal escalation.
- Reserve management in agile: maintained as a project-level contingency budget; released per sprint or iteration as risks are realized.

## Exam Application
- Scenario questions will present financial data (budget, actual spend, EV figures) and ask for the correct next action — focus on whether the variance is within tolerance, and whether governance notification is required.
- Graphic-based items may show an EVM chart or budget burn-down — identify status and recommend corrective action.
- Enhanced matching: pair financial terms (CPI, SPI, CV, SV) to their formulas and interpretations.
- Common distractors: confusing contingency reserves with management reserves, or treating all cost overruns as scope issues.

## Usage
- Trigger during planning (cost estimating, budget setting) and throughout execution (cost monitoring, financial reporting).
