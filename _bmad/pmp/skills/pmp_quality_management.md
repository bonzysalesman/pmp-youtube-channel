# Skill: Plan and Optimize Quality (pmp_quality_management)

## Domain: Process

## Focus Areas
Quality Requirements, QA/QC, Quality Management Plan, Cost of Quality, Sustainability, Regulatory Compliance, Continuous Improvement

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables" (Domain II, Task 7). The PM gathers quality requirements, plans and executes a quality management approach, manages cost of quality including sustainability considerations, and drives continuous quality improvement.

## ECO Enablers
- Gather quality requirements for project deliverables.
- Plan quality processes and tools.
- Execute a quality management plan.
- Help ensure regulatory compliance.
- Manage cost of quality (CoQ) and sustainability.
- Conduct ongoing quality reviews.
- Implement continuous improvement.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain II Process — Task 7 (2026 ECO).
- **Exam Weight**: Part of Process domain (41% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: quality management plan, quality audits, control quality inspections, formal QA reviews. Agile: definition of done, acceptance criteria per user story, test-driven development, sprint reviews as quality gates. Hybrid: formal quality gates at phase boundaries with agile-level quality checks within iterations.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: Cost of Quality now explicitly includes sustainability costs — green procurement premiums, waste reduction costs, carbon offset costs. AI supports defect prediction, automated testing, and quality trend analysis.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Cost of Quality (CoQ): conformance costs (prevention + appraisal) vs. non-conformance costs (internal failures + external failures). Lower CoQ = better quality investment.
- QA vs. QC: QA is process-focused (are we following the right process?); QC is product-focused (does the deliverable meet requirements?).
- Regulatory compliance: some industries require mandatory quality standards (ISO, FDA, SOX) — the PM must identify applicable regulations during planning.
- Sustainability in quality: environmental certifications (ISO 14001), social audits, and sustainability reporting are emerging quality requirements in many project environments.
- Continuous improvement tools: PDCA cycle, kaizen, control charts, Pareto analysis.

## Usage
- Trigger during planning (quality requirements gathering, plan development) and throughout execution (reviews, audits, corrective actions).
