# Skill: Plan and Manage Risk (pmp_risk_management)

## Domain: Business Environment

## Focus Areas
Risk Identification, Qualitative/Quantitative Analysis, Risk Response, Risk Register, Security Risk, Sustainability Risk

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Plan and manage risk" (Domain III, Task 5). The PM identifies, analyzes, and monitors risks — developing a risk management plan, maintaining a risk register, and executing risk responses including those for security and sustainability risks.

## ECO Enablers
- Identify risks.
- Analyze risks (qualitative and quantitative).
- Monitor and control risks.
- Develop a risk management plan.
- Maintain a risk register (e.g., poor IT security).
- Execute a risk management plan (e.g., risk response for security and managing sustainability risks).
- Communicate the status of a risk impact on the project.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain III Business Environment — Task 5 (2026 ECO). Note: moved from Process to Business Environment in 2026 update.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of Business Environment domain (26% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: formal risk register, probability-impact matrix, risk response strategies (avoid/mitigate/transfer/accept). Agile: risk-adjusted backlog prioritization, spike stories for high-uncertainty items, risk burndown charts. Hybrid: formal risk register for project-level risks; agile risk management for iteration-level uncertainty.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: Security risk (e.g., poor IT security, data breaches) and sustainability risk (e.g., supply chain environmental violations, regulatory changes) are explicitly named in the 2026 ECO as examples within this task.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Risk vs. issue: a risk is a future uncertain event; an issue is a risk that has materialized. The risk register tracks risks; the issue log tracks issues.
- Qualitative analysis: probability × impact matrix to prioritize risks for further analysis or response.
- Quantitative analysis: Monte Carlo simulation, decision tree analysis, expected monetary value (EMV) for high-impact risks.
- Risk responses: threats (avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept, escalate); opportunities (exploit, enhance, share, accept, escalate).
- Security risk: IT security vulnerabilities, cyber threats, data privacy breaches — now explicitly exam-tested.
- Sustainability risk: environmental regulation changes, supply chain sustainability failures, reputational risks from ESG non-compliance.
- Risk communication: stakeholders and sponsor must be kept informed of risk status; undisclosed risks that materialize are a PM accountability failure.
