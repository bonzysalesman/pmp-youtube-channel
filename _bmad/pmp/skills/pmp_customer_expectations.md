# Skill: Manage Stakeholder Expectations (pmp_customer_expectations)

## Domain: People

## Focus Areas
Customer Expectations, Internal/External Satisfaction, Outcomes Alignment, Expectation Monitoring

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Manage stakeholder expectations" (Domain I, Task 6). The PM identifies what internal and external customers expect, aligns project outcomes to those expectations, and monitors satisfaction throughout the project.

## ECO Enablers
- Identify internal and external customer expectations.
- Align and maintain project outcomes to internal and external customer expectations.
- Monitor internal and external customer satisfaction/expectations and respond as needed.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain I People — Task 6 (2026 ECO).
- **Exam Weight**: Part of People domain (33% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: formal requirements sign-off and change control. Agile: continuous stakeholder feedback loops, definition of done, sprint reviews. Hybrid: formal baselines with iterative validation checkpoints.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: Customer satisfaction data can be collected and analyzed using AI tools; sustainability commitments are increasingly part of customer expectations.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Internal customers: project sponsor, PMO, other departments consuming deliverables.
- External customers: end users, clients, regulatory bodies with outcome expectations.
- Distinction from T5 (Align expectations): T5 is about facilitation and categorization; T6 is about active monitoring, maintaining alignment, and responding when gaps emerge.
- Expectation creep: undocumented shifts in what stakeholders expect — a leading source of scope conflict and dissatisfaction.
- Satisfaction measurement tools: surveys, net promoter scores, sprint review feedback, formal acceptance criteria.

## Exam Application
- Scenario questions will present a situation where a stakeholder says deliverables "don't meet expectations" — correct answer is to first investigate whether expectations were properly captured and baselined, then assess the gap.
- Graphic-based items may show a satisfaction trend or stakeholder feedback matrix — identify which group is misaligned and what the PM should do next.
- Common distractors: treating expectation gaps as scope changes immediately, or escalating without first re-engaging the stakeholder.

## Usage
- Trigger when stakeholder or customer dissatisfaction signals emerge, or during periodic satisfaction reviews.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_stakeholder_engagement`, `pmp_stakeholder_collaboration`, `pmp_communications_management`.
