# Skill: Continuous Improvement (pmp_continuous_improvement)

## Domain: Business Environment

## Focus Areas
Lessons Learned, OPA Updates, Process Improvement, Retrospectives, Knowledge Reuse

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Continuous improvement" (Domain III, Task 6). The PM drives ongoing learning by utilizing lessons learned, ensuring CI processes are updated, and embedding findings into organizational process assets (OPAs) for future projects.

## ECO Enablers
- Utilize lessons learned.
- Help ensure continuous improvement processes are updated.
- Update organizational process assets (OPAs).

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain III Business Environment — Task 6 (2026 ECO).
- **Exam Weight**: Part of Business Environment domain (26% of exam — the domain tripled from 8% to 26%).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: formal lessons-learned register, post-project review, OPA updates at phase gates. Agile: sprint retrospectives, process improvement backlog items, team kaizen sessions. Hybrid: retrospectives throughout with formal OPA update at project close.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: AI can mine lessons-learned databases to surface patterns across projects; sustainability improvements (waste reduction, energy efficiency gains) are a growing category of CI output.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- OPAs (Organizational Process Assets): two categories — processes/procedures (templates, policies, guidelines) and corporate knowledge base (lessons learned, historical data, metrics).
- Lessons learned: captured throughout the project (not just at closure) and stored in the organizational knowledge base for reuse.
- Retrospectives vs. lessons learned: retrospectives are team-internal and action-oriented; lessons learned are organizational and archival.
- CI in business environment context: unlike quality management CI (focused on product/deliverable quality), this task focuses on improving the PM process and organizational capability.
- Kaizen, PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act), and DMAIC are common CI frameworks referenced in PMP questions.

## Exam Application
- Scenario questions will present a situation where a similar problem recurred from a previous project — correct answer involves first checking the lessons-learned register/OPAs before deciding on a response.
- A PM completing a project should update OPAs before final closure sign-off, not after.
- Common distractors: treating CI as only a quality management function (it's also a Business Environment function); skipping lessons-learned updates due to time pressure.
- Case/scenario items may span multiple CI cycles — identify the correct sequence: capture → analyze → update OPA → communicate → apply on next project.

## Usage
- Trigger at phase gates, retrospectives, project closure, and any time a recurring issue or process gap is identified.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_quality_management`, `pmp_closure_management`, `pmp_knowledge_transfer`, `pmp_governance_establishment`.
