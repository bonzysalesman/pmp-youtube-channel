# Skill: Build Shared Understanding (pmp_shared_understanding)

## Domain: People

## Focus Areas
Consensus Building, Active Listening, Facilitation, Common Ground, Documentation of Agreements

## Description
This micro-skill supports the broader "Develop a common vision" task (Domain I, Task 1) and underpins multiple People domain tasks. It covers the facilitation techniques the PM uses to surface and document shared understanding across the team and stakeholders — ensuring common interpretation of goals, definitions, constraints, and success criteria.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain I People — supporting Task 1 (Develop a common vision), Task 5 (Align stakeholder expectations), and Task 8 (Plan and manage communication) in the 2026 ECO.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of People domain (33% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: kickoff meetings, requirements workshops, definition of terms in the project charter. Agile: team working agreements, definition of done, definition of ready, sprint planning consensus. Hybrid: formal charter with iterative refinement of shared definitions per phase.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Shared understanding vs. shared vision: shared understanding is about common interpretation of facts, terms, and constraints; shared vision (`pmp_vision_development`) is about alignment on purpose and desired future state.
- Facilitation techniques for shared understanding: affinity mapping, nominal group technique, structured brainstorming, DACI/RACI clarification.
- Active listening: paraphrasing, summarizing, and confirming comprehension — exam questions test whether the PM takes responsibility for clear communication.
- Documentation of agreements: verbal consensus is fragile; project documents (charter, scope statement, team charter) make shared understanding durable.

## Usage
- Trigger at project initiation, team formation, and whenever ambiguity or misalignment is detected.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_vision_development`, `pmp_stakeholder_collaboration`, `pmp_team_building`, `pmp_conflict_management`.
