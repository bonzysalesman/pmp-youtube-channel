# Skill: Help Ensure Knowledge Transfer (pmp_knowledge_transfer)

## Domain: People

## Focus Areas
Critical Knowledge Identification, Knowledge Capture, Knowledge Transfer Environment, Explicit/Tacit Knowledge, Continuity

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Help ensure knowledge transfer" (Domain I, Task 7). The PM identifies knowledge critical to the project, gathers it systematically, and fosters an environment where knowledge flows freely across the team.

## ECO Enablers
- Identify knowledge critical to the project.
- Gather knowledge.
- Foster an environment for knowledge transfer.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain I People — Task 7 (2026 ECO). Note: moved from Process to People domain in 2026 update.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of People domain (33% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: lessons-learned register, project library, formal knowledge management plan. Agile: team wikis, pair programming, retrospective action items, cross-functional team structure facilitates organic knowledge transfer. Hybrid: formal knowledge repository with agile knowledge sharing practices.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: AI tools (semantic search, knowledge graphs) can surface relevant organizational knowledge; sustainability practices should be captured as transferable knowledge.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Explicit vs. tacit knowledge: explicit knowledge can be documented (processes, procedures, data); tacit knowledge lives in people's heads (judgment, expertise, relationships) — harder to transfer, requires mentoring and shadowing.
- Knowledge hoarding risk: team members may resist knowledge transfer due to job security concerns — the PM must foster psychological safety.
- Knowledge transfer timing: should happen continuously throughout the project, not only at closure.
- Departure planning: when a key team member leaves, critical knowledge must be documented and handed over before their exit.

## Usage
- Trigger during team onboarding, at phase gates, when key personnel transitions occur, and at project closure.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_training_facilitation`, `pmp_mentoring`, `pmp_continuous_improvement`, `pmp_closure_management`.
