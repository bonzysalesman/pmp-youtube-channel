# Skill: Develop a Common Vision (pmp_vision_development)

## Domain: People

## Focus Areas
Shared Vision, Stakeholder Alignment, Vision Promotion, Misunderstanding Root Cause

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Develop a common vision" (Domain I, Task 1). The PM establishes and sustains a shared project vision with key stakeholders — promoting it, keeping it current, and resolving misunderstandings before they erode alignment.

## ECO Enablers
- Help ensure a shared vision with key stakeholders.
- Promote the shared vision across the team and stakeholders.
- Keep the vision current as project context evolves.
- Break down situations to identify the root cause of a misunderstanding of the vision.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain I People — Task 1 (2026 ECO).
- **Exam Weight**: Part of People domain (33% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Apply across predictive (charter/kick-off), agile (sprint goals, product vision), and hybrid contexts.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: Vision may include sustainability outcomes; AI tools can support communication and tracking of vision adherence.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Vision vs. scope: vision is the "why/what-for," scope is the "what." Misaligning these is a common exam trap.
- Shared vision in agile: the product vision statement and sprint goals serve as the vehicle.
- Root-cause techniques for vision misalignment: 5-Whys, fishbone, retrospectives.
- Stakeholder communication plans must reinforce the vision, not just report status.

## Exam Application
- Scenario-based questions will present a team or stakeholder group diverging from the project's intended outcomes — correct answer involves re-establishing vision clarity before re-planning.
- Graphic-based items may show a stakeholder map or communications matrix; identify which groups need vision reinforcement.
- Common distractors: jumping to scope change or escalation before first confirming shared understanding of the vision.

## Usage
- Trigger when a project team or stakeholder group shows misalignment on goals, priorities, or success criteria.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_stakeholder_engagement`, `pmp_communications_management`, `pmp_shared_understanding`.
