# Skill: Engage Stakeholders (pmp_stakeholder_engagement)

## Domain: People

## Focus Areas
Stakeholder Identification, Power/Interest/Influence Matrices, Communication Tailoring, Engagement Plan Execution, Trust Building

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Engage stakeholders" (Domain I, Task 4). The PM identifies and analyzes stakeholders, tailors communication to their needs, executes the engagement plan, and builds trust to accomplish project objectives.

## ECO Enablers
- Identify stakeholders.
- Analyze stakeholders (power, interest, influence, impact).
- Analyze and tailor communication to stakeholder needs.
- Execute the stakeholder engagement plan.
- Optimize alignment among stakeholder needs, expectations, and project objectives.
- Build trust and influence stakeholders to accomplish project objectives.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain I People — Task 4 (2026 ECO). Note: moved from Process to People domain in 2026 update.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of People domain (33% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: stakeholder register, engagement assessment matrix, formal engagement plan. Agile: product demos, sprint reviews, continuous feedback loops. Hybrid: formal identification with iterative engagement cadence.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: AI tools can support stakeholder sentiment analysis; sustainability stakeholders (community groups, regulators, environmental bodies) are increasingly prominent.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Power/Interest grid: high power/high interest = manage closely; high power/low interest = keep satisfied.
- Stakeholder engagement assessment matrix: tracks current vs. desired engagement level (unaware → resistant → neutral → supportive → leading).
- Trust-building in agile: transparency through visible backlogs, frequent demos, and honest velocity reporting.

## Usage
- Trigger during initiation (identify/analyze) and throughout execution (execute plan, monitor, adjust).
- Cross-reference with `pmp_communications_management`, `pmp_stakeholder_collaboration`, `pmp_vision_development`, `pmp_customer_expectations`.
