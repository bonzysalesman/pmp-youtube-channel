# Skill: Remove Impediments and Manage Issues (pmp_impediment_removal)

## Domain: Business Environment

## Focus Areas
Impediment Evaluation, Prioritization, Intervention Strategy, Continual Reassessment, Risk-to-Issue Escalation

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Remove impediments and manage issues" (Domain III, Task 4). The PM evaluates and prioritizes impediments, applies intervention strategies, and collaborates with stakeholders when escalation is required.

## ECO Enablers
- Evaluate the impact of impediments.
- Prioritize and highlight impediments.
- Determine and apply an intervention strategy to remove/minimize impediments.
- Reassess continually to ensure impediments, obstacles, and blockers are being addressed.
- Recognize when a risk becomes an issue.
- Collaborate with relevant stakeholders on an approach to resolve the issues.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain III Business Environment — Task 4 (2026 ECO). Note: moved from People to Business Environment in 2026 update.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of Business Environment domain (26% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: issue log, escalation matrix, formal issue resolution procedure. Agile: impediment backlog, scrum master impediment removal, daily standup as identification forum. Hybrid: formal issue log for project-level blockers; agile impediment removal at team level.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Risk vs. issue: a risk is a potential future event; an issue is a problem that has already occurred. When a risk materializes it moves from the risk register to the issue log.
- Impediment escalation: some impediments are within PM authority; others require sponsor or steering committee intervention.
- Continual reassessment: closing an impediment requires verifying the resolution actually unblocked the team — not just marking it resolved.

## Usage
- Trigger whenever a blocker, obstacle, or unresolved risk materializes.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_issue_management`, `pmp_risk_management`, `pmp_governance_establishment`, `pmp_stakeholder_engagement`.
