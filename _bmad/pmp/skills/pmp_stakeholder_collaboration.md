# Skill: Align Stakeholder Expectations (pmp_stakeholder_collaboration)

## Domain: People

## Focus Areas
Stakeholder Categorization, Expectation Identification, Facilitated Alignment, Mentoring Opportunities

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Align stakeholder expectations" (Domain I, Task 5). The PM categorizes stakeholders, surfaces their expectations, facilitates discussions to build alignment, and creates mentoring opportunities to support expectation calibration.

## ECO Enablers
- Categorize stakeholders.
- Identify stakeholder expectations.
- Facilitate discussions to align expectations.
- Organize and act on mentoring opportunities.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain I People — Task 5 (2026 ECO). Reframed from general collaboration to specifically cover expectation alignment.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of People domain (33% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: requirements workshops, stakeholder interviews, formal expectation documentation in project charter. Agile: sprint reviews and backlog refinement as alignment mechanisms. Hybrid: formal expectation baseline with iterative validation.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: AI can surface expectation gaps by analyzing stakeholder communications; sustainability stakeholders often hold expectations beyond traditional project success criteria (scope/time/cost).
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Distinction from T6 (Manage expectations): T5 is the proactive facilitation step — categorize and align expectations before the project runs. T6 is the ongoing monitoring and response during execution.
- Stakeholder categorization models: power/interest grid, salience model (power/legitimacy/urgency), stakeholder cube.
- Mentoring opportunities: senior stakeholders mentoring junior stakeholders on PM practices; PM mentoring business stakeholders on technical constraints.
- Expectation alignment vs. requirements definition: expectations are broader (outcomes, experience, relationships); requirements are the specific deliverable specifications.

## Usage
- Trigger during initiation and early planning to baseline expectations before execution.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_stakeholder_engagement`, `pmp_customer_expectations`, `pmp_mentoring`, `pmp_vision_development`.
