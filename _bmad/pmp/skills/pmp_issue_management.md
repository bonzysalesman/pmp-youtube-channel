# Skill: Manage Project Issues (pmp_issue_management)

## Domain: Business Environment

## Focus Areas
Issue Log, Resolution Tracking, Escalation, Stakeholder Collaboration on Resolution

## Description
This micro-skill supports "Remove impediments and manage issues" (Domain III, Task 4). It covers formal tracking and resolution of issues emerging during project execution, working in tandem with `pmp_impediment_removal`.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain III Business Environment — Task 4 (2026 ECO). Note: moved from Process to Business Environment in 2026 update.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of Business Environment domain (26% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: issue log with priority, owner, target date, and status. Agile: impediment backlog or team-visible issues list. Hybrid: formal issue log for governance-level issues; team tracking within iterations.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Issue log contents: ID, description, priority, assigned owner, target resolution date, status, resolution notes.
- Every issue must have a named owner — unowned issues stall indefinitely.
- When a risk materializes it moves from the risk register to the issue log.

## Usage
- Trigger when a new issue is identified or an existing issue changes status.
- Cross-reference with `pmp_impediment_removal`, `pmp_risk_management`, `pmp_stakeholder_engagement`.
