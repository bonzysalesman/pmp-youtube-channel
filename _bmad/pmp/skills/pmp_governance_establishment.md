# Skill: Define and Establish Project Governance (pmp_governance_establishment)

## Domain: Business Environment

## Focus Areas
Governance Structure, OPAs, Decision Rights, Escalation Paths, Ethics, Success Metrics

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Define and establish project governance" (Domain III, Task 1). The PM establishes the governance structure, rules, procedures, reporting, ethics, and policies through OPAs — and defines success metrics and escalation thresholds.

## ECO Enablers
- Describe and establish the structure, rules, procedures, reporting, ethics, and policies through the use of OPAs.
- Define success metrics.
- Outline governance escalation paths and thresholds.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain III Business Environment — Task 1 (2026 ECO). Note: moved from Process to Business Environment in 2026 update.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of Business Environment domain (26% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: formal governance framework, steering committee, phase-gate reviews, project charter. Agile: lightweight governance through backlog ownership and product owner authority. Hybrid: formal portfolio-level governance with agile team-level execution.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: Governance frameworks increasingly include AI ethics policies (bias, transparency, auditability) and sustainability reporting obligations as mandatory components.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- OPAs as governance source: existing templates, policies, historical data, and lessons learned inform governance setup.
- Success metrics must be defined and agreed upon at initiation — not determined after delivery.
- Escalation thresholds: pre-defined tolerance levels (cost, schedule, quality variance) that trigger escalation to steering committee or sponsor.
- Project governance vs. organizational governance: project governance is the project-specific subset.
- Ethics in governance: conflicts of interest, procurement integrity, and reporting accuracy are exam-tested governance ethics scenarios.

## Usage
- Trigger during initiation and planning phases — governance must be established before execution begins.
