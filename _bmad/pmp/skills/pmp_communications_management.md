# Skill: Plan and Manage Communication (pmp_communications_management)

## Domain: People

## Focus Areas
Communication Strategy, Transparency, Feedback Loops, Reporting Requirements, Governance Reporting, Stakeholder Communication

## Description
This micro-skill enables the agent to perform the PMP ECO Task: "Plan and manage communication" (Domain I, Task 8). The PM defines a communication strategy, promotes transparency and collaboration, establishes feedback loops, understands reporting requirements, and creates reports aligned with sponsor and stakeholder expectations — including governance process support.

## ECO Enablers
- Define a communication strategy.
- Promote transparency and collaboration.
- Establish a feedback loop.
- Understand reporting requirements.
- Create reports aligned with sponsors and stakeholder expectations.
- Support reporting and governance processes.

## PMP Context
- **ECO Alignment**: Domain I People — Task 8 (2026 ECO). Note: moved to People domain (was Process) — reflects the relational and leadership emphasis of communication.
- **Exam Weight**: Part of People domain (33% of exam).
- **Approach Mix**: Predictive: formal communications management plan, status reports, earned value reports, lessons-learned reports. Agile: information radiators (burndown charts, Kanban boards), daily standups, sprint reviews. Hybrid: formal reporting at governance level with agile information sharing within the team.
- **AI/Sustainability Lens**: AI-generated reports and dashboards support real-time communication; sustainability reporting (ESG status, environmental impact metrics) is an emerging governance reporting requirement.
- **Knowledge Base**: Leveraging PMP Mastery content and BABOK v3 atoms.

## Key Concepts
- Communication methods: interactive (meetings, calls — most effective), push (email, reports), pull (intranet, document repositories).
- Communication channels formula: n(n-1)/2 — exponential growth with team size; relevant to planning communication complexity.
- Feedback loops: critical in both predictive (formal change requests as feedback) and agile (sprint reviews, retrospectives) contexts.
- Governance reporting: status reports must align with what the steering committee and sponsors need to make decisions — not just what the team tracks internally.
- Transparency in agile: visible backlogs, open velocity data, and honest impediment reporting are communication principles, not optional practices.

## Usage
- Trigger during planning (communications management plan) and throughout execution (status reporting, stakeholder updates, governance reporting).
